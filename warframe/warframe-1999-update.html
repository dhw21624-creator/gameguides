TENNO ARCHIVES — UPDATE 1999

Warframe 1999 Update: Proto-Frames, Höllvania & Infested Liches Guide

Tenno, the Origin System is no longer your only battlefield. Update 1999 transports you to an alternate-reality Höllvania on the eve of Y2K—a world where Warframes are human, the Infestation is just beginning, and a boyband leads the resistance. This archive documents everything.

I. The Höllvania Setting

Set in an alternate 1999, Höllvania is a city under siege by the Techrot—a precursor to the Infestation we know. The aesthetic is grunge, CRT monitors, and dial-up internet. You play as Arthur, a proto-Excalibur, alongside a crew of human-form Warframes known as The Hex.

The Hex (Proto-Frames)

  • Arthur (Proto-Excalibur): Balanced all-rounder with Exalted Blade.
  • Aoi (Proto-Mag): Magnetic control specialist, shield-based defense.
  • Lettie (Proto-Trinity): Combat medic, team sustain.
  • Amir (Proto-Volt): Speed-focused, electric attacks.
  • Quincy (Proto-Cyte-09): Sniper, long-range precision.
  • Eleanor (Proto-Nyx): Mind control, psychic disruption.

II. Infested Liches (Technocyte Coda)

The 1999 update introduces a new adversary system. Infested Liches—called Technocyte Coda—are personalized nemeses that hunt you across Höllvania.

  1. Creation: Use a Technocyte Injector on specific mission nodes to spawn a larval Lich.
  2. Progression: The Lich grows stronger as you defeat its thralls. It steals resources from your missions until you confront it.
  3. Requiem System: Same as Kuva Liches—discover the correct Requiem sequence through thrall murmurs.
  4. Vanquish or Convert: Vanquish for the Technocyte weapon, or convert to have it fight alongside you.
