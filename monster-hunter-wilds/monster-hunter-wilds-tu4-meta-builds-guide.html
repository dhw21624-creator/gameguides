Monster Hunter Wilds TU4 Meta Builds & Endgame Armor Guide

Category: Meta Builds • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Monster Hunter Wilds

Title Update 4 reshaped Monster Hunter Wilds' endgame. The December 16, 2025 update added the massive elder-level monster Gogmazios, introduced the Gogma Artian weapon line, and quietly redrew the armor meta around three sets: Gogmazios β, Jin Dahaad γ, and Arkveld γ. If you are chasing current best-in-slot gear, this guide gives you the armor plan, the weapon picks that actually win hunts, and the skill priority list that endgame theorycrafters agree on.

Meta in one line: mix Gogmazios β with Jin Dahaad γ or Arkveld γ pieces, run Long Sword for sustained DPS or Bow for ranged, and stack Critical Eye, Weakness Exploit, Agitator, and Maximum Might.

The TU4 Armor Meta: Three Sets That Define Everything

Title Update 4's armor landscape is built around three sets. Few players run a single full set; the strongest endgame loadouts mix pieces from all three to combine their skill budgets.

SetBest ForCore Value
Gogmazios βGeneral endgameHighest raw skill budget for attack-focused builds
Jin Dahaad γElemental matchupsGamma slots enable tight elemental skill stacking
Arkveld γSurvivability + damageComfort skills without sacrificing offensive uptime

The community mix-set consensus is to start from Gogmazios β chest and waist, fill with Jin Dahaad γ arms or Arkveld γ legs depending on your weapon, and finish with a head piece that carries your primary damage skill. Gogma Artian Weapons from the new boss are the damage ceiling, but the armor shell matters more than any single weapon stat.

Weapon Meta: What Is S-Tier in TU4

TU4 weapon rankings are closer than earlier updates, but a clear tier structure still exists for players who care about clear times.

  • Long Sword — S-tier sustained DPS. The Long Sword remains the benchmark for consistent, high uptime damage. Its Spirit Gauge loop pairs perfectly with the TU4 armor sets' affinity-heavy budgets.
  • Bow — best ranged option. The Bow keeps the crown at range, with strong elemental coverage and excellent synergy with Maximum Might for near-constant crit.
  • Great Sword — hardest single hits. If you are chasing wake-up hits and punish-window damage, the Great Sword delivers the biggest single numbers in the game.
  • Charge Blade — strong and technical. Charge Blade sits just below the top with a higher skill floor, rewarding players who master the SAED loop.

Whatever weapon you pick, the same two combat principles carry hunts: invest in the four core damage skills below, and exploit Focus Mode wounds for knockdowns whenever they appear.

Core Skill Priorities for Every TU4 Build

Endgame builds in TU4 are not about one gimmick skill. The four skills below appear across virtually every competitive set, and their priority order stays remarkably consistent:

  1. Critical Eye: raw affinity is the cheapest way to push your damage floor. Max it before anything else in most builds.
  2. Weakness Exploit: with wound uptime from Focus Mode, Weakness Exploit converts your affinity into near-guaranteed crits on the parts that matter.
  3. Agitator: TU4 monsters spend long stretches enraged; Agitator's raw + affinity boost is effectively always on in real fights.
  4. Maximum Might: paired with stamina-light weapons and perfect dodges, it caps your affinity during the windows where you actually attack.

Focus Mode itself is not a skill slot, but it is the playstyle key: wounds from Focus Strikes create the knockdowns that let you land full combo strings on the weak spot. Build your damage loop around wound generation and the skill stack above, and TU4 monsters fold noticeably faster.

Post-TU4: Ver 1.041 and Beyond

TU4 was not the end of the update line. Ver 1.041 (February 17, 2026) became the final major update, adding Arch-Tempered Arkveld, a Monster Hunter Stories 3 collaboration event line, and photo mode improvements. Arch-Tempered Arkveld's γ armor remains a cornerstone piece for comfort-focused endgame sets. The 2027 Ascendance expansion and a Switch 2 version have both been announced, so the TU4 meta described here remains the reference until that content lands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best armor set in TU4?

A: There is no single best full set. The meta runs Gogmazios β mixed with Jin Dahaad γ and Arkveld γ pieces to combine skill budgets.

Q: Is the Long Sword really S-tier?

A: Yes, for sustained DPS across a whole hunt. The Great Sword out-damages it per hit, but the Long Sword's uptime wins most realistic fights.

Q: Which skills should I max first?

A: Critical Eye, Weakness Exploit, Agitator, and Maximum Might, roughly in that order, on top of your weapon's mandatory skills.

Q: Do I need the Gogma Artian Weapons?

A: They are the damage ceiling, but a well-built existing weapon with correct skills still clears TU4 content comfortably.

Conclusion

TU4's meta is a mix-set game. Build around Gogmazios β, Jin Dahaad γ, and Arkveld γ, pair your armor with Long Sword, Bow, Great Sword, or Charge Blade based on your playstyle, and max Critical Eye, Weakness Exploit, Agitator, and Maximum Might. Exploit Focus Mode wounds for knockdowns and you will be playing the strongest version of Monster Hunter Wilds available today — the same meta that will carry you until the Ascendance expansion arrives in 2027.
