Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance Expansion — Everything We Know (2026)

Capcom dropped the reveal at Summer Game Fest 2026 on June 4: Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance, the first major paid expansion for the Forbidden Lands. New region above the clouds. Elder Dragons returning in force. A wrist-mounted tool that rewrites every weapon's moveset. Master Rank difficulty. And yes — a Nintendo Switch 2 port is in active development. This guide consolidates everything confirmed, everything teased, and everything hunters should do right now to prepare for the 2027 launch.

What Is Ascendance?

According to the official developer message from Producer Ryozo Tsujimoto and new Director Takuro Hiraoka, the name captures two ideas: climbing physically into the sky and advancing forward as hunters with new tools, new challenges, and a deeper story. The expansion continues the narrative of the Forbidden Lands, sending the Expedition Team into a completely new biome above the clouds. The scope mirrors what Iceborne did for Monster Hunter World — this is a full-scale expansion, not a title update.

The New Region: Skybound Eyrie

The flagship hunting ground for Ascendance is the Skybound Eyrie, a network of floating islands and ancient ruins suspended high above the Forbidden Lands. Capcom's explicit design goal: make players stop and stare the moment they arrive.

What Makes It Different

  • Vertical archipelago: Massive tiered islands connected by winding paths and climbing routes. Unlike the interconnected valleys of the base game, Skybound Eyrie forces genuine vertical movement.
  • Updraft traversal: Natural wind currents let you rise and glide between islands while mounted on your Seikret. These aren't just travel mechanics — updrafts appear throughout hunting grounds, meaning aerial positioning factors directly into combat.
  • Ancient ruins: The islands are covered in crumbling structures from a civilization that predates the Forbidden Lands settlements. Environmental storytelling suggests a connection to the Elder Dragons that now claim this sky as their territory.
  • No loading screens between islands: From trailer footage, the entire archipelago appears to be one seamless zone, with the Seikret handling transitions between floating landmasses.

Hunting Strategy: Master the Drop

The transition from gliding to dropping attacks creates a new damage layer. Practice landing aerial strikes on moving targets — Skybound Eyrie's verticality means you'll be initiating fights from above far more often than in any previous Monster Hunter map.

The Boost Bracer: How Every Weapon Changes

The single biggest gameplay addition is the Boost Bracer, a wrist-mounted gauntlet that slots alongside your familiar slinger. When activated, it temporarily overcharges your weapon with new attacks, new combos, and improved movement options.

Critical Distinction

Capcom stated explicitly in the developer message: weapon upgrades are not locked to the Bracer only. Every weapon type gets permanent new base attacks and combos that function without the Bracer. The Bracer adds an extra layer — think of it as a temporary super mode, not a replacement for your core moveset. Save Bracer activations for heavy burst windows when a monster is downed or trapped.

Elder Dragons: The Core of the Story

Director Hiraoka's direct quote from the developer message: "I can't go into much detail yet, but Ascendance's story will be deeply connected with Elder Dragons." Capcom confirmed Elder Dragons will "return in force" — the two revealed so far are almost certainly not the only ones.

Kushala Daora — The Walking Storm

The wind-based Elder Dragon appears in the reveal trailer throwing Guardian monsters around like debris and generating storms that visibly reshape the environment. For veteran hunters, Kushala is familiar — but the presentation here is different. It's being used to demonstrate what an Elder Dragon is actually supposed to be: a walking environmental disaster that changes the battlefield in real time.

Lao-Shan Lung — The Returning Legend

Capcom paid tribute properly. The reveal recreated the original cinematic from Monster Hunter 1 that introduced Lao-Shan Lung to the franchise. The developers noted it has been a very long time since players got to hunt it — the most recent appearance was a crystallized form in Monster Hunter Stories 3. This is a full-scale, living Lao-Shan Lung siege, and the Skybound Eyrie's vertical architecture seems tailor-made for a monster of this size.

Crimson Mutation: The Guardian Anomaly

One of the most intriguing details from the trailer: Guardian monsters are appearing in the Windwood Plains in large groups — well outside their normal territory. These aren't standard Guardians. The energy veins that typically glow white in base Wilds are now a deep, vivid red on these specimens.

What This Means

  • Wylk corruption: In the base game, Guardians draw on Wylk — the biological energy flowing through the Forbidden Lands. Red coloring suggests these Guardians are drawing on something else entirely.
  • Elder Dragon connection: Given that Ascendance's story centers on Elder Dragons, the Crimson Mutation is almost certainly linked to Elder Dragon activity — possibly a reaction to the presence of creatures as powerful as Kushala Daora or Lao-Shan Lung in the ecosystem.
  • Pack tactics: The trailer shows Guardians executing synchronized triangle dive-bombs. Breaking the pack leader's positioning disrupts the coordinated attacks — a new encounter design where multiple creatures of different sizes coordinate directly.

Combat Priority: Break the Leader First

Crimson Guardian packs use synchronized aerial dive attacks in triangle formation. The pack leader dictates the timing. Focus the leader to break formation and scatter the group into individual targets. Ignoring the leader means eating coordinated multi-hit combos that can cart an unprepared hunter in seconds.

Nintendo Switch 2 Port Confirmed

Capcom officially confirmed active development for the Nintendo Switch 2 version of Monster Hunter Wilds, including Ascendance. While no release date was given for the Switch 2 port specifically, the announcement opens several important questions:

  • Cross-play: Will Switch 2 hunters join PS5, Xbox, and PC lobbies? Capcom has not confirmed this yet but Monster Hunter's recent history with cross-play in Wilds makes it plausible.
  • Cross-save: Can you take your character from PC/console to Switch 2? Unconfirmed — but this would be a major quality-of-life feature for the portable hunting audience.
  • Performance targets: No frame rate or resolution details yet. The Switch 2's hardware is significantly more capable than the original Switch, but Wilds' open-world streaming demands remain high.

New Director, Proven Background

Takuro Hiraoka takes over as Director for Ascendance. His resume includes endgame design for Iceborne and monster design for Sunbreak — meaning the person now leading Ascendance has direct experience building the post-launch content that defined the last two Monster Hunter generations. Expect Master Rank to be punishing, creative, and mechanically rich.

How to Prepare: Pre-Expansion Checklist

What to Do Before 2027

  1. Complete the 7-star assignment "Awaking from a Dream" — this is the hard gate. You cannot access Ascendance content without it. Finish the base game campaign now to avoid launch-day gatekeeping.
  2. Farm the Abyssal β armor set from TU5 — this will be your entry-level gear for early Master Rank. The slot efficiency on this set is unmatched in High Rank.
  3. Craft at least one Abyssal weapon — Abyssal Great Sword or Hammer gives the strongest starting point for Master Rank's higher HP pools.
  4. Stockpile consumables: Max Potions, Ancient Potions, Dust of Life, Mega Demondrugs, and Armorskins. Master Rank hunts will burn through supplies faster than High Rank.
  5. Build at least 3 loadout variants: Raw damage, elemental, and support setups. Master Rank's expanded roster will demand flexibility.
  6. Farm Zenny: Master Rank armor upgrades historically cost 3-5x High Rank prices. Aim for at least 5 million Zenny.
  7. Master your main weapon's new base combos: The permanent moves coming in Ascendance (jet spin-stab, sprint strafe, powered guard counter) will be available immediately — start practicing now by watching the trailer for timing cues.
  8. Stock wind-negation gear: Kushala Daora's wind pressure is notorious. Craft Windproof decorations and armor pieces before launch.
