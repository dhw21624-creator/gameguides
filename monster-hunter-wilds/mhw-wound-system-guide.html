Hunter's Field Notes — Expedition Log

Mastering the Hunt: Wounds, Weapons & Elements

The Forbidden Lands hold new dangers—and new tools to overcome them. Monster Hunter Wilds introduces the revolutionary Wound system, transforming how every hunter approaches combat. This field-tested guide covers the mechanics that separate cart riders from true hunters.

I. The Wound & Focus Strike System

Repeatedly striking a monster's body part creates glowing red weak points called Wounds. These aren't just visual flair—they're your primary damage multiplier.

Focus Strike (L2 + R1): A universal input available to every weapon. Deals massive bonus damage and instantly breaks a wound. Striking multiple wounds in a single Focus Strike stacks the damage multiplicatively.

Weapons with unique Focus Strike effects gain the most: the Long Sword instantly fills its Spirit Gauge on a successful Focus Strike, while the Gunlance can chain Focus Strikes into full-burst combos. Always scan for wounds before committing your big attacks—a single well-placed Focus Strike can deal more damage than a 30-second combo on a non-wounded area.

V. Early-Game Progression Roadmap

The campaign splits into Low Rank (Chapters 1-3, HR 1-7) and High Rank (Chapters 4-6, HR 8+). Your first priority: get your weapon to +2 or +3 before challenging Balahara or any apex predator. Upgrade materials are most abundant in the central plains zone—grind there until you're comfortable with your gear.

Pro Tip: Status weapons (Poison, Sleep, Paralysis) outperform raw damage against monsters with no elemental weakness. Bring coatings and ammo to match.
