Battle Bus Briefing — Chapter 7 Season 3

Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 3: Runners

Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 3, titled "Runners," has arrived with a fresh map, a new arsenal of weapons, and an innovative Sprites system. The season introduces the Shattered Coast map, the explosive Chaos Exploder Rifle, the reliable Lancehead Pistol, the mobility-focused Seven Sliders, and the collectible Sprites that add a new layer of strategy. Here is everything you need to know about the new season.

This season leans harder into speed and repositioning than any previous chapter. Epic Games has reworked the island's flow so that rotations matter as much as aim, and the new tools are designed to keep players in constant motion. Whether you are a competitive player chasing Victory Royales, a casual player looking to complete the Battle Pass, or a builder who wants to master the new mobility items, understanding how the map, weapons, and Sprites interact is the key to staying ahead of the lobby. Below we break down each major addition and share practical loadout and rotation advice so you can drop in with confidence from day one.

I. The Shattered Coast Map

Chapter 7 Season 3 brings a major map overhaul with the Shattered Coast. This new region features a mix of coastal terrain, ruined structures, and new points of interest. The map is designed to reward fast movement and aggressive play, fitting the "Runners" theme of the season.

  • New POIs: Explore fresh named locations along the coast.
  • Coastal terrain: New verticality and water-based traversal options.
  • Ruined structures: Cover-rich areas perfect for close-quarters combat.

II. New Weapons

Season 3 introduces three standout weapons that are reshaping the meta:

Chaos Exploder Rifle

The Chaos Exploder Rifle is the season's headline weapon. It fires explosive rounds that deal area damage, making it devastating in close-quarters and against groups. Its explosive nature means you need to manage your positioning carefully to avoid self-damage, but in the right hands it is a game-changer.

Lancehead Pistol

The Lancehead Pistol is a reliable sidearm that fills the gap between early-game pistols and late-game SMGs. It offers solid damage, good accuracy, and fast handling, making it a versatile secondary weapon for any loadout.

Seven Sliders

The Seven Sliders are a mobility-focused item that lets you slide across the map at high speed. They are perfect for rotating, escaping the storm, or closing distance on an enemy. Mastering the Sliders is key to playing the "Runners" theme effectively.

III. The Sprites System

The new Sprites system adds collectible creatures to the map. Sprites can be found in the wild and provide various benefits when collected:

  • Passive buffs: Some Sprites grant movement or combat bonuses.
  • Active abilities: Others can be deployed for a one-time effect.
  • Strategic value: Collecting Sprites early can give you an edge in the mid-game.
Pro Tip: Sprites are most valuable in the early game. Grab them during your initial landing to secure passive buffs before the first fight. Their effects can be the difference between winning and losing an early engagement.

IV. Season Strategy

To succeed in Chapter 7 Season 3, adapt your playstyle to the new tools:

  • Embrace mobility: The Seven Sliders and coastal terrain reward fast, aggressive play.
  • Use the Chaos Exploder wisely: Its area damage is powerful, but watch your own positioning.
  • Collect Sprites early: Secure passive buffs before the storm closes in.
  • Learn the new POIs: Knowing the Shattered Coast's layout gives you a major advantage in rotations.

V. Battle Pass & Rewards

As always, the new season brings a fresh Battle Pass with new skins, emotes, and cosmetics. The "Runners" theme is reflected throughout the pass, with fast-moving, athletic designs. Check the Battle Pass tab in-game to see the full lineup and plan your grind.

Final Tip: Chapter 7 Season 3 is all about speed and aggression. Master the Seven Sliders, learn the Shattered Coast, and collect Sprites early to dominate the new season. Drop in, run fast, and outplay the competition.
