1. Key Changes in Patch 26.12

Patch 26.12 went live on June 10, 2026 and is the last patch before MSI 2026. Riot spent this patch nerfing pro-flex picks while handing solo-queue catch-up buffs to several champions. The most significant impact falls on the top side of the map, with three simultaneous jungle nerfs and three simultaneous top lane buffs reshaping the meta.

7. Low Elo Special Considerations

Most S-tier picks perform similarly across elos, with one major asterisk: Rammus is even stronger in low elo than Emerald+. Lower-elo teams peel carries away from his taunt more slowly and itemize armor penetration less efficiently. Jax and Tristana also overperform below Diamond thanks to their forgiving 1v1 and reset patterns. Control mages (Viktor, Hwei) climb harder in higher elo where their skill ceiling pays off.

8. Teleport Timing Changes

Teleport's empowered shield window dropped from 30 seconds to 10 seconds — a subtle but punishing tweak for delayed backline TPs. Early-game roams and reactive teleports now require much sharper timing. Top laners must commit earlier or risk arriving without the shield.

  • Old TP: 30s shield window — forgiving for delayed backline engages
  • New TP: 10s shield window — punishes late TPs, rewards proactive positioning
  • Impact: Top laners must plan roams earlier. Bot lane TP ganks are higher-risk. Defensive TPs to save turret plates are less reliable.

9. MSI 2026 & Patch 26.13 Preview

Patch 26.13 is scheduled for June 24, 2026 and will be the official MSI 2026 tournament patch. It also releases the new champion Locke, though Locke will be disabled for MSI competitive play. Expect 26.13 to be a more conservative, pro-focused balance patch — Riot historically avoids large solo-queue swings on tournament patches.

What to Expect

  • Locke Release: New champion arrives in 26.13 but won't appear in MSI matches. Expect Locke to shake up solo queue significantly.
  • Conservative Balance: Minimal solo-queue changes; focus on pro-play stability for MSI.
  • MSI Meta: The 26.12 shifts — buffed top laners, nerfed pro-flex junglers — will define the tournament meta. Watch for Aatrox/Jax/Gwen priority in top lane and Syndra/Sylas in mid.
