With three new champion prototypes entering testing, a redesigned Herald mechanic, and fundamental changes to jungle camp timings, every role must adapt. This report provides data-driven analysis for climbing the ranked ladder effectively.

FAQ

Q: Is Yunara worth maining for climbing in S16?
Yes. Her crit-based passive scales exceptionally well into late game. She has a 51.2% overall win rate, rising to 54% in Diamond+. Master her E timing for trading in lane.
Q: How does Mel's projectile reflection interact with ultimates?
It reflects the first instance but does not interrupt channels. Against Caitlyn R, it reflects the bullet. Against Karthus R, it only reflects if Karthus is in your vision range when the passive triggers.
Q: What's the optimal jungle clear for low elo?
3-Camp into gank (Red → Blue → Gromp). Low elo laners overextend constantly. Punish early and snowball rather than farming for late game.
Q: Should I always contest first Herald?
Not always. If your lanes have no priority (enemy laners are pushing), contesting Herald solo is suicide. Instead, trade for Dragon or invade enemy's opposite-side jungle.
