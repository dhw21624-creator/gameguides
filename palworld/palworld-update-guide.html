I. Sakurajima Update (June 2024)

Level cap raised to 55. New island with Arena (PvP), Oil Rig Fortress raid, endgame boss Blazamut Ryu. New resource: Crude Oil for Plasteel crafting. 24 new Pals, new weapons (Laser Rifle, Flamethrower, Gatling Gun).

II. Feybreak Island (December 2024)

Island Overview

6x larger than Sakurajima. Located southwest of main islands. Features anti-air defense zone — disable 22 missile turrets or use water mount to land safely. Level 58+ cave dungeons with high-tier loot.

Access: Fisherman's Point (-482, -748) → Scorched Ashland (-814, -824) → sail/fly southwest. Collect Nightstar Sand on beaches at night for high-end crafting.

III. New Mechanics

Predator Pals

Aggressive variants with red aura and unique skills. Defeat for Predator Cores (craft Meowmere from Terraria crossover). Spawn locations: Direhowl (Sakurajima), Blazehowl Noct (Astral Mountains), Kitsun (night, Astral Mountains), and more.

Xenolord Raid

Endgame mechanical dragon boss for level 60 players. Must be deliberately summoned. Multi-phase battle with top-tier loot.

Expeditions & Research

Send Pals on automated resource-gathering missions. New research tech tree for advanced gear, base structures, and dungeon types.
