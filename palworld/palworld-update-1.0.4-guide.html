Palworld Update 1.0.4 Guide: New Missions, Fishing & Battle Balance

Category: Update Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Palworld

Palworld Update 1.0.4, released September 7, 2026, is a focused patch that expands the endgame with two new missions, rebalances fishing and several weapons, and improves base Pal behavior across the board. While it is not a content bomb like the Sakurajima or Feybreak updates, it smooths out the post-1.0 experience and adds meaningful quest content for players who have already beaten the main story. Here is everything in the patch and how it affects your play.

Short version: Two new endgame missions, fishing adjusted at Sunreach and the World Tree, Power Bomb and Core Eject Shotgun rebalanced, and base Pals now prioritize their skills much better.

New Missions: Beyond Dimensions & From the Far Side of the Moon

Update 1.0.4 adds two new missions aimed at endgame players: "Beyond Dimensions" and "From the Far Side of the Moon". Both are positioned after the main story, and they use mechanics introduced in the 1.0 endgame — dimensional rifts and the lunar zone respectively.

Beyond Dimensions

Beyond Dimensions is a rift-based quest that sends you through unstable dimensional portals to fight a series of powerful Pals and collect rift-stabilizing materials. The mission rewards include unique gear schematics and a rare Pal egg line. It is best attempted with a team of fully leveled combat Pals, as the rift encounters scale above normal endgame content.

From the Far Side of the Moon

From the Far Side of the Moon sends you to the far side of the lunar map region, where a new settlement and boss encounter wait. The quest ties into the game's spacefaring lore and awards a unique companion Pal plus cosmetic base decorations. Like Beyond Dimensions, it is clearly designed for players who have cleared the core endgame loop.

Both missions appear in your quest log after the main story ends. If you are mid-story, you will not see them yet — they are strictly post-game content.

Fishing Changes at Sunreach & the World Tree

The patch adjusts fishing in two locations: Sunreach and the World Tree. In Sunreach, fish spawn rates and catch quality have been tuned so that the high-tier fish available there are more consistent — players fishing for rare cooking ingredients will spend less time waiting for spawns. At the World Tree, the patch rebalances which fish appear and the distribution of junk vs. valuable catches, tightening the odds toward useful loot.

If you fish for materials (especially for the endgame food buffs that require rare fish), these changes are a quality-of-life win. Community testing suggests Sunreach is now the best location for the rarest fish, so adjust your farming route accordingly.

Battle Balance: Power Bomb & Core Eject Shotgun

Two notable combat adjustments landed in 1.0.4:

  • Power Bomb minimum distance: The Power Bomb weapon now has a minimum arming distance, meaning you cannot detonate it point-blank on your own position as effectively. This forces more deliberate spacing — use it to deny chokepoints or bomb enemies from range, not as a panic button under your feet.
  • Core Eject Shotgun damage up: The Core Eject Shotgun receives a damage increase, making it a stronger close-range option. Paired with its unique knockback, the shotgun is now a legitimate pick for players who like aggressive, in-your-face combat.

These changes nudge the weapon meta without breaking existing builds. If you were using Power Bomb as a self-defense blast, retrain your muscle memory — the new minimum distance can leave you vulnerable if you detonate too close.

Base Pals Prioritize Skills Better

One of the most welcome changes in 1.0.4 is the base AI overhaul: base Pals now prioritize their skills better. Previously, Pals assigned to work at a base would sometimes idle or wander when the most important task was available, wasting time on low-priority jobs. The patch improves task selection so that Pals seek out work matching their Work Suitability levels first, with idle Pals stepping in for high-urgency tasks more reliably.

In practice, this means your base runs more efficiently: cooking, farming, and crafting queues complete faster, and Pals with specialized skills (like watering or medicine production) actually do their specialty when it matters. If your base has been running with a big workforce, you may notice you can trim the headcount for the same output.

Work Suitability Lv 1 for Ancient Bark

As part of the skill-prioritization pass, the patch adds Work Suitability Lv 1 for Ancient Bark, a new suitability that lets Pals process the Ancient Bark resource properly. Players who had to manually harvest or process Ancient Bark at base can now assign a Pal to the task, freeing up time for other jobs. Check your Pals' Work Suitability listings — some Pals gained this skill retroactively.

Pal Combat & Utility Tweaks

The patch also includes targeted Pal improvements:

  • Lapure swim speed buffed: Lapure is now noticeably faster in water, making it a stronger pick for any area with heavy water traversal. If you keep a dedicated swim Pal, this is a straight upgrade.
  • Lunaris Effigies home in on target: Lunaris Effigies — the homing projectiles used by Lunaris-related abilities — now track their target more reliably. This tightens the Pal's combat effectiveness in chaotic fights where projectiles previously whiffed.

Neither change is build-defining, but both smooth out gameplay. Lapure's swim buff is handy for fishing routes and ocean exploration, while the Lunaris accuracy fix makes an underrated combat Pal noticeably better.

What Should You Do After Updating?

After installing 1.0.4, here is a practical checklist:

  1. Open your quest log and confirm both new missions appear (post-game saves only).
  2. Reassign base Pals to the new Ancient Bark Work Suitability if you had a manual processing loop.
  3. Test the Core Eject Shotgun again — the damage buff may make it worth adding to your loadout.
  4. Route your rare fish farming to Sunreach for the best catch quality.
  5. Try Lapure on your next water-heavy expedition; the swim speed buff is immediately noticeable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are the new missions available before finishing the main story?

A: No. Beyond Dimensions and From the Far Side of the Moon are post-game content and appear only after the main story is complete.

Q: Did 1.0.4 nerf Power Bomb?

A: Not exactly — it added a minimum arming distance, so point-blank detonations no longer work. At range, the weapon is unchanged and still strong.

Q: Do I need to do anything special for the Ancient Bark Work Suitability?

A: Just assign a compatible Pal to the task. Some Pals gained the suitability retroactively; check your base assignment menu.

Q: Is the update save-compatible?

A: Yes. Existing saves carry over normally, and both new missions unlock automatically for eligible post-game saves.

Conclusion

Palworld 1.0.4 is a solid quality-of-life patch with a real endgame hook. The two new missions give post-game players fresh goals, the base AI improvements save real time, and the fishing and weapon tweaks tighten the sandbox. Update, reassign your base, and go hunt down those rift encounters.
