PALPAD ENTRY — SAKURAJIMA EXPEDITION LOG

Palworld Sakurajima Update Guide 2026: New Island, Pals & Features

The Palpagos Islands have expanded once again. The Sakurajima Update adds a massive new volcanic island, a quest-gated entry system, the Pal Fusion mechanic, new Pals, PvP Arena changes, and challenging bosses. This guide covers everything you need to conquer Sakurajima — but be warned: this is level 45+ content. Don't attempt it under-leveled.

Welcome to Sakurajima Island

Sakurajima is a stunning high-level zone blending treacherous lava flows, serene cherry blossom groves, and ancient temple-like ruins. The centerpiece of the 2026 update, this island challenges even the most seasoned Pal tamers. The terrain is vertical and dangerous — bring a flying or swimming mount.

How to Access Sakurajima — "The Rumbling Earth" Quest

Access isn't immediately obvious. You won't find a simple flight path. You must complete a questline that starts with the Fisherman NPC at the small settlement southwest of the Plateau of Beginnings.

Quest: "The Rumbling Earth"

The Fisherman tasks you with crafting a special Compass using late-game materials. Once crafted, the Compass points the way to Sakurajima.

Pal Fusion System — Compatibility Guide

The Pal Fusion system lets you combine specific Pals to create hybrid offspring with unique stats and abilities. Fusion is not random — only specific parent combinations produce valid results.

Confirmed Fusion Combinations

Note: Gale-Wing hybrid confirmed by community testing — an absolute monster in the PvP arena.

Fusion Tips

  • Level matters — higher level parents produce offspring with better base stats
  • Passive traits may carry over — breed parents with desirable traits before fusing
  • Not all combinations work — incompatible pairs simply won't fuse
  • Fusion is permanent — both parent Pals are consumed in the process

New Pals on Sakurajima

The island introduces several new Pal species adapted to the volcanic and cherry blossom environments. Key additions include:

  • Blazamut Ryu — Endgame boss Pal, Fire/Dragon type, devastating AOE attacks
  • Sakurajima-native variants — existing Pals with unique color palettes and enhanced stats
  • New alpha Pals — field bosses with guaranteed rare drops

PvP Arena Changes

The Sakurajima Update significantly rebalances the PvP Arena. Key changes include:

  • Fusion Pals are arena-legal — expect Gale-Wing hybrids to dominate the meta
  • New arena map layouts — volcanic terrain introduces environmental hazards
  • Ranked matchmaking improvements — fairer pairings based on Pal power level

Boss Strategies

Blazamut Ryu — The Sakurajima Endboss

Type: Fire / Dragon | Level: 55 | Location: Volcanic Crater, Sakurajima

Strategy: Bring Water and Ice-type Pals. Blazamut Ryu's AOE lava attacks cover wide areas — keep your Pals spread out. Phase 2 adds Dragon-type attacks that ignore elemental resistances. Target the glowing vents on its back during its charge-up animations for massive bonus damage.
