I. What's New in Version 1.0

Palworld Version 1.0 is the culmination of over two years of Early Access development. Here is the headline feature list:

  • New Island Chain: At least two new major landmasses with unique biomes, environmental hazards, and region-exclusive Pals.
  • 15+ New Pals: Including the viral sensation Dupin, plus several legendary-tier additions to the Paldeck.
  • Endgame Content: A proper final boss encounter, expanded tower challenges, and a new raid tier beyond Xenolord.
  • Base Overhaul: Multi-floor building, dedicated storage systems, automated Pal task queuing, and new production chains.
  • Quality of Life: Pal loadouts, improved pathfinding, mass crafting, and a redesigned Pal management UI.
  • PvP Arena Expansion: Ranked matchmaking, seasonal rewards, and spectator mode.

II. Dupin: The Fan-Favorite Dark-Type Pal

If there is one Pal that stole the spotlight in the Version 1.0 reveal trailer, it is Dupin — a compact, expressive Dark-type creature with cartoonish long ears and a performer's flair. Its apparent ability to clone itself mid-combat makes it far more dangerous (and useful) than its cute appearance suggests.

Pro Tip: Dupin's Clone Assault partner skill effectively multiplies your DPS by spawning independent attackers. In boss fights, the clones draw aggro away from you while dealing chip damage — making Dupin a top-tier support Pal for solo players tackling tough encounters.

How to Catch Dupin

Because Dupin is a Dark-type Pal, it follows the established nocturnal spawn pattern. Here is your game plan for launch day:

  1. Hunt at Night: Dark Pals almost exclusively roam during nighttime hours. Set out after sunset and be prepared with a torch or light-emitting Pal.
  2. Target Biome: Based on its design and typing, Dupin likely spawns in forested or shadow-biome regions — check the new Version 1.0 islands first, as new Pals typically debut in new areas.
  3. Bring Dark Counters: Dragon-type Pals (like Jetragon or Quivern) deal super-effective damage against Dark. Bring Ultra Spheres or higher to secure the catch.
  4. Watch for Clones: During combat, Dupin may summon clones. Focus on the original — clones vanish when the main Dupin is captured or defeated.

Breeding Dupin

If wild hunting proves difficult, Dupin should also be obtainable from a Dark Egg. The most reliable method is breeding two Dupin together once you have a breeding pair, or using the standard Dark-type breeding chain: breed any two Pals whose combined Breeding Power averages to Dupin's value. Keep an eye on community calculators post-launch for exact combinations.

III. New Islands & Regions

Version 1.0 expands the Palpagos map with at least two major new landmasses. Based on pre-launch footage and developer commentary, here is what to expect:

Northern Archipelago

A chain of floating islands accessible only via flying mounts. Features extreme verticality, wind currents that boost flight speed, and the new Sky Leviathan field boss. Rich in Aetherium Ore, a new resource for endgame weapons and armor.

Abyssal Rift

An underground biome network beneath the existing map, accessible through collapsed cave entrances on Sakurajima and Feybreak. Home to Dark/Ground dual-type Pals and the Void Titan raid boss. Requires a level 55+ team and heat-resistant armor for the magma chambers.

V. Base Building Optimization

Version 1.0 introduces multi-floor construction and automated task queuing, making base layouts more flexible than ever. Here are the key optimizations to implement early:

Multi-Floor Efficiency: Place production stations (Assembly Lines, Furnaces) on the ground floor for Pal pathfinding ease. Move storage, crafting benches, and Pal beds to upper floors — Pals do not need to physically access storage for items to be available in the crafting menu.

VI. Beginner Tips for Version 1.0

  1. Start fresh for the full experience: While existing saves carry over, Pocketpair recommends a new save to experience the rebalanced progression curve and early-game story beats added in 1.0.
  2. Rush the Glider Pal: The earliest flying-capable Pal is now accessible by level 12. Grab it immediately — the new vertical terrain on 1.0 islands is punishing on foot.
  3. Build near a fast-travel point: With multi-floor bases, you want quick access. The new automated task queuing means you can manage production remotely, but you still need to be physically present to start new crafting queues.
  4. Stockpile Dark Fragments: If Dupin drops them (highly likely), you will need hundreds for condensing and crafting Dark-type gear. Hunt every nocturnal Dark Pal you encounter.
  5. Join the community Day 1: Breeding calculators and spawn maps will flood Reddit and Discord within hours of launch. Bookmark them.
