Palworld Sakurajima & Oil Rig Raid Guide 2026: Endgame Walkthrough & Best Pals

The Sakurajima update (June 2024) remains Palworld’s largest content expansion, delivering a massive Japanese-themed island, a raised level cap, and the game’s first true Stronghold raid. For survivors reaching endgame in 2026, mastering Sakurajima and the Oil Rig is the gateway to Plasteel gear and Legendary Schematics.

I. Exploring Sakurajima Island

Located in the northwest corner of the Palpagos map, Sakurajima is a visually stunning but brutally challenging endgame zone. Cherry blossoms line misty forests, while a smoking volcano dominates the center. The island is patrolled by the Moonflower Faction, a new enemy group wielding advanced weaponry and commanding powerful Pals like Selyne.

Key Sakurajima Stats

  • Level Cap: Raised to 55 — essential for surviving the island’s bosses.
  • New Pals & Variants: Over 40 new Pals and elemental Fusion variants introduced.
  • New Resource: Crude Oil — the foundation of Plasteel, the strongest material in the game.
  • Moonflower Tower: Endgame boss fight against Saya & Selyne atop the Sakura Dancing Shrine.

III. The Oil Rig Raid (Stronghold)

Controlled by the Rayne Syndicate, the Oil Rig is a massive offshore fortress located east of Marsh Island. This is Palworld’s first true Stronghold-style raid and represents the ultimate endgame challenge.

Oil Rig Raid Checklist

  1. Approach by Swimming: The rig has automated laser turrets that shoot down any flying mount instantly. Use Jormuntide or Azurobe (water mounts) to swim in undetected.
  2. Anti-Air Disable (Optional): If you must fly, locate and destroy the anti-air emplacements first — though this alerts the entire rig.
  3. Target: Crude Oil: Oil extractors scattered across the rig are the primary source of Crude Oil, required for Plasteel crafting.
  4. Yellow Chests = Legendary Loot: Large yellow chests hidden across multiple decks contain Legendary Schematics and high-tier ores. Search every level thoroughly.
  5. Enemy Density: Rayne Syndicate troops swarm in large numbers. Bring AoE weapons (Grenade Launcher, Rocket Launcher).

V. Blazamut Ryu Raid Strategy

Blazamut Ryu is the pinnacle raid boss of the Sakurajima update, fought via the Summoning Altar. This level 55 behemoth arrives with over 500,000 HP and a brutal two-phase fight on a ten-minute timer.

Pre-Raid Preparation

  • Summoning Altar: Build a dedicated raid base — Blazamut Ryu will destroy everything nearby. Craft with 100 Stone + 20 Paldium Fragment.
  • Slab Fragments: Collect 4 Blazamut Ryu Slab Fragments from glowing purple chests in Cherry Blossom Cave dungeons across Sakurajima. Combine them at a High-Quality Workbench.
  • Weapons: Guided Missile Launcher + Gatling Gun for sustained DPS.
  • Armor: Lightweight Plasteel Armor or Legendary Pal Metal gear with Heat Resistance.

Phase 1 — Fire/Dragon

Blazamut Ryu opens as a Fire/Dragon type. Deploy Water-type Pals: Jormuntide, Azurobe, Suzaku Aqua, or Penking. Keep your distance, let your Water Pals tank while you fire from the edges. Aim for the head for critical damage.

Phase 2 — Electric (Rebirth)

At 1 HP, Blazamut Ryu regenerates one-third of its health and shifts to Electric-type. Your Water party becomes instantly vulnerable. Switch to Ground-type Pals immediately for the second phase. Same tactics apply — maintain distance and sustain fire.

Rewards: Randomized high-tier loot including Ancient Civilization Cores, Training Crystals, and a Huge Dragon Egg (guaranteed Blazamut Ryu hatch).

VI. Moonflower Tower: Saya & Selyne

Found at the Sakura Dancing Shrine, this dual-boss encounter pits you against Saya (riding Selyne on a crescent moon) and Selyne herself (Dark/Normal type).

Recommended Setup: Dragon-type Pals (Jetragon, Quivern, Astegon) at level 55. Focus fire on Saya first while your Dragon Pals occupy Selyne. Dodge the purple AoE rings by rolling, and watch for falling icicles that freeze on contact. The Guided Missile Launcher deals ~14,000 damage per hit and dramatically shortens the fight.
