Valorant Sensitivity Converter

Convert your in-game sensitivity when switching mouse DPI settings.

How it works: eDPI (effective DPI) = in-game sensitivity × mouse DPI. When you change your mouse DPI, adjust sensitivity to keep the same eDPI and maintain muscle memory. The formula is: newSens = oldSens × oldDPI / newDPI
