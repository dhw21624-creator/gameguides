Arthur's Journal — Compendium Notes

Gold Medals & Perfect Pelts: The Road to 100%

The Gold Rush achievement demands 70 Gold Medals across RDR2's 104 story missions. Meanwhile, crafting every satchel and garment at the Trapper requires dozens of perfect pelts — and the game never tells you where to find the rarest animals. This guide covers both grinds with precision.

I. Gold Medal Mission Checklist — Easiest 30

Replay missions from the Progress menu. Replays give you default stats and a standard horse — your Arthur's current gear doesn't carry over. This makes certain missions harder on replay than on your first run. The missions below are chosen because their Gold requirements are achievable even with default loadouts.

II. Perfect Pelt Hunting Guide

Three rules for a perfect pelt: (1) the animal must be 3-star quality — study it with binoculars before shooting; (2) use the correct weapon listed in the compendium; (3) aim for the head or heart for a clean kill. The Buck Antler Trinket (kill the Legendary Buck) gives you a chance to upgrade a 2-star pelt to 3-star.

III. Legend of the East Satchel — All Materials

This satchel increases your carrying capacity for all items to 99. Yes — 99 of every tonic, ingredient, and material. It's the single most impactful craftable in the game.

  • Pearson requires: 7 Perfect Deer pelts, 2 Perfect Elk, 1 Perfect Badger, 1 Perfect Squirrel, 1 Perfect Panther, 1 Perfect Iguana, 1 Perfect Cougar, 1 Perfect Wolf, 1 Perfect Rabbit, 1 Perfect Bison

IV. Panther & Cougar Spawn Strategy

Panther (Hardest Pelt)

Panthers spawn in a single wooded area south of Braithwaite Manor (Old Harry Fen), exclusively at night. Place Predator Bait in the woods, hide behind a tree with your rifle drawn, and wait. The panther will circle behind you — activate Dead Eye the moment you hear the growl. One missed shot and you're dead.

Cougar

Reliable spawn: the road northwest of Owanjila, just past the trapper. Ride slowly. The cougar's red dot appears on your minimap moments before it charges. Use a Springfield Rifle with Express ammo — aim for the head as it runs straight at you.
