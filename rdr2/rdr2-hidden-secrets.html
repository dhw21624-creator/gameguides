I. Supernatural Encounters

UFO Sightings

Hani's Bethel (Heartlands): Visit at 2 AM under a half-moon. A UFO appears above the shack. Inside, find the "Sermon of the Mount" letter from the cult. Mount Shann: At the peak at night, spot a UFO in the distance. Requires clear weather.

The Vampire of Saint Denis

Find 5 cryptic messages written in blood on Saint Denis walls. Once all found, a vampire spawns in a back alley between midnight and 1 AM. Defeat him for the Ornate Dagger — a unique melee weapon that cannot be obtained any other way.

Ghost Train

Scarlett Meadows tracks, between 3 AM and 4 AM. A translucent train with spectral passengers roars past. One-time only per save file. Stand near the tracks at the right time.

IV. Serial Killer Mystery

Find 3 murder scenes with dismembered bodies: near Valentine (under railroad tracks), south of Wallace Station (under a rock), and south of Rhodes (by a tree). Each has a map piece. Combine them to find the killer's hideout near Valentine. Loot the basement for a unique knife and significant cash.

V. Time-Sensitive Secrets

Braithwaite Secret Gold: Post-Chapter 3, the Braithwaite Manor ruins hold a gold bar in a lockbox under the burnt floorboards. Aberdeen Pig Farm: Let them drug you. Return as John Marston to collect Arthur's money stored behind the portrait.
