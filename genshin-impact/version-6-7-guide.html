Genshin Impact Version 6.7 launched on July 1, 2026 and runs through August 11, 2026. This patch introduces the highly anticipated 5-star Cryo Claymore character Sandrone, opens the new Frost Moon Area, brings four limited-time events, buffs seven characters, and adds two premium character skins. Whether you're saving Primogems for Sandrone, exploring the new region, or optimizing your roster with the balance changes, this guide covers everything you need to know.

1. New Character: Sandrone (5★ Cryo Claymore)

Sandrone is the headline addition of Version 6.7 — a 5-star Cryo Claymore user whose kit revolves around battlefield control through freezing mechanics. Her Elemental Skill deploys a Frost Construct that pulses Cryo damage in an area, while her Elemental Burst unleashes a massive Cryo AoE that applies a unique Deep Freeze state to enemies, extending freeze duration and increasing Cryo DMG taken.

3. New Area: Frost Moon Area

The Frost Moon Area is the major map expansion of Version 6.7. This frozen region features new environmental mechanics including permafrost platforms that shift and crack under pressure, ice tunnels that connect distant areas, and a new world boss tied to Sandrone's ascension materials.

4. Version 6.7 Events

Four limited-time events run during Version 6.7, offering Primogems, Mora, character ascension materials, and exclusive event rewards.

Sunny Summer Fontinalia

The flagship event of Version 6.7. A summer-themed festival event featuring mini-games, story quests, and a free 4-star character or weapon reward. Participate across all phases to earn the maximum Primogem rewards and an exclusive event namecard.

Final Long-Range Sightlines

A combat challenge event focused on ranged combat. Use bow and catalyst characters to defeat waves of enemies from fixed positions. Features score-based rewards with increasing difficulty tiers. Characters with charged attack bonuses (Ganyu, Tighnari, Lyney) excel here.

Dance Dance Easy-Breeze Disco

A rhythm-based mini-game event. Match button prompts to the beat across multiple difficulty levels and music tracks. Rewards include Primogems, Mora, and talent books. This event is purely skill-based — no character investment required.

Ley Line Overflow

During this event period, you can claim double rewards from Blossom of Revelation and Blossom of Wealth ley lines using Original Resin. The perfect opportunity to stockpile Mora and character EXP books — especially useful for leveling Sandrone and any buffed characters you plan to build.

FAQ

Q: Is Sandrone worth pulling if I already have Ayaka or Wriothesley?
Sandrone occupies a different niche than Ayaka (burst-focused) and Wriothesley (Normal Attack-focused). Her Deep Freeze mechanic extends freeze windows and boosts team-wide Cryo DMG, making her a strong pull even if you own other Cryo DPS units. She synergizes particularly well with Hydro sub-DPS characters in Freeze teams.
Q: How long does it take to fully explore the Frost Moon Area?
Expect 6-10 hours for 100% exploration, depending on whether you use an interactive map. The permafrost platform puzzles add a new traversal mechanic that takes some adjustment.
Q: Which buffed character is most worth building now?
Yae Miko gains the most from the Version 6.7 changes. Her improved turret range and base damage make her significantly more consistent in both single-target and AoE content. Cyno's extended burst is also transformative if you enjoy his playstyle.
Q: Are the new skins limited-time?
Charlotte's Hurlock Variations is free during Sunny Summer Fontinalia and will be permanently available in the Shop afterward. Citlali's Whispers of Stars and Smoke has a discounted price during Version 6.7 and will remain available at full price after the patch.
Q: When does Version 6.8 start?
Version 6.8 is expected to launch on August 12, 2026, following the standard 6-week patch cycle. The Version 6.8 Special Program livestream will air approximately 10-12 days before launch.
