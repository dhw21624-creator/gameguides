Adventurers' Guild — Field Report 5.4

Patch 5.4 Meta Teams & Spiral Abyss Domination

Patch 5.4 redefined the meta with Mavuika's release, cementing Pyro hypercarry as a premier playstyle. Meanwhile, Neuvillette + Furina remains the most reliable 36-star combo for players who value consistency over speedrun clips. This guide covers the four strongest teams, character rankings, and F2P alternatives that clear Floor 12.

II. S-Tier Team: Mavuika Hypercarry

Team: Mavuika / Xilonen / Citlali / Bennett

Rotation: Mavuika E → Xilonen E Q → Citlali E Q → Bennett Q E → Mavuika Q + Charged Attack spam

Why it dominates: Mavuika's burst depends on Nightsoul stacks from Natlan teammates. Xilonen shreds enemy RES while Citlali provides Cryo application for Melt reactions. Bennett's ATK buff pushes Mavuika's burst into one-shot territory against stunned bosses. Without Xilonen or Citlali, Mavuika loses 40-50% damage — she is aggressively team-locked to Natlan supports.

Artifacts: Mavuika (Obsidian Codex 4pc), Xilonen (Scroll of the Hero 4pc), Citlali (Scroll 4pc or Tenacity), Bennett (Noblesse Oblige 4pc).

III. Most Consistent Team: Neuvillette + Furina

Team: Neuvillette / Furina / Kazuha / Zhongli (or Baizhu)

Rotation: Furina E Q → Zhongli E → Kazuha E Q → Neuvillette E Q + Charged Attack ×3

Why it's brain-dead easy: Neuvillette's Charged Attacks ignore positioning requirements and hit enemies in a massive horizontal beam. Furina's Fanfare stacks boost teamwide damage as HP fluctuates. Zhongli's shield eliminates all interruption risk. This team clears Floor 12 chambers with minimal effort at C0 with 4-star weapons.

Budget Alternative: Replace Zhongli with Layla (shield) and Kazuha with Sucrose (VV shred + EM share). Damage drops ~20% but still comfortably 36-stars.

IV. AoE Specialist: Nilou Bloom

Team: Nilou / Nahida / Kokomi / Dendro Traveler (or Collei)

Nilou Bloom excels against grouped enemies that aren't immune to Dendro damage. The current Abyss favors this team on the first half with its waves of mobs. Bountiful Cores ignore shields and deal true Dendro damage — ideal for shielded enemies that counter traditional DPS comps.

Warning: Nilou teams are hard-locked to Hydro + Dendro only. Bring a second team that can handle Cryo shields and single-target bosses.
