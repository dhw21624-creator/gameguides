All Nod-Krai Characters Leaked — Full Release Schedule & Guide

Disclaimer: All information below is based on leaks from reliable sources (Shiroha, Team Mew, HxG, Seele) and official teaser content. Release dates, elements, and rarities are subject to change until official HoYoverse announcements.

The Heavy Hitters: Harbingers & Legends

Columbina — The Third Fatui Harbinger (Version 6.3)

Element/Role: Hydro Catalyst — Hybrid (DPS + Support)
Why She Matters: Described as one of the most powerful characters in all of Teyvat, which justifies her Third Harbinger title. Her hybrid role suggests build flexibility rarely seen in Harbinger characters — she can function as both main DPS and team support depending on artifact investment.

Durin — The Shadow Dragon (Version 6.2)

Element/Role: Pyro or Anemo — Main DPS
Lore: A legendary shadow dragon created by Rhinedottir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. He fought Dvalin and his resurrection/rehabilitation arc is expected to be a major Nod-Krai story beat.
Playable Significance: If real, Durin would be one of the most unique character models in the game — a full dragon playable character rather than a human-form vessel.

The Long-Awaited Arrivals

Varka — Grand Master of the Knights (Version 6.5)

After being mentioned since the game's launch, Varka is FINALLY arriving. The Grandmaster of the Knights of Favonius is expected as an Anemo Claymore user — fitting for Mondstadt's military leader. His role as an Anemo DPS suggests crowd-control synergy with Viridescent Venerer, likely positioning him as a premium on-field carry.

Alice — Klee's Mother (Version 6.7)

The legendary adventurer and Hexenzirkel member Alice will arrive as a Pyro DPS in Version 6.7. This makes perfect sense given her daughter Klee's affinity for explosions. As one of the most powerful and knowledgeable characters in Teyvat's lore, expectations are sky-high for her kit design.
