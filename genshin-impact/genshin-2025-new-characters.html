FAQ

Q: Should I pull Lauma or Flin first in 6.0?
If you have strong Hydro units (Yelan, Xingqiu, Kokomi), prioritize Lauma for Lunar-Bloom teams. If you prefer Electro-charged or Aggravate teams, Flin is your pick.
Q: Is Aino worth building as a free 4-star?
Yes. Aino provides solid off-field Electro application and energy generation. She's a strong F2P option for Lunar-Bloom and Aggravate teams.
Q: How does Lunar-Bloom differ from regular Bloom?
Lunar-Bloom cores have a larger AoE radius and deal bonus Dendro damage based on Lauma's Elemental Mastery. The reaction requires Lauma in the party.
Q: When will Natlan's remaining regions unlock?
Natlan will expand through Versions 6.1-6.3, similar to Fontaine's rollout pattern. Expect the volcano summit and colosseum areas next.
