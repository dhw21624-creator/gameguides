Genshin Impact's 2025-2026 content roadmap is anchored by the Natlan region, the nation of Pyro. Natlan introduced new character archetypes, traversal mechanics unique to the region, and a continuation of the Traveler's journey through Teyvat. The Natlan expansion began with Version 5.0 and continues through the 5.x patch cycle.

This guide covers the key features of the Natlan era, from officially released character overviews and team building strategies to elemental reaction fundamentals and endgame content planning. Whether you're a day-one player or a recent arrival to Teyvat, this information will help you navigate the evolving world of Genshin Impact.

Spiral Abyss Tips (5.x Era)

  • Floor 12 Lineup Awareness: Check the current blessing and enemy lineup before building your teams. Floor 12 blessings can significantly shift which characters perform best.
  • Element Coverage: Bring at least one character who can handle elemental shields common in the current rotation.
  • Energy Management: Floor 12 often has energy-draining mechanics. Characters with low burst costs or strong energy generation (Favonius weapons, Raiden Shogun) are valuable.
  • Team Flexibility: Build two versatile teams that can handle single-target and AoE scenarios. The meta shifts with each Abyss reset.

Primogem Farming (F2P)

Free-to-play players can earn approximately 8,000-10,000 Primogems per patch cycle through:

  • Daily Commissions: 60 Primogems/day
  • Spiral Abyss (Floors 9-12): up to 600 Primogems per reset
  • Events: varies by patch (typically 400-1,000 per event)
  • Exploration and quest completion: varies by region
  • Paimon's Bargains: 5 Intertwined + 5 Acquaint Fates per month

FAQ

Q: Which Natlan 5-star character should I prioritize?
Your pull priority should depend on your existing roster. Mualani excels in Vaporize teams if you have strong Pyro supports (Xiangling, Bennett). Kinich is a strong Dendro DPS if you can maintain Burning uptime. Xilonen provides universal support value. Check current banner schedules and community tier lists for the latest recommendations.
Q: Which elemental reaction team is best for Natlan content?
The best reaction team depends on your character roster and the specific content. Hyperbloom teams (Dendro + Hydro + Electro) remain one of the most consistent and low-investment options. Vaporize teams with Mualani offer high single-target damage for boss fights. For general Natlan exploration, any well-built team with a Natlan character for traversal mechanics will work well.
Q: Are the Local Legend bosses required for story progression?
No, they're optional challenges. However, their rewards are valuable for character ascension and talent leveling. Defeating them weekly accelerates your account progression significantly.
Q: Will there be a new element in Natlan?
No new elements have been announced. Natlan focuses on expanding existing elemental systems with new reactions and character mechanics rather than introducing a seventh element.
Q: How much Primogems can I save for Natlan?
F2P players can save approximately 8,000-10,000 Primogems per patch. Starting saving at version 5.5 gives you enough for one guaranteed 5-star character (14,400 Primogems) by 6.0 launch.
