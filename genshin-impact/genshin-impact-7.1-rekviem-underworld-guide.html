Genshin Impact 7.1 "A Rekviem for the Underworld" Guide: Vesna, Vodyanitsa & New Boss

Category: Version Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Genshin Impact

Genshin Impact Version 7.1, "A Rekviem for the Underworld," launched on September 23, 2026, and continues the year's story arc with two new 5-star characters, the next chapters of the Archon Quest, and a new world boss built for Vodyanitsa's ascension. It also lands in the middle of the anniversary window, so the selector deadlines matter as much as the banners. This guide covers everything you need: who to pull, what the new content adds, and which endgame dates to mark on your calendar.

Quick take: Vesna and Vodyanitsa headline Version 7.1, the new "Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow" boss is required for Vodyanitsa ascension materials, and anniversary 5-star selectors expire November 3 — redeem before the deadline.

New Characters: Vesna & Vodyanitsa

Version 7.1's event banners introduce two new 5-star characters:

  • Vesna — the first half's headline 5-star, bringing a new kit built around the version's star-related reaction identity.
  • Vodyanitsa — the second half's 5-star, whose ascension materials come from the new world boss added in this patch.

If you plan to build Vodyanitsa early, pre-farm the new boss the moment it unlocks, since the "Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow" is the only source of her ascension drops. Both characters benefit from the Spiral Abyss buffs this cycle — see below — so a 7.1-focused roster is unusually well-positioned for Floor 12 this month.

Archon Quest Chapter VII: Acts III & IV

The story continues with two new Archon Quest chapters. Chapter VII Acts III & IV push the "Rekviem for the Underworld" narrative forward, expanding on the version's central themes and introducing new story content tied to Vesna and Vodyanitsa's lore. As usual, completing the Archon Quest chapters also unlocks the banner-relevant story contexts and rewards that make the update feel substantial beyond the gacha.

New World Boss: Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow

The patch's new World Boss is the Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow, a snow-themed confrontation designed around the new reaction meta. Two things matter for practical players:

  • Ascension drops: the boss is the exclusive source of Vodyanitsa's ascension materials, so expect to farm it for weeks if you pull her.
  • Combat identity: the fight favours teams that lean into the star-related reactions the version is pushing, matching the Floor 12 buff design below.

Bring a flexible roster — the boss punishes one-element spam, so a team with two different reaction drivers clears faster than a hypercarry nuke.

Endgame Rotations & Anniversary Deadlines

Version 7.1's endgame calendar is packed, and two dates matter most for planning:

EventDateWhat Changes
Imaginarium Theater refreshOctober 1 & November 1New character pools and rotation modifiers
Spiral Abyss refreshOctober 16Floor 12 buffs favour star-related reactions (+75% star reaction DMG and related bonuses)

If you have an unredeemed anniversary selector, do not leave it to the last minute — the November 3 deadline is firm. And if you are pushing Floor 12 this cycle, the +75% star reaction damage buff is the single biggest team-building signal: run star-reaction teams over raw hypercarry setups until the next Abyss rotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When did Version 7.1 release?

A: September 23, 2026.

Q: Who is the better pull, Vesna or Vodyanitsa?

A: Both are strong in this cycle's meta. Vesna is the safer generalist pick; Vodyanitsa is more powerful in star-reaction teams and needs the new boss farmed for her ascension.

Q: Where do I get Vodyanitsa ascension materials?

A: From the new World Boss, Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow, added in Version 7.1.

Q: When does the anniversary selector expire?

A: November 3, 2026.

Conclusion

Version 7.1 is a banner-and-boss update done right: two genuinely useful 5-stars, an Archon Quest chapter that advances the year's story, and a new world boss that ties directly into Vodyanitsa's build. Farm the Guardian Blade early, build star-reaction teams for the October Abyss, and redeem your anniversary selector before November 3. That is the whole 7.1 checklist — enjoy the Underworld.
