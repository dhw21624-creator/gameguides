Infinite Rupees & Hidden Secrets of Hyrule

Even in 2026, Tears of the Kingdom continues to reward explorers who know where to look. This guide covers the most reliable rupee farming exploit still working in the latest patch, plus hidden armor locations, rare weapon spawns, and Zonai device combinations that transform how you traverse Hyrule.

I. The Infinite Rupee Glitch (Still Working in 2026)

Patch Status: Confirmed working on all versions through 2026. This is a tutorial-requirement glitch — not a cheat, but a sequence break.

How It Works

Complete the entire Great Sky Island tutorial without picking up a single material. No mushrooms, no Brightbloom Seeds, no Zonai Charges — nothing. Once you descend to Hyrule and speak to any merchant through their dialogue selling interface, the game's sell menu glitches: weapon, bow, and shield categories become selectable. You can now sell your equipment — even Key Items — for rupees. The game prevents you from permanently losing essential items by keeping them in your inventory after the sale, but the rupees are credited.

Step-by-step:

  1. Start new game or load a save on Great Sky Island before collecting materials
  2. Complete all four shrines and obtain the Recall ability
  3. Dive to Hyrule without collecting any materials (this is the hard part)
  4. Find any merchant (Lookout Landing has the easiest access)
  5. Open the sell menu — weapons/shields/bows are now sellable
  6. Sell high-value weapons like the Royal Guard's Claymore (found in Hyrule Castle)

II. Hidden Hylian Shield Location

The Hylian Shield is in the Hyrule Castle Docks. From the Lookout Landing Skyview Tower, glide northeast toward Hyrule Castle. Drop into the docks area on the castle's western side. Inside a large chamber with a Stalnox, light the central brazier with fire — the chest containing the Hylian Shield appears. If it breaks, you can rebuy it from Cece in Hateno Village for 3,000 rupees after completing her questline.

V. Best Rupee Farming Methods (Non-Glitch)

Method 1: Rare Talus Hunting

Each Rare Stone Talus drops 1,000-2,000 rupees worth of gems. There are 8 Rare Taluses in Hyrule. Full circuit: ~12,000 rupees per Blood Moon. Use a Stone Smasher or Cobble Crusher for 2x damage against Talus ore deposits.

Method 2: Meat Skewer Sales

Hunt moose and bears in the Hebra region with a bow. Cook 5 Gourmet Meats into a Meat Skewer — sells for 315 rupees each. A 20-minute hunting session yields ~5,000 rupees.

Method 3: Lynel Guts Elixir

Cook 4 Lynel Guts + 1 Tireless Frog. The resulting elixir sells for 1,443 rupees. Silver Lynels drop 2-4 guts each.
