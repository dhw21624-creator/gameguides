Hyrule Field Notes — For New Travelers

Zelda TOTK Beginner Guide: 15 Essential Tips

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is a vast, open-ended adventure that can feel overwhelming at first. With the Depths, the Sky Islands, and a host of new mechanics, knowing where to start is half the battle. This beginner guide covers 15 essential tips to get you on the right path, from your opening route to mastering Ultrahand and Fuse.

I. Getting Started: The Opening Route

Your first hours in TOTK set the tone for the entire adventure. Here is how to make the most of them:

  • Follow the main quest initially: The opening quests teach you the core mechanics and give you essential gear.
  • Complete the Sky Island tutorial: Do not rush through the tutorial — it teaches Ultrahand, Fuse, and Ascend, which you will use constantly.
  • Head to Rito Village early: The Rito region offers the Tulin ability, which is one of the most useful traversal tools in the game.

II. Understanding Gloom & Sundelion

The Depths are filled with gloom, a purple corruption that damages your hearts and reduces your maximum health. Understanding how to counter it is essential:

  • Gloom damage: Gloom reduces your max hearts until you heal at a Lightroot or with specific items.
  • Sundelion: This flower is your best friend in the Depths. Cook it to make meals that restore gloom-affected hearts.
  • Lightroot: These glowing roots act as fast-travel points in the Depths and restore your gloom-damaged hearts when you touch them.
Key Tip: Always carry Sundelion-based meals before descending into the Depths. They are the only reliable way to restore gloom-damaged hearts while exploring.

III. Mastering Ultrahand & Fuse

These two abilities are the heart of TOTK's creativity:

Ultrahand

Ultrahand lets you pick up, move, and attach objects together. Use it to build vehicles, bridges, and contraptions. The possibilities are nearly endless — experiment freely.

Fuse

Fuse lets you combine weapons, shields, and arrows with materials to create powerful new items. Fusing a rock to a stick creates a hammer; fusing a monster part to an arrow adds elemental damage. Always fuse before combat.

IV. 15 Essential Beginner Tips

  1. Do the tutorial thoroughly — it teaches mechanics you will use all game.
  2. Head to Rito Village early for the Tulin ability.
  3. Always carry Sundelion meals before exploring the Depths.
  4. Activate Lightroots as you explore the Depths for fast travel.
  5. Fuse everything — never fight with an unfused weapon.
  6. Use Ultrahand to build — even simple contraptions solve problems.
  7. Cook meals with buffs — attack and defense buffs are game-changers.
  8. Upgrade your armor at Great Fairy fountains.
  9. Mark interesting locations on your map as you explore.
  10. Don't fear the Depths — bring light sources and gloom cures.
  11. Use Ascend to escape caves and reach high ground.
  12. Paraglide strategically — it is your best traversal tool.
  13. Complete shrines for heart and stamina upgrades.
  14. Talk to everyone — NPCs give quests and useful hints.
  15. Experiment freely — TOTK rewards creativity above all.

V. Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

  • Rushing the main quest: Take time to explore and upgrade.
  • Ignoring Fuse: Unfused weapons are dramatically weaker.
  • Entering the Depths unprepared: Always bring Sundelion meals and light.
  • Neglecting armor upgrades: Defense matters more than you think.
  • Not using Ascend: It is one of the most versatile abilities in the game.
Final Tip: Tears of the Kingdom is a game about curiosity and creativity. Follow the tips above to build a strong foundation, then let your imagination run wild. The best experiences in Hyrule come from experimenting with Ultrahand, Fuse, and the world around you.
