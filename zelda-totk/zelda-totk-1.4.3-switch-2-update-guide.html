Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom Ver 1.4.3 & Switch 2 Edition Update Guide

Category: Update Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Tears of the Kingdom's latest patch, Ver 1.4.3 (released February 17/18, 2026 as part of Zelda's 40th anniversary wave), is a small but meaningful fix for completionists: it resolves the Black Hinox medal bug and includes general stability fixes. It also follows up on the Switch 2 Edition support that arrived in Ver 1.4.0. This guide covers everything in the current update line, from the Hinox fix to ZELDA NOTES, and explains exactly how to update your game and what the Switch 2 Edition includes.

Quick take: Update to Ver 1.4.3 to unlock the Hinox Monster Medal (the Black Hinox at Hyrule Castle no longer blocks it). The Switch 2 Edition adds ZELDA NOTES, amiibo support for Sidon, Tulin, Yunobo, and Riju, plus Brazilian Portuguese support.

Ver 1.4.3: The Black Hinox Medal Fix

The headline fix in Ver 1.4.3 targets a long-standing completion blocker: the Black Hinox in Hyrule Castle could appear undefeated even after you defeated it, which kept the Hinox Monster Medal from unlocking. With the fix applied, the medal can now be claimed normally. The update also includes general fixes for stability and other reported issues, making it a safe install for everyone.

If you were stuck on the medal track, update first, then check the Hyrule Castle Black Hinox once more — the medal should register on your next visit.

Switch 2 Edition: What Ver 1.4.0 Brought

Ver 1.4.0 (June 3, 2025) was the big Switch 2 Edition update. It added three headline features:

  • ZELDA NOTES — an in-game feature layer tied to the Switch 2 Edition, giving players new note and tracking tools during play.
  • New amiibo support — Sidon, Tulin, Yunobo, and Riju amiibo now work with the game.
  • Brazilian Portuguese — full language support added for Brazilian players.

Ver 1.4.2 followed with targeted fixes for ZELDA NOTES Daily Bonus bugs — specifically the Health Recovery / Hearty Meal bonus not behaving correctly at max hearts — plus a fix for Switch 2 battle music.

How to Update TOTK

Keeping Tears of the Kingdom current is straightforward on either console:

  • Automatic: go to System Settings > Internet and make sure software auto-update is enabled; the console downloads updates in the background while connected.
  • Manual: on the game's tile in the home menu, press the + (or -) button, then select Software Update > Via the Internet.

Once updated, you can verify the version number from the same menu — it should read 1.4.3.

Switch 2 Edition: How to Get It & Save Transfer

The Switch 2 Edition is available as an upgrade path through the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack. If you own the base game, the upgrade keeps your progress: save data carries over from the original Switch version, so you can jump straight back into Hyrule without restarting. For players on the standard Switch, the Ver 1.4.x updates remain fully compatible — the Switch 2 features are additive, not exclusive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does Ver 1.4.3 change?

A: It fixes the Black Hinox in Hyrule Castle appearing undefeated after defeat — which was blocking the Hinox Monster Medal — plus general fixes.

Q: How do I get the Switch 2 Edition?

A: It is available via the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack upgrade for owners of the base game.

Q: Will I lose my save if I upgrade?

A: No — save data carries over to the Switch 2 Edition.

Q: Is Ver 1.4.3 available on the standard Switch?

A: Yes, the update is available for all versions of the game, and Ver 1.4.x features are additive rather than Switch 2-exclusive.

Conclusion

Tears of the Kingdom's 2026 update line is small but valuable: Ver 1.4.3 finally lets completionists claim the Hinox Monster Medal, while the Switch 2 Edition features from Ver 1.4.0 and 1.4.2 — ZELDA NOTES, the Sidon/Tulin/Yunobo/Riju amiibo support, and the language additions — keep the game fresh four years on. Update to 1.4.3, claim your medal, and enjoy the 40th-anniversary wave.
