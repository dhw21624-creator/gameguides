Advanced Zonai Building Guide

Hoverbikes — Battle Tanks — Flying Fortresses — Maximum Energy Efficiency

Overview

The Ultrahand ability and Zonai device ecosystem are what elevate Tears of the Kingdom from a great Zelda game to a genre-defining sandbox. Two years after launch, the community has refined building techniques to an art form. This guide compiles the most efficient, battle-tested vehicle designs — from the essential hoverbike every player should know, to multi-story flying fortresses and optimized korok-transport solutions. Every blueprint has been validated for energy efficiency, stability, and combat effectiveness.

Prerequisites: Maxed-out Energy Well (16 full batteries), Autobuild ability, and at least 100 Zonaite in your inventory for on-demand fabrication.

1. The Air Bike (Essential Hoverbike) — 9 Zonaite

The most famous build in TOTK. The Air Bike is a two-fan, one-steering-stick vehicle that provides unlimited flight for a minimal Zonaite cost. It handles all terrain, reaches any sky island, and costs only 9 Zonaite to Autobuild.

Components: 2 Fans + 1 Steering Stick Assembly: Attach Fan #1 to the front of the Steering Stick at a 45-degree upward angle. Attach Fan #2 to the back, angled 45 degrees downward. The stick should tilt slightly forward for neutral flight. Energy Drain: ~0.8 cells per second at neutral throttle — 20+ minutes of flight with max battery.
Pro Tip: Slightly adjust the rear fan angle if your Air Bike drifts left or right. No two builds are perfectly identical — micro-corrections with Ultrahand before saving to Autobuild favorites resolve drift.

2. The Lynel Buster (Combat Tank) — 27 Zonaite

Designed specifically for Lynel and Gleeok encounters. A Big Wheel chassis with 4 Beam Emitters on a Construct Head turret provides auto-targeting firepower while you focus on dodging. The sled base prevents the tank from flipping during Lynel ground pounds.

Components: 1 Sled (base) + 4 Big Wheels + 1 Construct Head + 4 Beam Emitters + 1 Steering Stick + 1 Shock Emitter (optional) Assembly: Place Steering Stick on Sled. Attach Wheels to corners. Mount Construct Head on a raised platform (use a wooden board). Attach Beam Emitters to the Construct Head (they auto-aim). Add Shock Emitter for AoE stun. Energy Drain: ~2.5 cells/sec with all weapons active. Deploy only for major encounters.

3. The Sky Fortress (Combat Airship) — 45 Zonaite

A massive floating platform with 6 fans, a full turret array, and space for a second player (via Sages). Use this to raid monster camps from above — enemies cannot hit airborne targets that stay above their vertical range.

Components: 1 Wing (from any Sky Island dispenser) + 6 Fans + 2 Construct Heads + 6 Beam Emitters + 1 Steering Stick + 2 Rockets (for initial launch) Assembly: Place 4 Fans under the Wing for lift, 2 Fans on the back for forward thrust. Mount Steering Stick in the center. Place Construct Head + Beam Emitter pair on each wingtip. Rockets on the sides for emergency escapes. Energy Drain: 4-5 cells/sec. Short flight duration (~5 min) but devastating in that window.

3 Creative Community Builds Worth Trying

  1. The Korok Space Program: Attach a Korok to a Stake, then attach 5 Rockets to the Stake. Fire all rockets simultaneously. The Korok launches into the stratosphere. Technically counts as "delivering" the Korok to its friend if they're on a sky island.
  2. The Bridge-on-Demand: Save a 20-meter wooden plank bridge (planks + stakes on each end) to Autobuild. Deploy it to cross any chasm instantly. Works on lava if you use metal plates instead of wood.
  3. The Portable House: Attach a Cooking Pot, a Travel Medallion, and a Flame Emitter to a wooden platform. Save to Autobuild. Deploy anywhere for instant cooking and fast-travel point.

FAQ

Q: How do I get more Energy Wells quickly?

Farm Zonaite in the Depths (under Central Hyrule has the highest density). Each Energy Well upgrade costs 100 Crystallized Charges (300 Zonaite at a Forge Construct). Prioritize mining camps with multiple ore deposits — a single run under the Great Plateau yields ~150 Zonaite.

Q: Why does my Air Bike drift to one side?

Fan attachment angle asymmetry. The front fan must be exactly centered with a 45-degree upward tilt. Even a 2-degree offset causes drift. Rebuild from scratch rather than adjusting — it's faster to get a clean alignment.

Q: Can flying machines reach the highest sky islands?

Yes, but you need at least 8 full batteries. The Air Bike climbs ~10 meters per second at full throttle. The highest sky islands (above the Temple of Time) are ~2,000 meters up — that's roughly 3.5 minutes of climbing. Use Recall on falling debris or a Rocket-assisted launch to save battery.

Master Engineer

The Air Bike alone transforms TOTK's traversal. Build it, favorite it, and never walk across Hyrule Field again. For combat, the Lynel Buster tank trivializes even Silver Lynels — the auto-turret deals ~120 damage per second while you hold shield. Prioritize your Energy Well upgrades (max battery is 16 cells — each cell is a third of a full battery ring), always carry 100+ Zonaite for Autobuild, and experiment with the building system. The best vehicle is the one you design yourself. Happy engineering, Hylian.
