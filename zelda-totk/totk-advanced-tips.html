I. Advanced Combat Techniques

Perfect Dodge & Flurry Rush Mastery

The Flurry Rush window is generous, but the real skill is knowing when not to use it. Against Lynels, side-hop their charging slash (not backflip) for a longer punish window. Against Gleeoks, sprint perpendicular to their beam then jump for bullet-time arrow shots to each head.

II. Zonai Device Engineering

The Hoverbike (Essential)

2 Fans + 1 Steering Stick. Angle fans 45 degrees down-forward. This costs 9 Zonaite and flies indefinitely on 3 battery cells. The single most efficient traversal tool in the game.

Pulse Laser Turret

Construct Head + 4 Beam Emitters arranged in a diamond. The head tracks enemies while lasers fire in pulses for 3x efficiency. Deploy near enemy camps or during siege segments.

Spring Shield

Fuse a Spring to any shield. Shield-surfing launches you high enough for bullet-time. Zero durability cost on the Spring. Replaces Revali's Gale.
