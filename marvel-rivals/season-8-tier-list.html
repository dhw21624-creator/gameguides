HERO FILES — SEASON 8 TIER LIST

Marvel Rivals Season 8 Hero Tier List & Meta Guide

Season 8 of Marvel Rivals is in full swing, and the competitive meta has crystallized around a clear set of dominant heroes. Based on win rate data collected through July 5, 2026 from Counterwatch.gg, this tier list ranks heroes by shrunk win rate — a methodology that pulls each hero's raw win rate toward the role baseline, weighted by sample size. This prevents small-sample flukes from topping the charts.

The data covers all ranks, with S-Tier heroes beating the role baseline by a significant margin with enough sample size to trust the numbers. Keep in mind that the meta shifts dramatically between ranks — a hero dominant in Bronze may underperform in Grandmaster. Use the role-specific breakdowns to find the best picks for your playstyle.

How the Tier List Works

Heroes are ranked by shrunk win rate — raw win rate pulled toward the role baseline and weighted by match sample size. S-Tier means the hero beats the role baseline by a wide margin with enough data to trust it. F-Tier means the opposite: enough games to be confident the hero is underperforming.

Peni Parker (Vanguard) — 55.9% Win Rate

Peni Parker is the undisputed queen of Season 8. Her mine network generates passive value without requiring teammate coordination, making her the rare Vanguard who dominates both solo queue and organized play. She controls space effortlessly, her ultimate is one of the most punishing area-denial tools available, and she requires no babysitting from supports. With a 25% pick rate and very high confidence in the data, Peni's position at the top is rock-solid. If you learn only one hero this season, make it Peni Parker.

Magik (Duelist) — 55.4% Win Rate

Magik's win rate is staggering for a Duelist. Her portal mechanic makes her the most unpredictable dive threat in the game, and her crowd control output rivals most Vanguards. At 19% pick rate, she is popular but not ubiquitous — which means many lobbies still don't know how to handle her. Her CC chains and repositioning make her nearly impossible to punish when played correctly. She is a high-priority ban in competitive for good reason.

Rocket Raccoon (Strategist) — 54.6% Win Rate

Rocket Raccoon is the most-picked Strategist at 34% and carries a 54.6% win rate. His combination of consistent healing output, mobility, and the ability to resurrect teammates via his B.R.B. beacon gives his team a permanent safety net. His small hitbox makes him difficult to dive, and his team-up synergy with Groot adds even more value. At 21.4 assists per 10 minutes, Rocket is the engine that keeps his team running.

Devil Dinosaur (Vanguard) — 53.3% Win Rate

The newest Vanguard has already earned S-Tier placement with a 53.3% win rate and the highest pick rate on the list at 47%. Devil Dinosaur brings devastating crowd control, high survivability, and a disruptive ultimate that reshapes the frontline. He is the most popular Vanguard by far, and his numbers justify the hype.

Ultron (Strategist) — 53.2% Win Rate

Ultron's 53.2% win rate on a 12% pick rate suggests he is underplayed relative to his power level. His drone deployment provides persistent healing and damage, and he can pivot into a DPS threat when the situation demands. Ultron does not have a weak matchup — he fits into virtually any composition.

Daredevil (Duelist) — 53.1% Win Rate

Daredevil is the best pure melee Duelist investment. His combo loop is punishing once mastered — he can tag and track targets through walls, making isolated supports miserable. At 14% pick rate, he is less contested than Magik but delivers comparable results. The skill ceiling is real, but a 53.1% win rate with very high confidence is proof that the investment pays off.

Notable A-Tier: Mantis

Mantis sits at the top of A-Tier with a 52.9% win rate and 15% pick rate. Her ability to damage-boost allies while providing consistent healing makes her the most flexible Strategist after Rocket Raccoon. She enables aggressive dive compositions without sacrificing sustain — a rare combination that keeps her relevant at every rank.

Notable A-Tier: Captain America

At 52.1% win rate with a 14% pick rate, Captain America is the sleeper pick of A-Tier. His shield provides unmatched defensive utility, and his mobility lets him peel for supports or pressure backlines depending on the situation. He is the best Vanguard for players who want to carry through game sense rather than mechanics.

III. Role-Specific Breakdown

Vanguard (Tanks)

S-Tier: Peni Parker (55.9%), Devil Dinosaur (53.3%)
A-Tier: Captain America (52.1%), Bruce Banner (51.8%), Thor (51.3%)
Peni Parker and Devil Dinosaur define the frontline meta. Peni's passive mine damage and area denial make her the best solo-queue Vanguard, while Devil Dinosaur's high pick rate (47%) reflects his overwhelming popularity and effectiveness. Captain America and Thor offer strong alternatives for different playstyles.

Duelist (DPS)

S-Tier: Magik (55.4%), Daredevil (53.1%)
A-Tier: Hawkeye (51.5%), Star-Lord (51.0%), Psylocke (50.8%)
Magik is the #1 Duelist by a clear margin, with Daredevil close behind. Both are melee assassins that reward mastery — Magik through CC chains and portal repositioning, Daredevil through wall-tracking and combo execution. Hawkeye and Star-Lord provide reliable ranged alternatives.

Strategist (Support)

S-Tier: Rocket Raccoon (54.6%), Ultron (53.2%)
A-Tier: Mantis (52.9%)
Rocket Raccoon dominates the Strategist role with the highest pick rate (34%) and a 54.6% win rate. His B.R.B. resurrection beacon is the single most impactful support ability in the game. Ultron provides DPS flexibility, and Mantis rounds out the top tier with damage amplification.

IV. Rank-Specific Meta Differences

The tier list above reflects all-ranks data, but the meta shifts dramatically between Bronze and Grandmaster. Here is what changes at different skill levels:

RankMeta Characteristics
Bronze – GoldSelf-sufficient heroes dominate. Peni Parker and Devil Dinosaur thrive because they generate value without teammate coordination. Rocket Raccoon's resurrection beacon carries teams through mistakes.
Platinum – DiamondTeam compositions start to matter. Dive comps with Magik and Daredevil become more effective as players coordinate engages. Mantis's damage boost enables one-shot combos.
Grandmaster+Hero pools shrink to 10-15 picks. Peni Parker, Magik, Rocket Raccoon, and Ultron are near-mandatory. Daredevil requires perfect execution. Counter-picking and bans define the draft phase.

VI. Key Takeaways for Ranked Climbing

  • Learn Peni Parker first. At 55.9% win rate and 25% pick rate, she is the single most impactful hero across all ranks. Her mine network generates value passively without teammate coordination.
  • Magik rewards mastery. A 55.4% win rate makes her the best Duelist investment. Her portal mechanic and CC chains are nearly impossible to counter when played correctly.
  • Rocket Raccoon is the support king. 54.6% win rate, 34% pick rate, and a resurrection beacon that wins fights. If you main Strategist, start here.
  • Devil Dinosaur is everywhere. At 47% pick rate, he is the most contested hero in the game. Learn to play him — and learn to play against him.
  • The gap between S/A-Tier and the rest is wide. With a roster of 50+ heroes, only about 13 consistently deliver above-50% win rates. Pool wisely.
