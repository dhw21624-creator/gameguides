II. Role-Specific Tier Lists

Vanguard (Tanks) — S-Tier: Groot, Magneto, Doctor Strange, Invisible Woman

Groot is the premier pick for brawl/sustain with his Season 7 Unstoppable buff. Magneto excels at initiation and peeling. Invisible Woman flexes between frontline and backline support.

Duelist (DPS) — S-Tier: Gambit, Hela, Magik, Daredevil, Phoenix

Gambit is the #1 Duelist with poke, area denial, and a team-wide ultimate. Phoenix offers self-sufficiency with healing, flight, and a devastating anti-shield ultimate. Elsa Bloodstone counters stealth and tank-heavy comps.

Strategist (Support) — S-Tier: Luna Snow, Cloak & Dagger, Invisible Woman, Rocket, Mantis, White Fox

Luna Snow is the top healer/damage booster. White Fox is a newer S-tier Strategist who counters dive heroes with movement manipulation. Cloak & Dagger provide clutch healing and invisibility.

IV. Counter-Pick Guide

Against Dive comps: pick White Fox, Mantis, Groot, Magneto. Against Poke/Brawl: triple-Strategist with Luna Snow and Gambit. Fill missing roles: need heals? Luna Snow or Invisible Woman. Need frontline? Groot or Magneto.
