1. Current Competitive Map Pool

As of June 2026 (Season V26, Act 3, Patch 12.08), the ranked pool consists of seven maps: Haven, Ascent, Split, Pearl, Breeze, Lotus, and Fracture. The most recent rotation brought Ascent back in and rotated Bind out. Maps such as Bind, Sunset, Icebox, Corrode, and Abyss are currently disabled in ranked but playable in Unrated and other modes.

2. Why Composition Beats Individual Picks

No single agent wins a map. What wins maps is covering four jobs across your five players:

  • Controller — Block sightlines with smokes. Most critical on open maps (Ascent, Pearl, Breeze).
  • Initiator — Clear angles and gather info. Essential on layered maps (Split, Lotus, Fracture).
  • Sentinel — Lock flanks, anchor sites. Best on multi-entry maps (Haven, Fracture, Breeze).
  • Duelist — Take space, entry frag. 1 on cramped maps, up to 2 on open maps.

The best agent on any map is the one filling the job your team is missing. Recommendations below are starting points — build and validate a full team around them.
