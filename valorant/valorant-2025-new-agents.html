1. New Agent Tejo (Initiator) — Complete Breakdown

Released on January 7, 2025 alongside Episode 5 Act 1, Tejo is an Initiator designed to dismantle enemy defensive setups through reconnaissance and high-impact area denial.

Best Maps for Tejo

  • Split: Narrow corridors make Guided Salvo extremely effective for clearing angles. Stealth Drone can safely check mid and B heaven.
  • Ascent: Armageddon covers both A and B site entry points. Use Stealth Drone to clear catwalk and market.
  • Bind: Portal-heavy map favors Tejo's ability to gather info and reposition quickly.

Tejo excels at "formation-breaking" — using reconnaissance to identify enemy positions, then suppressing them with AoE abilities. He is not a solo-entry fragger; coordinate with your Duelist for maximum impact.

3. Predicted New Sentinel Agent — 2026 Meta Shift

While not yet officially confirmed, leaks and community analysis point toward a new Sentinel agent designed around "sustained surveillance + punishment" mechanics rather than one-time burst damage.

Expected Impact on Meta

  • Double Sentinel Comps: The new agent's deployable surveillance devices could pair with Cypher or Killjoy to create near-impenetrable defensive setups. Expect "Double Sentinel" or "Sentinel + Info" compositions to rise in pro play.
  • Slower Pace: Teams may shift toward a "gather info → rotate → re-probe" rhythm, reducing the dominance of rush-heavy compositions.
  • Counterplay: Sova and Fade will become even more valuable for revealing and destroying deployed devices before executing site takes.

FAQ

Q: When did Tejo release in Valorant?
Tejo was released on January 7, 2025, with Episode 5 Act 1.
Q: Is Tejo good for solo queue?
Tejo works best with coordinated teams. In solo queue, his Stealth Drone provides valuable self-sufficient info, but his full potential requires team communication.
Q: Phantom or Vandal in 2026?
Phantom dominates close-range (0-20m) with faster fire rate and no tracers. Vandal excels at 30m+ with one-tap headshots. Map choice matters: Phantom on Split/Bind, Vandal on Breeze/Icebox.
Q: What's the best way to practice recoil control?
Use the Practice Range's shooting test with infinite ammo enabled. Spend 15 minutes daily on Phantom 2-burst and Vandal 3-burst rhythms.
