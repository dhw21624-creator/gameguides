ROAD TO OLYMPUS — EVERY NOCTURNAL ARM RANKED

Hades 2 Weapon Tier List 2026: Best Weapons Ranked & Boon Synergies

Supergiant's Hades 2 has been in Early Access for over a year now, and the weapon meta has crystallized after multiple balance patches. Five Nocturnal Arms — the Witch's Staff, Sister Blades, Moonstone Axe, Argent Skull, and Umbral Flames — each fill distinct combat niches, but not all are created equal. This tier list ranks every weapon by three criteria: boss-killing power, room-clearing speed, and overall consistency across the full run.

One critical note before we dive in: Hades 2's boon system means any weapon can become the best weapon with the right god combination. The tier list reflects average performance with typical boon luck. A weapon in B-Tier can outpace S-Tier if you roll the perfect synergy early. Always adapt to what the run gives you.

S-Tier Weapons — The Best of the Best

Witch's Staff — The Best All-Rounder

Versatile range, forgiving learning curve, and devastating Omega attacks. The weapon you can always rely on.

The Witch's Staff occupies a perfect sweet spot between melee and ranged combat. Its normal attack chain covers a wide arc — handling crowds without repositioning — while its Omega special delivers a chargeable ranged blast that deals heavy damage and benefits enormously from cast-boosting boons. The Staff is the only weapon in Hades 2 that provides comfortable answers to every enemy type in the game.

The Staff's real strength emerges with boon synergy. Aphrodite's Heartthrob boon on the Omega special creates one of the strongest interactions in the entire game: applying Weakness to everything caught in the blast radius, which dramatically reduces incoming damage while you chain into follow-up attacks. Pair Aphrodite with Hephaestus for bonus Omega damage, and the Staff transforms into a room-clearing machine. No other weapon benefits from as many god combinations — nearly every Olympian's boons synergize meaningfully with the Staff's hybrid kit.

For beginners: the Staff has the most forgiving learning curve. Its range keeps you safe from melee enemies, its attack speed is moderate enough to dodge-cancel comfortably, and its Omega attacks give you burst options for boss encounters. If you are unsure what to pick when starting a run, pick the Staff.

Best Boon Combos:
  • Aphrodite (Heartthrob) + Hephaestus — Weakness + Omega damage multiplication for devastating room-clearing blasts
  • Zeus (chain lightning) + Hera — Arc-wide coverage with multiple bolt sources stacking across crowds
  • Demeter (freeze) + Poseidon — Keep enemies locked down while Omega charging safely

Sister Blades — Maximum Damage Per Second

The fastest weapon in Hades 2. Boon proc frequency is multiplicative — and attack speed is king.

The Sister Blades are the highest DPS weapon in Hades 2, bar none. Their rapid attack chain procs boon effects more frequently than any other weapon, and in a game where boon triggers are multiplicative rather than additive, raw attack speed is the single most valuable stat. The Blades' 6-8 hit combo sequence means every boon that triggers on-hit activates multiple times per second, creating a cascade of bonus damage that spikes into absurd territory with the right synergy.

Artemis boons are the Blades' best friend. Critical hit chance on a weapon that hits 6-8 times per combo translates to consistent burst damage that melts bosses faster than any other setup. The downside: the Blades force you into melee range, and several bosses in Hades 2 — particularly the final encounter — punish close-range positioning hard. The Blades' special attack compensates by throwing them at range, giving you a poke tool for bosses with dangerous melee patterns. It is not as strong as the Staff's ranged options, but it keeps you in the fight when closing distance would get you killed.

Best Boon Combos:
  • Artemis (Deadly Strike) + Hermes (Attack Speed) — The classic crit-speed multiplicative loop; kills bosses before they can cycle through their movesets
  • Dionysus (Hangover stacks) + Aphrodite — DoT stacking across rapid hits, melting health bars passively while you reposition
  • Ares (Doom) + Athena — Doom triggers multiple times per combo with Deflect protection during recovery frames

A-Tier Weapons — Strong, Specialized Performance

Moonstone Axe — The Boss Killer

Slow, heavy, devastating. A single charged Omega attack can erase 20% of a boss health bar.

The Moonstone Axe has the highest per-hit damage of any weapon. One fully charged Omega attack can delete 20% of a boss health bar in a single swing, making it the best choice for boss encounters where you get narrow damage windows between attack patterns. The tradeoff is clear: the Axe is sluggish against groups, and its recovery frames leave you dangerously vulnerable if you commit to a swing inside a crowd.

Demeter boons work exceptionally well with the Axe. Freeze effects give you the crowd control needed to safely charge heavy attacks, and her damage bonuses scale multiplicatively with the Axe's already massive base numbers. The Apollo recovery boon provides the sustain that the Axe lacks — healing between combos offsets the vulnerability windows inherent in the weapon's design. With Demeter freeze + Apollo sustain, the Axe becomes a methodical, unstoppable force that trivializes bosses while requiring patience through regular rooms.

Best Boon Combos:
  • Demeter (Frost Flourish) + Apollo (healing) — Freeze crowd control enables safe charge windows; Apollo heals through chip damage between swings
  • Aphrodite (Weakness) + Athena (Deflect) — Damage reduction + Deflect stacking creates defensive uptime during long recovery animations
  • Hephaestus (heavy strike bonus) + Poseidon — Massive single-hit burst with knockback for breathing room after each swing

Argent Skull — Ranged Safety Net

Explosive projectiles from across the room. Safe, consistent, and punishing against predictable bosses.

The Argent Skull is Hades 2's dedicated ranged option, throwing explosive projectiles that deal AoE damage from a distance. It is the safest weapon against bosses with dangerous melee zones, and it makes several encounters trivially easy by letting you damage them from across the room while staying completely out of harm's way. The cost is lower sustained DPS — the Skull's projectiles have travel time and can miss mobile enemies, and its close-range options are weak. You are trading damage ceiling for consistency and safety.

The Skull's true potential unlocks with Poseidon's knockback boons, which create a stunlock loop against most bosses by interrupting their attack animations with every projectile impact. When combined with additional cast speed boons from Hermes, the Skull becomes a ranged lockdown machine that prevents enemies from ever reaching you.

Best Boon Combos:
  • Poseidon (Tempest Strike/Typhoon's Fury) — Knockback creates stunlock cycles; bosses lose the ability to attack in sequence
  • Zeus (chain lightning) + Hermes — Projectiles chain to nearby enemies, clearing groups while maintaining safe distance
  • Demeter (chill on hit) — Slowed enemies become even easier to hit at range, creating a positive feedback loop

B-Tier — Specialized, Niche-Dependent

Umbral Flames — AoE King, Boss Weakling

Best room-clearing weapon in the game. Falls off hard against single-target bosses.

The Umbral Flames specialize in area damage. Their attack pattern creates lingering damage zones that stack with boon effects, making them possibly the strongest weapon for clearing regular rooms — no other weapon clears screen-wide packs as effortlessly. Dionysus poison boons and Zeus chain lightning are the premier pairings: poison DoT zones combined with chain lightning that jumps between every enemy standing inside the flame patches. Against standard rooms, this combination clears everything without requiring any thought.

The problem is single-target damage. Against the final boss — or any boss with a large health pool — the Flames' spread damage falters compared to the concentrated output of the Staff or Blades. The Dionysus + Aphrodite stacking combo helps: poison and Weakness layered together create damage-over-time zones that passively melt health bars while you dodge. But the ceiling is simply lower than S-Tier options.

Best Boon Combos:
  • Dionysus (Hangover) + Zeus (chain lightning) — DoT zones with chain lightning; fastest room-clear setup in the game
  • Dionysus + Aphrodite — Stacking damage-over-time that passively melts everything, including bosses (albeit slowly)
  • Ares (Doom) + Demeter (chill) — Doom triggers inside frozen damage zones for double-dipping area damage
