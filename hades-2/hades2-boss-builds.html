II. Boss Strategies & Recommended Builds

Hecate (First Major Boss)

Dodge through Pulsing Rifts at the last second. When cursed, dodge towards the curse. Recommended: Witch's Staff (Circe/Anubis) or Moonstone Axe (Charon/Nergal).

Scylla & The Sirens

Prioritize the stationary Drummer Siren. Use AoE to hit both Scylla and Sirens. Recommended: Witch's Staff (Circe) or Moonstone Axe (Charon) with Omega Geyser.

Cerberus

Stay mobile, avoid red eruption zones. Dodge diagonally after ground slams. Recommended: Witch's Staff (Circe) for mid-range or Umbral Flames (Moros) for AoE pressure.

Chronos (Final Boss)

Pure DPS and patience check. Avoid Temporal Stop circles. Destroy summoned Flags to remove invulnerability. Recommended: Moonstone Axe (Charon) — safest and most consistent with Omega Geysers.

Polyphemus

Stay mid-range. When he yells "Where's My Sheep?", get away immediately (instant-kill grab). Destroy all six pylons before the fight. Recommended: Witch's Staff (Circe) or Moonstone Axe (Charon/Nergal).

III. How to Unlock & Upgrade Aspects

Unlock all 6 Nocturnal Arms → meet Hecate → brew Aspects of Night and Darkness (5 Bronze + 1 Nightshade). Upgrade at Training Grounds: Ranks 1-3 use common reagents; Ranks 4-5 require Nightmares from high-end content.
