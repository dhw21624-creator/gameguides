I. Current State of Destiny 2 (2026)

The game has transitioned from seasonal content to Episodes—larger, more narrative-driven content drops that last 4-6 months each. The current Episode, "Heresy," takes Guardians into the Dreadnaught to confront lingering echoes of the Hive god Oryx.

III. Prismatic Subclass: Build Guide

Prismatic combines abilities from all subclasses. The meta build for all classes revolves around Transcendence uptime:

  • Hunter: Combination Blow + Stylish Executioner. Invisible after every melee kill, infinite ability loop.
  • Warlock: Bleak Watcher + Devour. Freeze the battlefield while healing on every kill.
  • Titan: Consecration + Knockout. Slam-spam for massive AoE damage and constant healing.
