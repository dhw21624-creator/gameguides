Stardew Farmer's Almanac — Version 1.6.9

Stardew Valley 1.6.9: New Features & Money-Making

Stardew Valley 1.6.9 continues the steady stream of quality-of-life improvements and new content that began with the massive 1.6 update. This patch introduces a multi-currency display, a handy cookbook, and a range of smaller fixes and additions. This guide breaks down everything new in 1.6.9 and shares the best money-making strategies to take advantage of the update.

I. Multi-Currency Display

One of the most welcome quality-of-life changes in 1.6.9 is the multi-currency display. Previously, players had to track gold, Qi Gems, and other currencies separately. Now the game shows your balances for multiple currencies at once, making it much easier to plan purchases and track your wealth at a glance.

  • Gold: Your primary currency for most purchases.
  • Qi Gems: Earned from Mr. Qi's challenges and special orders.
  • Other currencies: Displayed as relevant, such as casino tokens and special event currencies.

II. The Cookbook

The new cookbook is a fantastic addition for cooking enthusiasts. It tracks all the recipes you have learned and provides a clear reference for ingredients and effects. This makes it much easier to plan meals for buffs, gifts, and quests.

  • Recipe tracking: See every recipe you have unlocked at a glance.
  • Ingredient lists: Check what you need for each dish.
  • Buff information: Review the effects of each meal before cooking.

III. Other New Features in 1.6.9

  • Legendary fish in fish ponds: You can now place legendary fish in fish ponds, opening up new farming opportunities.
  • Lost items recovery: The Secret Woods now offers a way to recover lost items, reducing the sting of losing gear.
  • Balance tweaks: Adjustments to crop values, artisan goods, and other systems based on community feedback.
  • Bug fixes: A long list of fixes across farming, fishing, and multiplayer.

IV. Best Money-Making Strategies in 1.6.9

With the new features in mind, here are the most effective ways to build your fortune in the current patch:

1. Artisan Goods Remain King

Processed goods still offer the best return on investment. Turn your crops into wine, preserves, and cheese. Starfruit wine and ancient fruit wine remain top earners, especially when aged in casks.

2. Legendary Fish Ponds

The new ability to place legendary fish in fish ponds is a game-changer. Legendary fish produce high-value roe that can be processed into aged roe for significant profit. This is one of the best new money-making methods in 1.6.9.

3. Greenhouse & Ginger Island

Maximize your greenhouse and Ginger Island farm with high-value crops. Ancient fruit, starfruit, and sweet gem berries are the classic choices. Use the island's year-round growing season to keep production running.

4. Truffle Empire

Pigs remain one of the most profitable animals. With the Botanist profession, truffles are always iridium quality, and turning them into truffle oil adds even more value.

V. Tips for the New Patch

  • Check the cookbook regularly: It is the fastest way to plan buff meals for mining and fishing days.
  • Invest in fish ponds early: Legendary fish ponds pay off over time, so start them as soon as you catch the fish.
  • Use the multi-currency display: Keep an eye on your Qi Gems and plan your Mr. Qi purchases.
  • Don't forget the Secret Woods: The lost items recovery feature is a safety net worth knowing about.
Final Tip: 1.6.9 is a quality-of-life patch that makes the game more comfortable to play, but the legendary fish pond addition is a genuine new money-making opportunity. Catch those legendary fish, build your ponds, and watch the gold roll in.
