Stardew Valley 1.6: The Complete Update Almanac

Version 1.6 is the largest content update Stardew Valley has ever received. Whether you're returning to Pelican Town or starting a fresh farm, this almanac covers everything new—from the Mastery Cave to the Desert Festival and beyond.

I. New Farm Types

Two new farm maps join the roster, each with unique terrain and gameplay advantages:

Forest Farm

A sprawling wooded property with forageable items, unique hardwood stumps that respawn, and special logs that can be cleared for rare items. Perfect for players who want easy access to foraging experience and hardwood without leaving home.

Riverland Farm

A chain of small islands connected by bridges. Fishing is accessible from your doorstep, and some islands feature special layouts that unlock new gameplay opportunities. Ideal for fishing-focused playthroughs.

II. The Mastery System

After maxing all five skills (Farming, Mining, Fishing, Combat, Foraging), the Mastery Cave appears on your farm. Inside, five statues grant ultimate abilities:

III. The Bookseller

A traveling merchant appears twice per season near the JojaMart (or Movie Theater). Books provide permanent bonuses—they're not consumable, they're upgrades.

  • The Book of Stars: Grants massive experience in all skills at once.
  • The Art o' Crabbing: Crab pots gain 25% chance for double items.
  • Additional volumes for each skill tree—check every visit.

V. New Machines & Quality of Life

  • Dehydrator: Turn 5 fruits into dried fruit or 5 mushrooms into dried mushrooms.
  • Fish Smoker: Doubles the value of any fish processed through it.
  • Pet Hats: Yes, you can now place hats on your dog or cat.
  • Second Pet: After maxing friendship with your first pet, adopt another.
  • Winter Outfits: Every villager now has unique winter clothing.

VI. 1.6 Quick-Start Checklist

  1. Choose Forest Farm (foraging) or Riverland Farm (fishing)
  2. Repair the bus to access the Desert Festival
  3. Max out one skill ASAP to unlock the Mastery Cave
  4. Check the Bookseller twice per season
  5. Craft a Dehydrator and Fish Smoker for profit
  6. Visit the Giant Stump in Cindersap Forest after a storm

Happy farming, and may Yoba bless your harvest!
