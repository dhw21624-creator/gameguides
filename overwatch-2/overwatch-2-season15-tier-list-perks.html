OVERWATCH FIELD MANUAL v2.15 — HERO EVALUATION REPORT

Overwatch 2 Season 15 Hero Tier List & Perks Guide (June 2026)

Season 15 has reshaped the competitive landscape with the introduction of the Perks system—a game-changing mechanic that grants every hero Minor and Major passive bonuses as matches progress. This new layer of strategy has catapulted unexpected heroes into S-tier, while forcing former staples to adapt or fall behind. This guide breaks down the complete Season 15 tier list across all three roles and explains the Perks that define the current meta.

How the Perks System Works

Introduced in Season 15, the Perks system is a mid-match progression mechanic resembling a simplified talent tree. Every hero receives access to two Minor Perks and two Major Perks per match, unlocked as you accumulate performance score. Minor Perks provide subtle stat boosts or quality-of-life improvements, while Major Perks fundamentally alter how an ability functions—creating new synergies and playstyle pivots that can swing the outcome of a match. The system has revitalized role strategy by rewarding adaptive decision-making: you can now tailor your hero to counter the enemy composition on the fly.

I. DPS Tier List

The Perks system has revitalized the DPS role more than any other, propelling previously niche heroes like Bastion and Torbjörn to the top of the charts. Torbjörn currently boasts a staggering 55.65% win rate across all ranks—his highest in the game's history—thanks to Major Perks that dramatically improve turret uptime and armor distribution. Bastion benefits from Perks that reduce his Configuration: Sentry cooldown and grant damage resistance during tank form, making him a terrifying frontline presence.

S-Tier DPS: The Perk Revolution

Bastion — Perks reduce Sentry cooldown by 20% and add ironclad-style damage resistance while transformed. Combined with a pocket healer, he melts shields and tanks alike.
Echo — Perks extend Flight duration and grant bonus ult charge during Duplicate, letting her clone and pop off faster than ever.
Sojourn — Railgun energy retention Perks let her hold fully charged shots between fights. Coupled with her Disruptor Shot slowdown, she punishes any positioning error.
Torbjörn — Turret durability Perks and Overload cooldown reduction make him a one-dwarf fortress. His 55.65% win rate is the highest among all DPS heroes this season.

II. Tank Tier List

Zarya continues her reign of terror with a 51.40% win rate across all ranks. Her new Major Perk, "Energy Lance," causes her Particle Cannon beam to pierce through enemies when her energy exceeds 50%—a condition she meets effortlessly when opponents mindlessly fire at her bubbles. Sigma, despite a modest ~1.30% pick rate, remains the thinking player's tank. His "Levitation" Major Perk grants temporary flight during Gravitic Flux, finally addressing his historical mobility weakness.

Zarya — Major Perk: Energy Lance

When Zarya's energy exceeds 50%, her primary fire beam pierces through all enemies in a line. This transforms her from a single-target brawler into a team-wiping menace. In Graviton Surge combos, Energy Lance melts clustered enemies before they can react.

Sigma — Major Perk: Levitation

During Gravitic Flux, Sigma can now ascend and descend freely. This lets him lift enemies, reposition mid-air to dodge counter-fire, and slam them onto high ground or into environmental hazards—turning his ultimate from a simple stun into a tactical repositioning tool.

III. Support Tier List

Ana remains the undisputed queen of Support with an 8.55% pick rate across all ranks—the highest among all heroes in the role. Her Biotic Grenade alone can swing entire teamfights by denying healing and amplifying her team's sustain. The new Biotic Bounce Minor Perk lets her grenade bounce once, adding subtle but meaningful utility. Kiriko follows with a 4.06% pick rate, and her Perks now allow Kunai damage to provide passive healing to nearby allies—rewarding players who can balance aggression and support seamlessly.

Ana — Minor Perk: Biotic Bounce

Biotic Grenade now bounces once upon hitting a surface, extending its effective range and enabling tricky bank shots around corners. Skilled Ana players use this to anti-heal enemies hiding behind shields or to hit allies just out of line-of-sight.

Kiriko — Major Perk: Kunai Restoration

Landing critical Kunai hits now emits a small healing pulse to nearby allies. This rewards Kiriko players who weave damage between their Ofuda heals, effectively letting them function as a DPS-Support hybrid without sacrificing team sustain.

V. Tips for Choosing the Right Hero

While tier lists provide a solid framework, success in Overwatch 2 ultimately depends on team composition and map selection. Here are three rules for Season 15:

  • Prioritize Perk synergy over raw stats. A hero whose Perks complement your team's playstyle (e.g., Zarya Energy Lance with a Gravitic Flux combo) will outperform a statistically stronger hero with no synergy.
  • Counter-pick with Perks in mind. If the enemy runs double barrier, Ana's Biotic Bounce can anti-heal from unexpected angles. If they run dive, Sigma's Levitation lets him escape and reposition.
  • Don't sleep on B-tier. Heroes like Reinhardt and Mei may be B-tier overall, but on maps like King's Row or Lijiang Tower, their Perks can flip the script entirely.

"Adapt or fall. Season 15 rewards the flexible and punishes the stubborn. Choose your Perks wisely."
