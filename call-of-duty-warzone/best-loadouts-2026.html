1. Top 5 Meta Loadouts

These are the five strongest weapon combinations in Warzone right now. If you're not sure what to run, start here. Each loadout has been tested and verified across thousands of ranked matches.

#1 XM4 + Jackal PDW — The Default Meta

AR SMG

The XM4 handles everything at range while the Jackal PDW dominates close quarters. This combination has virtually no weakness — reliable at every distance, competitive TTK across the board, and easy enough for any skill level.

WeaponAttachmentsRole
XM4Suppressor, 11.5" Reinforced Barrel, Microflex LED, Commando Foregrip, 30 Round Fast MagMid-to-long range AR; handles everything 15m+
Jackal PDWCompensator, 5.5" Reinforced Barrel, Foregrip Assault, 25 Round Fast Mag, Stippled Grip TapeClose quarters; ~220ms TTK, 0-15m dominant

Perks: Lightweight, Scavenger, Paranoia

#2 GPR 91 + Jackal PDW — The Long-Range Specialist

AR SMG

Swap the XM4 for the GPR 91 if you prioritize ranged engagements. The GPR has better first-range TTK and slightly superior damage at distance. The Jackal PDW covers close range as always.

WeaponAttachmentsRole
GPR 91Kepler Microflex, Reinforced Barrel, Commando Grip, Extended Mag I, SuppressorLong-range AR; exceptional damage out to 50m
Jackal PDWSame as aboveClose quarters SMG

Perks: EOD, Overkill, Amped

#3 LR 7.62 + PP-919 — The Sniper Combo

SNIPER SMG

The LR 7.62 one-shots to the head, chest, and arms — the most generous one-shot zones of any sniper in the game. The PP-919 has the best damage range of any SMG, perfectly bridging the gap when targets are too close for your sniper. Devastating for players with good aim.

WeaponAttachmentsRole
LR 7.62Monolithic Suppressor, 28.9" Barrel, Tac Laser, FTAC Sport Comb, Stippled Grip TapeOne-shot sniper; head/chest/arm kills
PP-919Monolithic Suppressor, 10.6" Task Force, Spetsnaz Grip, 50 Round Drum, Serpent WrapBest SMG damage range; bridges sniper gap

Perks: Cold-Blooded, Overkill, Amped

#4 RAM-7 + HRM-9 — The MWIII Classic

AR SMG

Still competitive. The RAM-7 is one of the most consistent ARs ever made, and the HRM-9 has an excellent close-range TTK. A reliable, time-tested combination that will not let you down.

WeaponAttachmentsRole
RAM-7Monolithic Suppressor, FSS Ranger Barrel, Commando Foregrip, 50 Round Mags, VLK 3.0x OpticVersatile AR; consistent across all ranges
HRM-9Compensator, 9.3" CQB Barrel, Foregrip Assault, 30 Round Mag, Stippled GripAggressive SMG; excellent close TTK

Perks: Flak Jacket, Scavenger, Ghost

#5 Pulemyot 762 + Kompakt 92 — The Anchor Setup

LMG SMG

For players who love holding positions and beaming at range. The Pulemyot 762 is the ultimate low-recoil long-range weapon, while the Kompakt 92 provides reliable close-range backup without any major weaknesses.

WeaponAttachmentsRole
Pulemyot 762Monolithic Suppressor, 26.9" Barrel, Commando Foregrip, 150 Round Belt, VLK 3.0x OpticLowest recoil long-range beam; massive magazine
Kompakt 92Compensator, 7.2" Reinforced Barrel, Foregrip Assault, 40 Round Mag, Ergonomic GripReliable close-range backup SMG

Perks: Double Time, Restock, Battle Hardened

  • Aggressive Rusher? Loadout #1 (XM4 + Jackal PDW) — best for pushing, objective modes, small maps
