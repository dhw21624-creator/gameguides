About GameGuides

GameGuides is an independent game guide website focused on practical, easy-to-read guides, checklists, and tools for popular games. We believe game guides should be clear, accurate, and free of unnecessary filler—just the information you need, when you need it.

What We Focus On

  • Beginner-friendly guides — Clear explanations that assume no prior knowledge, helping new players get started quickly.
  • Quick answers — Structured content with headings, tables, and bullet points so you can scan and find what you need without reading walls of text.
  • Updated game information — We note which game version or patch each guide was written for, and revisit content after major updates.
  • Simple calculators and tools — Practical utilities like pity calculators, crafting lookups, and build planners to support your gameplay.
  • Clear explanations without unnecessary filler — Every sentence should earn its place. We avoid padding guides with obvious tips or repeated information.

Editorial Standards

We hold ourselves to a few simple standards to keep our content trustworthy:

  • We avoid publishing unverified facts. When information comes from community testing, early datamines, or unofficial sources, we label it clearly.
  • When we cannot confirm a detail, we mark it as uncertain rather than presenting it as fact.
  • We update core guides after major patches that significantly change gameplay—balance reworks, new mechanics, system overhauls.
  • We prioritize practical, scannable structure over lengthy prose. If a guide can be a checklist, it should be.
