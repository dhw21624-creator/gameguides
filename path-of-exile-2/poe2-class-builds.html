III. Recommended Endgame Builds

Pathfinder — Poison Bow / Explosive Arrow (SS)

Massive poison stacks with bows/crossbows. Tanky with flask sustain. Smooth mapping, great boss DPS. Low gear requirement to start.

Blood Mage — Fireball / Ember Fusillade (S)

Converts life cost into massive damage and regeneration. One of the highest DPS options. Very tanky due to life leech/overflow. Gear-intensive for optimization.

Deadeye — Lightning Arrow / Ice Shot (A)

Blasts packs with projectiles, using Tailwind and chain mechanics. King of mapping speed. Gear-dependent for defense.

Oracle — Mjolnir Storm Shield Lightning (A)

Procs lightning with Mjolnir + Resonating Shield for constant AoE. Insane clear speed, tanky with block/evasion. Complex setup.
