FIELD REPORT — Super Earth Intelligence Division

Helldivers 2: New Faction Intel & Elite Tactics (2026)

Attention, Helldiver. The galaxy has grown more dangerous. A mysterious fourth faction has emerged, and the Automatons and Terminids are evolving faster than High Command predicted. This field report contains everything you need to survive your next drop.

I. The New Faction: Illuminate Returns

Reports from the outer rim confirm it: the Illuminate have returned. These psionic aliens wield advanced energy shields, teleportation tech, and mind-control abilities that can turn your squad against itself. Unlike the horde-style Terminids or the ranged-focused Automatons, the Illuminate demand a completely new tactical approach.

Illuminate Unit Types

Observer: Floating scout drone that calls reinforcements when alerted. Eliminate on sight. Silent weapons preferred.
Adept: Shielded infantry with plasma rifles. Break the shield first with rapid-fire weapons, then finish with precision shots.
Obelisk: Heavy psionic walker that projects shield barriers for allies and fires tracking energy orbs. Highest priority target.

Against the Illuminate, energy weapons bypass their shields entirely. The Arc Thrower chains between clustered enemies and stuns Adepts long enough for your team to focus fire. Avoid explosive weapons—Illuminate shields absorb blast damage.

III. Difficulty Scaling & Mission Types

New mission types introduced in 2025-2026 include Data Retrieval (extract intel from Illuminate structures while defending against psionic waves) and Fortress Assault (breach heavily fortified Automaton strongholds with coordinated airstrikes). On Difficulty 7+, enemy patrol density increases by 40% and elite unit spawns double. Squad coordination is no longer optional.

IV. Team Roles & Communication

  • Scout: Light armor, jetpack, marksman rifle. Primary job: ping threats early and call in stratagems from safe positions.
  • Heavy Gunner: Medium/heavy armor, machine gun or autocannon. Holds the line while others complete objectives.
  • Demo Specialist: Grenade launcher, supply pack. Breaches structures and clears nests/fabricators.
  • Medic: Shield generator pack, stim pistol. Keeps the squad alive during prolonged engagements.

Remember: Managed Democracy is non-negotiable. Spread liberty. One planet at a time.
