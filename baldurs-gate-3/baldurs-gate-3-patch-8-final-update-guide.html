Baldur's Gate 3 Patch 8 Final Update Guide: Subclasses, Cross-Play & What to Play Now

Category: Update Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Baldur's Gate 3

Patch 8 is Baldur's Gate 3's final major update. It is the biggest patch the game ever received — 12 new subclasses, full cross-play across every platform, photo mode, and more than a thousand bug fixes — and it remains the definitive end-state of the game through the current hotfix maintenance line. This guide covers everything Patch 8 added, where the game stands after the 2026 hotfixes, and the best ways to play Baldur's Gate 3 now that the roster is complete.

Quick take: Patch 8's 12 new subclasses are the headline feature. Full cross-play unites PC, PS5, Xbox, and Mac, and official cross-platform mod support is now the game's longevity engine.

What Patch 8 Added: The Final Major Update

Shipped in April 2025, Patch 8 closes Baldur's Gate 3's post-launch roadmap with the features players asked for most:

  • 12 new subclasses — one for each class, drawn from the tabletop subclass options that players had been requesting since launch.
  • Full cross-play — PC, PS5, Xbox, and Mac players can share multiplayer sessions, with cross-save through a Larian account.
  • Photo mode — a dedicated camera tool for capturing your party and the game's best moments.
  • 1000+ bug fixes — a sweeping polish pass across combat, dialogue, multiplayer, and performance.

The Hotfix Maintenance Line: Where the Game Stands Now

Since Patch 8, Larian has kept the game alive with a maintenance line of hotfixes rather than new content. The two most recent milestones:

  • Hotfix #35 (November 20, 2025): improved overall performance and stability.
  • Hotfix #36 (March 26, 2026): fixed corrupted-save and performance crashes introduced by an earlier hotfix — the most important fix for anyone who hit loading-screen crashes.

That means the version you are playing today is the polished, final build: Patch 8's features plus two years of stability work. There is no planned major update after this line.

What to Play Now: The Final Meta and Replay Paths

With the roster complete, a full Patch 8 party that showcases the new subclasses looks like this:

  • Giants Barbarian (frontline) — massive damage and threat control at the front.
  • Swords Bard (support/control) — party-wide buffs and battlefield control.
  • Death Cleric (offensive support) — necrotic damage with reliable healing backup.
  • Bladesinger (arcane melee) — the new Wizard face that protects concentration in melee.

Beyond new builds, the best replay paths in 2026 are the classic ones, all still fully supported: a Dark Urge run for the most story-integrated evil playthrough, Honour Mode for the definitive challenge, and origin character runs to see the personal questlines from the inside. On top of that, official cross-platform mod support — including curated mods on consoles through mod.io — is now the game's longevity engine, so a fresh run with community content can feel like a brand-new game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Patch 8 really the final major update?

A: Yes. Larian has confirmed Patch 8 is the final major content update; the game now receives maintenance hotfixes only.

Q: Does cross-play work on every platform?

A: Yes. PC, PS5, Xbox, and Mac players can all join the same sessions, with cross-save via a Larian account.

Q: Which new subclass is strongest?

A: Hexblade Warlock is the strongest single addition and the best multiclass dip; Bladesinging Wizard is the strongest full-class build of the batch.

Q: I hit crashes after an earlier hotfix — what fixes them?

A: Hotfix #36 (March 26, 2026) specifically fixed corrupted-save and performance crashes; make sure your game is fully updated.

Conclusion

Baldur's Gate 3 ended its update journey exactly where it should: 12 subclasses deep, fully cross-play, and stable after years of hotfixes. Whether you build a Hexblade, replay as the Dark Urge, or load mods from mod.io, the final meta has something for everyone — and with official mod support still expanding, the definitive version of the game is only getting better.
