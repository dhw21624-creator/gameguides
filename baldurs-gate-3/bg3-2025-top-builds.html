2. Divination Haste Mage: Complete Build Guide

The Divination Haste Mage is arguably the strongest build in Baldur's Gate 3 for Honour Mode. By combining the Divination Wizard's Portent dice with the Haste spell, you can control the battlefield while dealing massive damage. This build requires precise spell selection and positioning but rewards you with unparalleled power.

Spell Selection Decision Tree

Cantrips (Level 1):
  • Fire Bolt (damage) or Ray of Frost (slow)
  • Mage Hand (utility)
  • Minor Illusion (distraction)
Level 1 Spells (Choose 4):
  • Magic Missile (guaranteed damage)
  • Shield (reaction defense)
  • Sleep (early game control)
  • Grease (area control)
Level 2 Spells (Choose 3):
  • Misty Step (positioning)
  • Scorching Ray (damage)
  • Hold Person (control)
Level 3 Spells (Choose 2):
  • Haste (core spell)
  • Fireball (AoE damage)
Level 4+ Spells:
  • Greater Invisibility (Level 4)
  • Cloudkill (Level 5)
  • Disintegrate (Level 6)

Feat Progression

  1. Level 4: War Caster (advantage on concentration saves)
  2. Level 8: Ability Score Improvement (INT +2 to 20)
  3. Level 12: Alert (+5 initiative, can't be surprised)

4. Honour Mode Strategy: Surviving the Impossible

Honour Mode introduces permadeath, limited saves, and enhanced boss mechanics. These strategies are essential for survival:

  • Pre-buffing: Cast Mage Armor, Longstrider, and Aid before any major encounter.
  • Positioning: Use high ground for +2 attack bonus. Place your mage where they can't be targeted by melee enemies.
  • Crowd Control: Use Grease, Web, and Hypnotic Pattern to control enemy numbers. Focus fire on one target at a time.
  • Resource Management: Don't blow all your spell slots in one fight. Keep at least one Level 3+ slot for emergencies.
  • Escape Plan: Always have Misty Step or Dimension Door prepared to escape unwinnable fights.

FAQ

Q: Is the Divination Haste Mage viable for Honour Mode?
Yes, it's one of the strongest Honour Mode builds. The Portent dice can force critical failures on enemy saves or guarantee your Haste concentration checks. Prioritize War Caster at Level 4 to maintain Haste.
Q: What's the best party composition for a first playthrough?
Lae'zel (Fighter), Shadowheart (Cleric), Gale (Wizard), and your custom character as a Rogue or Ranger. This covers all roles and provides good story interaction.
Q: How do I deal with the Githyanki patrol in Act 1?
Don't fight them at Level 3-4. Use dialogue options to avoid combat. If combat starts, focus the mage first, use Grease to control melee fighters, and have Shadowheart cast Bless on your party.
Q: What's the most important item for a Wizard?
The Staff of Spellpower (Act 2) gives +1 to spell attack rolls and DC, and allows you to cast a free Level 3 spell once per long rest. Pair it with the Cloak of Protection for +1 AC and saving throws.
