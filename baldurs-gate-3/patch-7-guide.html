Baldur’s Gate 3 Patch 7 Complete Guide

New Dark Urge Endings, Honor Mode Tips, Best Builds & Mod Support — Everything Added in Patch 7

What’s New in Patch 7

Patch 7 is one of the most substantial updates to Baldur’s Gate 3 since launch, delivering 13 new evil ending cinematics for the Dark Urge origin, official mod support on console and Mac via mod.io integration, expanded dialogue with over 1,500 new lines, and significant Honor Mode balance adjustments. Whether you’re a returning veteran planning a new playthrough or a newcomer diving into the Sword Coast for the first time, Patch 7 reshapes the endgame and adds layers of replayability.

Important: Patch 7 requires a fresh start for some content to trigger correctly — particularly the new Dark Urge endings and expanded companion reactivity in Act 3. If you’re loading a pre-Patch 7 save from Act 3, some new dialogue options may not appear. Larian recommends starting a new game for the full Patch 7 experience.

Patch 7 dramatically expands the Dark Urge origin’s ending repertoire, particularly for players who embrace Bhaal’s legacy. The new endings cover multiple branching outcomes based on your choices throughout Acts 2 and 3, companion relationships, and your final decision at the Morphic Pool.

2. Honor Mode Strategies — Surviving the Hardest Difficulty

Honor Mode remains BG3’s ultimate challenge: a single save file, legendary actions on all major bosses, and permadeath if your party wipes. Patch 7 did not nerf Honor Mode difficulty but added subtle balance tweaks: Raphael’s Soul Pillars now have slightly less HP, and the Orin duel has a tighter turn limit. Here are the strategies that will carry you to the golden dice.

Raphael Boss Fight — The Lethic Orb Strategy

The Setup

Raphael in the House of Hope is widely considered the hardest Honor Mode fight after the Netherbrain. His 666 HP, high AC, Soul Pillar regeneration, and devastating AOE spells can wipe an unprepared party in two rounds. The Lethic Orb + Globe of Invulnerability strategy neutralizes him almost entirely.

Step-by-Step Execution

  • Step 1 — Pre-buff: Before initiating dialogue, cast Heroes’ Feast (immunity to frightened), Death Ward on all party members, and apply Elixir of Fire Resistance (Raphael’s hellfire deals fire damage). Summon any elementals, cambions, or planar allies you have available.
  • Step 2 — Globe of Invulnerability: On Round 1, have your party’s caster (Gale or a Sorcerer) cast Globe of Invulnerability in the center of the arena. All four party members stay inside. Raphael’s hellfire, Ascended Fiend attacks, and Yurgir’s grenades cannot penetrate the globe — you are effectively immune to damage for 3 turns.
  • Step 3 — Destroy Soul Pillars: From inside the globe, use ranged attacks and spells to destroy all four Soul Pillars. Each pillar has ~100 HP. Eldritch Blast with Repelling Blast can knock cambions off the pillars. Once all four pillars are gone, Raphael loses his healing and his damage output drops by roughly 40%.
  • Step 4 — Lethic Orb: Once pillars are down and Globe expires, have your Cleric cast Hold Monster on Raphael (use Heightened Spell or stack DC-boosting gear to ensure it lands — Raphael has +9 WIS saves). With Hold Monster active, every melee hit is an automatic critical. A Fighter with Action Surge can deal 200+ damage in one round.
Backup Plan: If Hold Monster fails, use Otto’s Irresistible Dance as a fallback — Raphael gets no saving throw on the first round. Follow with a Portent Die (Divination Wizard) to guarantee a critical control spell lands. Bring at least 2 scrolls of Globe of Invulnerability in case the fight runs long.

General Honor Mode Survival Tips

  • Always have an escape plan: Keep one character (preferably a Rogue or Monk) with Dash + Cunning Action ready to flee combat if things go south. Invisibility potions on every character’s hotbar save runs.
  • Alert feat on everyone: Going first in Honor Mode is non-negotiable. +5 initiative ensures your party acts before legendary actions trigger. The difference between acting first and second is often the difference between a clean fight and a TPK.
  • Camp casting abuse: At camp, have hirelings cast long-rest buffs (Warding Bond, Protection from Poison, Freedom of Movement, Death Ward) on your active party, then swap them out. These buffs persist until long rest.
  • Avoid unnecessary fights: The Inquisitor in the Githyanki Crèche, Bernard in the Arcane Tower, and Ansur are Honor Mode run-enders. Skip them or return at level 12 with optimal gear. The golden dice are worth more than pride.

3. Best Patch 7 Builds — SorLock & Thrower Dominate the Meta

Build 1: The 10/1/1 SorLock (S-Tier Controller)

Why It’s Meta

The SorLock combines the Sorcerer’s Metamagic (Quickened Spell, Heightened Spell, Twinned Spell) with Warlock’s Eldritch Blast scaling and short-rest spell slots. At level 12, this build can fire 9 Eldritch Blast beams per turn (Quickened Spell + Action + Haste) each dealing 1d10+10 force damage with Agonizing Blast and Potent Robe, totaling 99–180 force damage per round before critical hits. With Hex active, add 9d6 necrotic damage.

Level Split

Sorcerer (Draconic — Fire/Gold)10 levels
Warlock (The Fiend / Great Old One)1 level
Fighter1 level (for CON save proficiency + Heavy Armor)

Key Equipment

  • Potent Robe: Adds CHA modifier to cantrip damage. At 22 CHA, that’s +6 per beam.
  • Spellmight Gloves: Take -5 to hit for +1d8 damage on spell attacks. Combined with advantage from Risky Ring, this is pure upside.
  • Birthright / Hood of the Weave: +2 CHA (cap at 24 with Mirror of Loss) or +2 spell DC.
  • Markoheshkir: Free cast of any spell once per short rest, plus Arcane Battery.
  • Amulet of Greater Health: Sets CON to 23, freeing up ASIs for CHA.
Gameplay Loop: Start combat with Quickened Spell → Hex, then Eldritch Blast (3 beams at level 10 Sorcerer). Next turn: Haste yourself with Twinned Spell if another party member needs it, then fire 6–9 beams per subsequent turn. Use Heightened Spell on Hypnotic Pattern or Confusion to lock down entire encounters while your martials clean up.

Build 2: Thrower Barbarian (S-Tier DPS)

Why It Still Dominates

The Tavern Brawler Thrower build remains untouched by Patch 7 balance changes and continues to be the highest sustained single-target DPS build in the game. At level 12, a properly geared Thrower Barbarian outputs 200–300+ damage per round with 4 throws per turn (Extra Attack + Action Surge + Haste), each dealing 30–40 bludgeoning + 3d4 thunder (Nyrulna) + lightning charges + rage bonus.

Level Split

Barbarian (Berserker)5 levels (Extra Attack, Frenzied Throw as bonus action)
Fighter3 levels (Action Surge, Champion for crit range)
Rogue (Thief)4 levels (Fast Hands: second bonus action = another Frenzied Throw)

Key Equipment

  • Nyrulna: Legendary trident from the Jungle in Act 3. Returns when thrown, deals 3d4 thunder AOE on impact. The undisputed best throwing weapon.
  • Ring of Flinging: +1d4 to thrown weapon damage.
  • Kushigo Gloves: +1d4 to thrown weapon damage.
  • Tavern Brawler feat: Add STR modifier twice to thrown attack rolls and damage. At 22–24 STR, that’s +12–14 to hit and damage per throw.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to start a new game for Patch 7 content?

Not strictly required, but strongly recommended. Existing saves from Act 3 may not trigger the new Dark Urge ending cinematics or expanded companion dialogue. If you load a pre-Patch 7 save, the new mod support and gameplay tweaks will still apply, but narrative content may be partially unavailable. For the full experience, start a fresh Dark Urge playthrough.

Q: What’s the hardest fight in Honor Mode after Patch 7?

The consensus remains Raphael as the #1 run-ender, followed closely by Ansur and the Inquisitor in the Githyanki Crèche. The Netherbrain finale is less dangerous with proper preparation (Globe of Invulnerability + summoned allies). Patch 7 slightly reduced Raphael’s Soul Pillar HP, which makes the fight marginally easier if you prioritize pillar destruction.

Q: Is the SorLock or Thrower build better for a first Honor Mode run?

The Thrower Barbarian is more forgiving for Honor Mode beginners. It comes online earlier (level 4 with Tavern Brawler), has high HP and damage resistance from rage, and doesn’t rely on saving throws landing. The SorLock requires more game knowledge (positioning, spell slot management) but offers superior crowd control in later acts. Run both in your party for the strongest Honor Mode composition.

Q: Can I get the golden dice on a modded save?

No. Any save that has mods enabled will be flagged and achievements & Honor Mode completion rewards (including the golden d20) are disabled. If you want the golden dice, you must play a completely unmodded Honor Mode run from start to finish. Larian has been strict about this policy.

Summary

Patch 7 is Larian’s love letter to the community — deeper Dark Urge storytelling, official mod support on all platforms, and Honor Mode refinements that make the game’s hardest challenge feel more rewarding. Whether you’re chasing the golden dice with a Thrower Barbarian + SorLock party, exploring the 13 new ending cinematics as Bhaal’s Chosen, or diving into console mods for the first time, there has never been a better moment to return to Faerûn. Roll for initiative — the Absolute won’t wait.
