Patch 8: The 12 New Subclasses — Complete Guide

Updated: June 2026 | Patch 8 (Final Major Update) | Reading time: 12 min

Patch 8 — released April 15, 2025 — marks the final major content update for Baldur's Gate 3. Larian Studios delivered 12 new subclasses (one per class), Cross-Play, Photo Mode, Xbox Series S split-screen, and a modding toolkit update. This guide ranks every subclass for Honour Mode viability and provides optimized builds for the strongest picks.

2. S-Tier Deep Dives

Bladesinger Wizard — "The Untouchable God"

The Bladesinger combines a Wizard's full spell list with a martial's Extra Attack at level 6. During Bladesong (proficiency bonus uses per long rest), you add your INT modifier to AC, gain +10 ft movement, and advantage on Acrobatics. At level 14, you add INT to melee damage. With Mage Armor, 20 DEX, and 20 INT, your AC reaches 23 before items — rivaling heavy armor tanks while maintaining full spellcasting.

Optimal Build: High Elf, 8 STR / 16 DEX / 14 CON / 17 INT / 10 WIS / 10 CHA. Feats: War Caster (4), INT +2 (8), Mobile (12). Key spells: Shield, Haste, Shadow Blade, Tenser's Transformation.

Death Domain Cleric — "The Reaper"

Death Domain's Reaper feature lets you target two adjacent enemies with necromancy cantrips. At level 6, your necrotic damage ignores resistance. Combine with Toll the Dead and Spirit Guardians for devastating AoE damage that pierces through typical undead defenses.

Optimal Build: Dwarf, 15 STR / 10 DEX / 17 CON / 8 INT / 16 WIS / 8 CHA. Heavy armor + shield. Feats: WIS +2 (4), Resilient CON (8), War Caster (12).

Swashbuckler Rogue — "The Solo Artist"

The Swashbuckler's Rakish Audacity lets you Sneak Attack without an ally within 5 ft — and you can disengage for free after attacking. At level 9, Panache lets you charm or taunt enemies. This subclass is the premier choice for solo Honour Mode runs.

Optimal Build: Half-Elf, 8 STR / 17 DEX / 14 CON / 8 INT / 10 WIS / 16 CHA. Rapier + shield. Feats: DEX +2 (4), Dual Wielder (8), Alert (12).

4. Which Subclass Should You Pick?

  • Solo Honour Mode: Swashbuckler or Bladesinger — self-sufficient, high AC, no reliance on allies.
  • 4-Player Party: Death Domain Cleric (damage) or Stars Druid (support/healing flexibility).
  • Maximum Damage: Giant Barbarian with Tavern Brawler feat and thrown weapons.
  • Fresh Experience: Arcane Archer Fighter — ranged superiority with magical arrow effects.
