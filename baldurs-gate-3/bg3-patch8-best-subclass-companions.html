Baldur's Gate 3 Patch 8: 12 New Subclasses & Companion Picks

Patch 8 is the final major content update for Baldur's Gate 3, and it delivers what fans have been asking for since launch: twelve brand-new subclasses, one for each class in the game. From the Hexblade Warlock to the Bladesinging Wizard, these subclasses dramatically expand build options. This guide breaks down all 12 new subclasses and recommends the best one for each companion.

II. The Standout Subclasses

Hexblade (Warlock)

The Hexblade is arguably the most anticipated subclass in Patch 8. It lets Warlocks fight in melee using their Charisma for both attacks and damage, making them single-ability-dependent (SAD) and incredibly powerful. The Hexblade's Curse adds bonus damage and crit range, while the ability to use Charisma for weapon attacks opens up powerful multiclass options with Paladin and Sorcerer. This is the strongest new subclass for both solo and companion builds.

Bladesinging (Wizard)

The Bladesinging Wizard combines martial combat with full spellcasting. Bladesong grants a bonus to AC, concentration saves, and movement speed, making the wizard surprisingly tanky in melee. It rewards a hybrid playstyle — weave cantrips and weapon attacks together — and is a fantastic option for players who want a gish character without sacrificing spell progression.

Circle of Stars (Druid)

The Circle of Stars Druid is a versatile caster with three star map forms: Archer (bonus ranged attack), Chalice (bonus healing), and Dragon (bonus to attack rolls and concentration). It is one of the most flexible new subclasses, able to fill damage, support, or control roles depending on the situation.

Swashbuckler (Rogue)

The Swashbuckler is a mobile duelist who can disengage for free after attacking, making it nearly impossible to pin down. It rewards aggressive, single-target play and pairs beautifully with the Rogue's Sneak Attack. A top pick for players who love hit-and-run tactics.

IV. Multiclass Synergies

The new subclasses open up powerful multiclass combinations:

  • Hexblade + Paladin: The classic "Padlock" — Charisma SAD melee with smites. One of the strongest builds in the game.
  • Hexblade + Sorcerer: "Sorlock" — quickened Eldritch Blast plus melee options.
  • Bladesinging + Fighter: Action Surge with bladesong for burst rounds.
  • Swashbuckler + Fighter: Extra Attack with free disengages for a mobile skirmisher.
Pro Tip: Patch 8 also includes the long-awaited crossplay and photo mode. When you respec a companion, remember that their starting subclass choice can be changed at Withers for a small fee — so experiment freely with the new options.

V. Final Thoughts

Patch 8 is a love letter to Baldur's Gate 3 players. The twelve new subclasses add enormous replay value, and the Hexblade alone is worth a fresh playthrough. Whether you are building a new Tav or respeccing your favorite companion, the options above will set you up for success in the final version of the game.
