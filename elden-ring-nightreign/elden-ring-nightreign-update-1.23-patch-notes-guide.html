Elden Ring Nightreign Update 1.23 Patch Notes & Current Version Guide

Category: Patch Notes • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Elden Ring Nightreign

If you booted up Elden Ring Nightreign after September 18, 2026 and saw a new version number, you are looking at Ver 1.23 (build 1.003.004). This is a small but important patch: it is a technical update focused on online security and network stability, which means it changes none of the balance, bosses, or content you care about for builds and runs. This guide explains exactly what Ver 1.23 contains, how it fits into the patch history that led here, and what the current endgame looks like after the Forsaken Hollows expansion.

Short version: Ver 1.23 / 1.003.004 is safe to install. It does not touch Nightfarer balance, Relic tuning, or Expedition rewards. Your existing save and build remain valid.

What Ver 1.23 (1.003.004) Actually Changes

Update 1.23 is classified by Bandai Namco as a technical patch. Its stated goals are online security hardening and network stability improvements. In practice that means:

  • Online security: additional checks and safeguards on the multiplayer session layer to close exploit vectors reported since the Forsaken Hollows launch.
  • Network stability: fixes for session drops and connection timeouts that could interrupt co-op Expeditions under heavy load.
  • No gameplay changes: there is no Nightfarer buff or nerf, no weapon or Relic adjustment, and no new content in this patch.

For most players the only visible difference is a faster, more stable matchmaking experience and a quieter lobby. If you were already clearing Nightreign runs smoothly, nothing about your strategy needs to change.

Patch History: How We Got Here

Ver 1.23 is the latest step in a longer patch line. Two earlier updates matter much more for how the game actually plays, and it helps to know which changes are already live before you plan a build.

Ver 1.03.5 Hotfix (March 31, 2026) — Multiplayer Performance

The March hotfix targeted one of Nightreign's most visible problems at the time: multiplayer Expedition performance. Players on Xbox were reporting crashes and stutter during co-op runs, especially on the busier Day 3 fights. Ver 1.03.5 addressed those Expedition-specific performance issues and made co-op sessions noticeably more stable. If you play with friends, this patch is the reason your runs stopped freezing mid-boss.

Ver 1.03.2 (January 15, 2026) — Nightfarer Rebalance

The January balance patch is the most significant gameplay patch since launch. It rebalanced a wide slice of the Nightfarer roster: Duchess, Guardian, Raider, Revenant, Executor, Scholar, and Undertaker all received adjustments, from straight buffs and nerfs to skill-specific tweaks. The practical result is a narrower power gap across the roster, so characters that felt weak in the base game are now genuinely competitive in both solo and co-op runs.

Current Content State: The Forsaken Hollows Recap

If you have not played since the base game, the current endgame is defined by The Forsaken Hollows DLC, which launched December 4, 2025. Everything added there is still live and unchanged by Ver 1.23. The expansion introduced two new Nightfarer classes — Scholar and Undertaker — each with a distinct skill kit built for the expansion's longer runs.

The DLC also added two new Nightlords: Harmonia and Dreglord, both of which can appear as the final boss of an Expedition. Their fights reward players who learn the new tells rather than players who simply stack damage, so re-read your Nightlord weaknesses before a long session.

On the map side, the Shifting Earth modifier randomises terrain and enemy placement between Expedition days, forcing route adjustments on the fly. The DLC also extended runs with an extra Expedition day, which gives you one more full loop of loot, upgrades, and risk before the Nightlord. Finally, the Murk currency earned during Forsaken Hollows runs is spent at the Small Jar Bazaar, where rotating stock includes Relic upgrades and exclusive consumables.

What To Do Now

Because Ver 1.23 is purely technical, your pre-patch plan is still the correct plan. A few practical reminders:

  1. Confirm your game shows Ver 1.23 / 1.003.004 on the title screen so you have the latest security fixes before playing online.
  2. If you have not tried Scholar or Undertaker since the January rebalance, revisit them — both received adjustments that matter at high difficulty.
  3. Use the extra Expedition day in Forsaken Hollows to over-invest in Relic colours before the Nightlord reveal.
  4. Spend Murk at the Small Jar Bazaar before long runs; stock rotates and the best upgrades sell out quickly in practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Did Update 1.23 nerf or buff any Nightfarer?

A: No. Ver 1.23 is a technical patch covering online security and network stability only. No balance or content changes were included.

Q: Do I need to change my build after 1.23?

A: No. Your existing build, Relic setup, and strategy remain fully valid.

Q: What was the last patch that actually changed gameplay?

A: Ver 1.03.2 (January 15, 2026) rebalanced seven Nightfarers. The Forsaken Hollows DLC added the most recent content back in December 2025.

Q: Will there be more balance patches?

A: Bandai Namco has not announced a timeline. The current patch line is maintenance-focused, but the developers have continued to ship balance passes through 2026.

Conclusion

Elden Ring Nightreign's current version, Ver 1.23 / 1.003.004, is a clean technical update that hardens online play without disrupting the meta. The builds and routes that worked before still work, and the Forsaken Hollows content remains the definitive endgame. Install the patch, jump into a co-op Expedition, and enjoy the stable sessions — the balance landscape you learned in January is the one you should still be playing.
