Field Journal — Surviving the Eternal Night

A Tarnished's Survival Manual: Nightreign

Welcome, weary traveler. You have been pulled into Limveld, a fractured realm where the sun never rises. Your mission: survive three in-game days and nights, grow stronger, and defeat the Nightlord that rules this cursed domain. This manual contains everything a fresh recruit needs to know—from choosing your warrior to conquering the final boss alone.

Each run follows a strict rhythm. Understanding this cycle is your first survival lesson:

Land in Limveld → Loot & Explore → Fight Mini-Bosses & Events → Return to Roundtable Hold (restock, upgrade flasks/weapons) → Push to Next Area → Repeat for 3 Cycles → Face the Nightlord

Efficient rune farming is the difference between victory and a swift death. The central castle area delivers more runes than all other zones combined, making it the optimal leveling ground from levels 1 through 9.

II. Map Breakdown & Elemental Zones

Every camp on the map bears an elemental icon—Fire, Ice, Lightning, and more. This icon tells you everything: what element your enemies wield, what loot drops, and what boss rewards await.

Day 1 Strategy: Rush safe zones and one medium zone. Grab 2-3 weapon upgrades before touching any hard area. The rain is your enemy—it applies rune penalties and cancels revive mechanics.

IV. Solo Survival Tactics

Fighting alone changes everything. The death penalty is brutal—you revive once after a boss kill, but dying to the storm or rain skips the revive and applies a permanent rune loss.

  • Farm the Castle: The central castle offers the best rune-per-minute ratio in the game. Go there every run.
  • Avoid Rain Fights: Unless facing the Nightlord, never engage in rain. It's not worth the penalty.
  • Boss Preparation: Always carry a spirit ashes-of-war and fully upgraded flasks. Use walls and cliffs to break line-of-sight and force 1v1 engagements.
  • Environmental Kills: Many arenas have traps and hazards. Lure enemies into them to save resources.

V. Secrets, Endings & Platinum Roadmap

True mastery requires defeating every Nightlord with every character and collecting all offerings from Roundtable Hold vendors. For trophy hunters on PlayStation, a version-switching exploit exists: play on one version to unlock items, switch to the other to duplicate them, then return to spend the duplicated currency.

The "Dawn" ending is the standard path—select the normal dialogue option after the final battle. Special endings require specific items: equip the Silver Tear or Law of Regression, or find the hidden room with a crying baby while playing as the Recluse.

Final Tip: The journey through darkness is a marathon, not a sprint. Learn each boss pattern, master one character before branching out, and never fight fair when the environment offers an advantage.
