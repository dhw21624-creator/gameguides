Elden Ring Nightreign: Best Builds for Every Nightfarer (2026)

After 100+ hours in Nightreign's roguelike format, these are the builds that consistently clear runs — whether you're playing solo or co-op. Unlike base Elden Ring, you're building from scratch each run, so the best setups must work with random relic drops, scale from early to late game, and synergize with teammates. This guide ranks and details the four strongest Nightfarer builds as of June 2026.

1. Wraith Summoner (S-Tier) — The Passive Powerhouse

Why It Dominates

The Wraith Calling Bell summons deal massive damage that scales with your relics. Combined with the Night of the Lord relic, you output damage while staying completely safe — the summons do the work while you focus on dodging. This is the easiest build for beginners and the most consistent for clearing runs regardless of relic RNG.

2. Faith Guardian (A-Tier) — The Stance-Break Tank

Why It's Great

Great Spears have exceptional reach and stance damage in Nightreign. With the Faith scaling relic, you can charge incantations during stance-break openings for massive burst damage. This build thrives as the team's anchor — drawing aggro, breaking boss stances, and creating DPS windows for your squad.

3. Bleed Assassin (A-Tier) — The Boss Melter

Why It Excels

Hemorrhage procs deal percentage-based damage, making this build exceptionally effective against high-HP bosses. Dual katanas or curved swords stack bleed incredibly fast. This is the build for aggressive players who want to delete bosses before mechanics become a problem — but it demands excellent dodge timing and positional awareness.

4. Comet Sorcerer (B-Tier) — The Glass Cannon

High Risk, High Reward

Comet Azur still deletes bosses when you have the FP to sustain it. The risk is higher in the roguelike format due to FP management — you can't guarantee finding FP regen relics early. This build drops to B-Tier because of its dependency on specific relic drops. When it works, it's devastating. When RNG doesn't cooperate, you're a glass cannon with no cannon.

Co-op Squad Composition — The Golden Trio

The ideal co-op squad follows the classic RPG trinity: 1 Tank + 1 DPS + 1 Support. Bosses have more health in co-op but aggro splits across players, making coordinated teams far more effective than solo play.

RoleRecommended BuildResponsibility
TankFaith GuardianDraw aggro, stance break, create openings
DPSBleed Assassin or Wraith SummonerMaximum damage output during openings
SupportHealer with AOE buffsDefensive talismans, team-wide buffs, emergency heals

Pro Tips for Consistent Run Clears

Survival Priority Checklist

  • Adapt to drops: Don't force a build if relics aren't supporting it — flexibility beats stubbornness
  • Prioritize survivability relics in the first two areas — defense scales better than offense early
  • Always grab Spirit Ashes when offered — they provide free damage and aggro splitting
  • Learn boss patterns before optimizing DPS — fundamentals beat build min-maxing every time
  • Builds are locked per run — you can't respec in Nightreign, so choose your Nightfarer wisely
