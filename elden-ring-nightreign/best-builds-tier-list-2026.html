Elden Ring Nightreign: Best Builds Tier List 2026 — S-Tier & A-Tier Rankings

After 100+ hours in Nightreign, these are the builds that consistently clear runs. Whether you're playing solo or co-op, this guide covers the strongest setups for each Nightfarer, with weapon and relic recommendations that actually work in the roguelike format. Unlike base Elden Ring, Nightreign's structure means you're building from scratch each run — the best builds work with random relic drops, scale well from early to late run, have flexible weapon options, and synergize with co-op teammates.

Understanding Nightreign's Roguelike Build System

Nightreign throws out Elden Ring's permanent character building. Each run is a fresh start. You pick one of 8 Nightfarers, each with unique abilities and stat spreads. Relics drop randomly from enemies and chests. The best builds don't force a specific weapon — they work with whatever the RNG provides.

Golden Rule: Adapt to drops. Don't force a build if relics aren't supporting it. Early game matters most — prioritize survivability relics in the first areas. No build saves you from bad dodges.

S-Tier Build: Wraith Summoner

AttributeDetail
Core ItemWraith Calling Bell
Key RelicNight of the Lord
Backup WeaponsAny with good stagger
PlaystyleSummon, dodge, let wraiths do the work
Best NightfarerSummoner-class characters

Why it's #1: Wraith Calling Bell summons deal massive damage and scale with your relics. Combined with Night of the Lord, you output damage while staying at a safe distance. This build trivializes boss encounters because your wraiths continue dealing damage even when you're dodging attack patterns. It's also the most beginner-friendly build — you learn boss patterns while your summons do the work.

A-Tier Build: Faith Guardian

AttributeDetail
Core WeaponAny Great Spear
Key RelicFaith Scaling Relic
TalismansStance damage + Crit boosters
PlaystylePoke, stance break, charged incantation
Best NightfarerGuardian-class

Why it's great: Great spears have excellent reach and stance damage. With the Faith relic, you can charge incantations during stance break openings for massive burst damage. The stance break tactic is particularly effective in co-op — while the boss is staggered, your entire team can unload their strongest attacks. This build excels at controlling the fight's tempo.

A-Tier Build: Bleed Assassin

AttributeDetail
Core WeaponsDual Uchigatanas or Rivers of Blood
Key RelicsBleed buildup + Attack speed
TalismansLord of Blood's Exultation
PlaystyleAggressive, stay close, proc hemorrhage

Why it's great: Hemorrhage procs deal percentage-based damage, melting bosses regardless of their total HP pool. Dual katanas or curved swords stack bleed incredibly fast. The percentage damage mechanic makes this build scale exceptionally well into late-run content where boss HP values become massive. Lord of Blood's Exultation grants an attack power buff on every hemorrhage proc, creating a self-reinforcing damage loop.

B-Tier Build: Comet Sorcerer

AttributeDetail
Core SpellComet Azur
Key RelicsFP regen + Magic damage
TalismansGraven-Mass + Cerulean Amber
PlaystylePosition safely, nuke during openings

Comet Azur still deletes bosses when you have the FP to sustain it. The risk is higher in the roguelike format due to FP management — you can't guarantee finding FP regen relics early. This build drops to B-Tier because of its dependency on specific relic drops. When it works, it's devastating. When RNG doesn't cooperate, you're a glass cannon with no cannon.

Co-op Squad Composition — The Golden Trio

The ideal co-op squad follows the classic RPG trinity: 1 Tank + 1 DPS + 1 Support. Bosses have more health in co-op but aggro splits across players, making coordinated teams far more effective than solo play.

Early Survival Priority — First Areas Matter Most

Survival Checklist

  • Prioritize survivability relics in the first two areas — defense scales better than offense early
  • Always grab Spirit Ashes when offered — they provide free damage and aggro splitting
  • Learn boss patterns before optimizing DPS — fundamentals beat build min-maxing
  • Can't respec in Nightreign — builds are locked per run, choose your Nightfarer wisely
