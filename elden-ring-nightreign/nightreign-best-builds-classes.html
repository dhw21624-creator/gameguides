Nightfarer's Codex — Run #347

Nightreign Best Builds & Class Guide

Unlike base Elden Ring, Nightreign's roguelike structure means you build from scratch each run. Relic drops are random. Weapons are randomized. Your class choice is the only constant. After hundreds of runs, four builds consistently clear Nightlord encounters — whether solo or in co-op. This guide ranks every Nightfarer and details the relic synergies that make them work.

II. S-Tier: Wraith Summoner

The Wraith Summoner builds around the Wraith Calling Bell and the Night of the Lord relic, which together create an army of spectral summons that deal massive damage while you stay at range. Summons scale with your relic's damage bonuses, and unlike most builds, you're outputting damage while dodging. Against Nightlords, the wraiths draw aggro while you land safe hits during recovery windows.

Core Setup: Wraith Calling Bell (primary), Night of the Lord relic, any backup weapon with high stagger for critical windows. Playstyle: summon, dodge, maintain distance, let wraiths work.

III. Faith Guardian (Tank/DPS)

Great Spears have exceptional reach and stance damage. The Faith scaling relic lets you charge incantations during openings for massive burst. The Guardian's natural poise allows trading hits while charging — something no other class can do safely. Works best in co-op where teammates can capitalize on the stagger windows you create.

Core Setup: Any Great Spear + Faith Scaling relic + Stance Damage talisman + Crit Booster talisman. Playstyle: poke from range, break stance, charge incantation for the crit window.

IV. Bleed Assassin (Burst DPS)

Hemorrhage procs deal percentage-based damage that melts bosses regardless of their total HP pool. Dual katanas with Hemorrhage Buildup relics apply bleed in 2-3 hits. Against roguelike bosses with inflated HP, percentage damage is king. The Assassin's mobility tools (quickstep, backstab iframes) provide survivability that pure DPS classes lack.

VI. Map Mechanics: Shifting Earth Events

Nightreign features a dynamic map where locations shift between runs. Shifting Earth events — like moving fortresses — contain high-tier loot but require defeating a mini-boss under a strict time limit. Securing these events is the difference between a failed run and a Nightlord victory. Always prioritize Shifting Earth markers when they appear.
