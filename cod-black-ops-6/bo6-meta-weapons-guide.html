II. Map Control Protocols

Highrise — Control Mode

Power Position A: Rooftop AC unit — sightline on two entrances, hard to flank.
Power Position B: Elevator shaft corner — ambush rotating players with pre-aimed headshots.
Rotation Rule: Always pop smoke when crossing between floors. The open sightlines are death traps.

Nuketown — Search & Destroy

Defense: Garage right-side closet for flanking bomb plants. Second-floor bedroom wardrobe for the cross-map angle on A-site.
Post-Plant: Rotate immediately. Staying near the bomb site on Nuketown is a spawn-trap invitation.

IV. Omni-Movement Mastery

The new movement system allows 90-degree slides in a single motion. Master the "slide-peek" technique: sprint → slide → turn 90° mid-slide → fire. Practice in the firing range until it's muscle memory. TTK is ~15% faster than previous titles—vertical recoil control now matters more than horizontal.

V. Zombies Mode: Early Survival Guide

Perks are now tied to specific map zones and must be physically carried and slotted—no more passive buffs. The ASG-89 with Explosive Round attachment deals 3x damage to special zombies in melee range. Make it your priority acquisition.

Final Directive: The difference between a 2.0 K/D and a 0.8 K/D is map knowledge. Learn every sightline. Pre-aim every corner. Control the power positions.
