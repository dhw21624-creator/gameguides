Gears of War: E-Day Campaign Guide — Tips & Walkthrough Essentials

Category: Campaign Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Gears of War: E-Day

Gears of War: E-Day, released October 6, 2026 on Xbox Series X|S and PC (day one on Game Pass, with Premium early access from October 1), takes the series back to the beginning. Developed by The Coalition, the campaign is a prequel set on Emergence Day — the day the Locust Horde burrowed up from the planet's depths and humanity's war began. You play as a young Marcus Fenix alongside Dom Santiago, experiencing the invasion that shaped the Gears saga. This campaign guide covers the combat fundamentals you need, from treating firefights like puzzles to mastering the new verticality.

Short version: Treat every E-Day firefight like a puzzle: coordinate with squadmates, use flanking routes and high ground, trust cover only for a few seconds (it is destructible), and grab weapon placements along the path.

Combat as a Puzzle: Squad Coordination First

The campaign's core design philosophy is simple: combat is a puzzle. Encounters are built as problem-solving scenarios rather than raw DPS checks. The fastest route through any firefight is not the most aggressive one — it is the one that uses the environment and your squadmates to break the enemy's formation.

Concrete habits that win E-Day fights:

  • Coordinate with squadmates. AI squadmates respond to positioning; create crossfire lanes so enemies cannot safely focus one target.
  • Use flanking routes. Most arenas have a route that loops around the enemy's exposed side. Take it while your squad draws fire, then hit the Locust from behind.
  • Take high ground. Vertical positions give you better sightlines and force enemies to climb into your line of fire. The game's new verticality makes high ground genuinely valuable.
  • Exploit enemy focus. When enemies are targeting your allies, they ignore you. That is your window to reposition, reload, or flank.

Cover Mastery: Cover Is Destructible

Cover has always been Gears' signature, but E-Day adds a crucial wrinkle: cover is destructible. The walls, barriers, and sandbags you hide behind will not protect you forever — sustained enemy fire, grenades, and heavy weapons chew through them.

The practical rule: only trust cover for a few seconds. Use it to reload, pop out for a burst, and reposition before it shatters. If you plant yourself behind one wall and wait, the Locust will blow it apart with you behind it. Treat cover as a temporary tool, not a permanent shield.

When cover is breaking, your options are: move to the next cover point (check the lane before sprinting), use the destructible wall's collapse as cover for a smoke-style advance, or push out aggressively while enemies are reloading their heavy weapons. Standing still behind crumbling cover is the classic E-Day death.

New Verticality & Jump

E-Day expands positioning with new verticality and a jump ability. Ledges, rooftops, and raised platforms are now legitimate routes, not just decoration. Use them to:

  • Flank above enemies and drop in behind their line.
  • Escape encirclement when the ground floor is collapsing.
  • Hold high ground during holdout sections where the enemy must funnel up a single approach.

The jump is short but decisive — it clears low obstacles, opens ledge grabs, and changes how you read arenas. Practice using it mid-combat: a well-timed jump to high ground can reset an engagement that was turning against you.

Weapon Placements: Pick Up Along the Path

E-Day's arenas are seeded with weapon placements along the path — heavy weapons, grenades, and special tools positioned where the designers expect you to need them. Pay attention to them: a marked weapon placement is almost always a hint about the coming encounter.

When you see a placement, plan around it. A sniper rifle placement before an open courtyard says "clear sightlines first." A Boomshot placement before a tanky enemy says "save the heavy shot for the big target." Grabbing placements proactively and carrying them into the next fight gives you an immediate edge over the Locust's numerical superiority.

Tip: Weapon placements reset or respawn in some sections, so if you cleared a heavy weapon, return for it before a tough boss encounter rather than saving ammo you will never use.

Modes Overview: Horde Siege & PvP

Beyond the campaign, E-Day ships with Horde Siege, a PvE mode, and new 4v4 PvP maps. Horde Siege modernizes the classic Horde formula — wave defense with escalating enemy types and boss waves — with the siege twist: enemies breach from multiple directions, and you must manage defensive positions as they shift. It is the best place to practice the campaign's combat fundamentals with friends.

The new 4v4 maps are built for E-Day's faster movement and verticality. Cover destruction plays a major role in PvP, too: if you camp the same wall, expect it to be exploded out from under you. Movement, map knowledge, and weapon placements decide matches as much as aim.

Classic & New Weapons

E-Day brings back the series' classic arsenal — the Lancer with its chainsaw bayonet and the Gnasher shotgun remain essential — while adding new anti-Locust weapons built for the invasion setting. The new arsenal includes tools designed specifically to handle Locust armor and swarm tactics: heavier suppression options, precision tools for the Locust's armored units, and deployables that change arena control.

For campaign efficiency: keep a Lancer for mid-range suppression and revved-chainsaw punishes, a Gnasher for close quarters, and swap your third slot based on the weapon placements ahead. Boss enemies with heavy armor reward anti-armor weapons — check your loadout before a big fight.

Collectibles & Side Objectives

E-Day's campaign tracks collectibles and side objectives through the walkthrough. Collectibles include lore documents, dog tags, and environmental set pieces that flesh out the Emergence Day story. Side objectives are optional combat challenges — hold positions, eliminate priority targets, or complete sections without dying — that reward extra currency and gear unlocks.

If you are a completionist, explore every arena before advancing: several collectibles are hidden behind the new verticality (rooftops, ledge paths) and require using the jump. The side objectives are clearly marked on the map, but some only appear if you enter an area from a specific route, so vary your approach on repeat plays.

Walkthrough Essentials: Starting Strong

For the opening hours, keep these principles in mind:

  1. Follow your squad's positioning but never stand still — cover breaks, lanes collapse, and enemies flank.
  2. Use the first hour to practice the jump and high-ground routes; later arenas assume you understand verticality.
  3. Collect weapon placements aggressively; they are the designers' cheat sheet for each encounter.
  4. When enemies focus your squadmates, move — that is the flank window.
  5. Save heavy weapons for armored targets and boss waves, not trash mobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to play the older Gears games before E-Day?

A: No. E-Day is a prequel set on Emergence Day, so it is a natural entry point. Veterans will recognize the setup, but newcomers get everything they need in-game.

Q: Is cover really destructible everywhere?

A: Most cover is destructible to some degree, but the campaign clearly marks the strong points. Use light cover for seconds, strong cover for longer — and never stay put.

Q: What is the best weapon loadout for the campaign?

A: Lancer + Gnasher covers almost everything. Add a heavy or precision weapon from placements depending on the encounter ahead.

Q: Can I play the campaign in co-op?

A: Yes, the campaign supports co-op, which makes squad coordination mechanics even more powerful.

Conclusion

Gears of War: E-Day is a return to the series' roots with modern movement and destructible cover shaking up the formula. Treat combat like a puzzle, coordinate with your squad, never over-trust cover, and use the new verticality — and you will survive Emergence Day ready for whatever the Locust throw at you.
