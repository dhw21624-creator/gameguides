GTA 6 Complete Guide 2026: Gameplay, Money Making & Combat Tips

Welcome to Leonida — Rockstar's most ambitious open world yet. Grand Theft Auto 6 doesn't just iterate on the formula; it rewrites the rules of crime, police response, and economic progression. This complete guide covers the dual protagonist system, the revamped wanted mechanics, the Leonida economy, and the strategies that separate street-level thugs from criminal empire builders.

Dual Protagonist System — Lucia & Jason

Unlike GTA V's three-character free-switching, GTA 6's dual protagonist system emphasizes interdependence. Lucia and Jason aren't interchangeable — they're complementary.

CharacterSpecialtyBest Used For
LuciaPrecision & StealthInfiltration, lockpicking, quiet takedowns, high-security area access
JasonBrute Force & Weakness MarkingHeavy combat, crowd control, enemy weakness identification, fire support
Tactical Switching: In missions, switch roles to cover different angles. Have Jason provide suppressing fire while Lucia advances to the objective. Jason's special ability reveals enemy weak points — mark them before Lucia engages for efficient clearing.

The Revamped Wanted System

Forget everything you know about GTA police chases. The new system is a strategic chess match, not a speed contest.

Dynamic Police Response

Police no longer spawn randomly. They respond based on your real-time criminal behavior, establishing precise search perimeters. Ground patrols coordinate with aerial surveillance in fixed patterns. Simply driving fast won't save you anymore — the game demands tactical evasion.

Visual & Feature Recognition

  • Visual Identification: Police remember your clothing and vehicle. Always keep spare outfits in your trunk.
  • Mask System: Use masks to delay identification — but they only work if you change before being spotted.
  • Feature Recognition: NPCs can record your crimes on phones and post to "Social Club," raising your wanted level in certain areas.

Evasion Tactics

  • Underground passages break line of sight from helicopters
  • Narrow alleys force police vehicles into single-file pursuit
  • Tactical flanking by police means staying in one spot equals death
  • 6-star wanted level returns — military-level response for maximum chaos

The Leonida Economy — Money Making Guide

Active Income: Heists

Heists form the backbone of your active income. Planning matters more than ever — scout locations with binoculars, identify security cameras, and plan escape routes before you pull the trigger.

Gulf Heist Mission Chain (Mid-Game Priority)

The Gulf Heist series is the most profitable mid-game mission chain. Unlock it as soon as you have 2 million in capital. The setup missions are extensive, but the final payout funds your first high-end property. On Hard Mode, the finale grants a +25% payout bonus — worth the difficulty spike.

Team Roles: Hacker (bypass security), Driver (escape vehicle + route), Heavy (crowd control + suppression). Don't solo high-difficulty heists — GTA 6 AI is significantly smarter.

Community data shows players focused on passive income assets earn approximately 32% more per hour than those relying solely on active heists. The formula: active heists for big money, passive businesses for steady money.

Critical Warning: Never sell high-value cargo in public sessions. Use "invite-only" or "friends-only" sessions for 100% delivery safety. The slight income reduction (5-10%) is worth avoiding complete cargo loss.
