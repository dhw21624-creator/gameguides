GTA Online: Ultimate Money Guide 2026

Best Heists — Passive Businesses — Weekly Updates — Smart Investments — Updated June 2026

Overview

With GTA VI on the horizon, Rockstar has been more generous than ever with GTA Online payouts. The Cayo Perico Heist remains the gold standard for solo money-making, but several new businesses and missions introduced in recent updates have closed the gap significantly. This guide ranks every major money-making method by $/hour, distinguishes solo vs. crew activities, and provides a weekly checklist to maximize your earnings with minimal grind. All figures assume optimized strategies and average completion times.

Top 7 Money-Making Methods Ranked by $/Hour

#1 Cayo Perico Heist $1.5-1.8M / hour

SOLO The undisputed king. With the Kosatka submarine and Sparrow helicopter, a full Cayo Perico run (scope + prep + finale) takes 55-65 minutes. Always target the Pink Diamond (primary) with 2 stacks of cocaine (secondary) for maximum payout. After the cooldown nerf (144 minutes solo), you can complete 2-3 runs per day.

Optimal approach: Kosatka → Drainage Tunnel entry → Main Dock exit. Fill your loot bag at the Main Dock before escaping — the boats there guarantee a clean getaway.

#2 The Contract — Dr. Dre VIP Contract $1.0-1.2M / hour

SOLO The Agency's VIP Contract pays $1,000,000 for the finale after 11 setup missions. Setup missions take ~10 minutes each during the first playthrough. On repeat completions, you can skip the cutscenes, bringing total time to ~50 minutes. The passive income from the Agency safe ($20K/48 min) is a bonus.

#3 The Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid $750K / hour

SOLO The newest heist addition, available for free from Vincent at the Vespucci Police Station. 5 setup missions + finale. Payout is $500K base + $250K first-time bonus. Setup missions are significantly easier than Cayo Perico, making this the best entry-level money method for new players.

#4 Nightclub Warehouse (Passive) $60K / hour (passive)

SOLO Once fully upgraded with 5 technicians assigned to South American Imports, Pharmaceutical Research, Cargo and Shipments, Sporting Goods, and Cash Creation, the Nightclub generates ~$60,000 per real-time hour passively. Key: keep popularity maxed (swap DJs for $10K when it drops below 90%). Sell when the warehouse is ~$800K to guarantee a single vehicle delivery.

#5 Acid Lab $350K per sell (2.5 hours production)

SOLO Complete all First Dose and Last Dose missions from Dax to unlock the Acid Lab and its upgrade. With the equipment upgrade, a full Acid Lab produces $351,840 (in a public lobby with the high-demand bonus). Production takes ~2.5 hours. Buy supplies for $60K — the profit margin is excellent.

#6 Auto Shop Contracts $300-500K / hour

SOLO The Union Depository contract pays $300K for ~30 minutes of work. The Auto Shop also unlocks exotic exports ($20K per vehicle) and customer vehicle delivery ($25-50K per vehicle). A solid supplement to your main money method.

#7 Bunker (Passive) $210K per sell (11.5 hours production)

CREW Buy supplies ($75K), wait for them to be converted to stock, sell to Los Santos for $210K (with full upgrades). Always sell after one batch of supplies to guarantee a single vehicle. Selling solo is possible but tight on time for the Dune FAV mission.

FAQ

Q: What's the fastest way to make $10 million from scratch?

Buy the Kosatka ($2.2M) → grind Cayo Perico 6 times (1.5M average payout each) → ~7 hours total. Add the Nightclub and Acid Lab for passive income while you heist. Total time to $10M: roughly 10-12 hours of optimized play.

Q: Is the Cayo Perico cooldown still 144 minutes?

Yes. The solo cooldown is 144 minutes (2.4 hours). If you run it with 2+ players, the cooldown drops to 48 minutes. Having a reliable heist partner doubles your Cayo Perico income potential.

Q: Should I buy the Oppressor Mk II in 2026?

If you're running businesses, yes. The Oppressor Mk II is the fastest way to travel between your Nightclub, Bunker, Acid Lab, and Agency. It pays for itself through time saved. However, with GTA VI approaching, consider if the $8M investment is worth it for your remaining playtime.

THE BOTTOM LINE

GTA Online's economy in 2026 revolves around three pillars: Cayo Perico for active income (best $/hour), Nightclub + Acid Lab for passive income (money while you're doing other things), and weekly bonuses for opportunistic gains. The optimal weekly routine: Cayo Perico → sell Nightclub when full → resupply Acid Lab → check weekly bonuses → repeat. With this rotation, $10 million per week is achievable with ~2 hours of daily play. Spend wisely — prioritize income-generating assets over cosmetic purchases, and always buy on discount weeks.
