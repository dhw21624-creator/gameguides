I. Release Date & Platforms CONFIRMED

Launch Date: November 19, 2026

Platforms at Launch: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S

PC Release: Confirmed to arrive after console launch. Based on Rockstar's historical patterns (GTA V: 18-month delay, RDR2: 13-month delay), expect a 2027 PC port.

II. Setting: Vice City Reimagined CONFIRMED

The neon-soaked streets of Vice City return—but this is not the 1980s playground you remember. Rockstar promises a "significantly larger and more detailed" version of their Miami-inspired metropolis, with realistic lighting, dynamic weather, and water physics that set a new standard for open-world immersion.

III. Dual Protagonists CONFIRMED

For the first time in the series, the story follows both a male and female lead—reportedly a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style duo. You'll switch between them as the narrative unfolds, similar to GTA V's triad system but with deeper character-specific abilities and relationship mechanics.

As of May 2026, Rockstar has not confirmed any monetization model for the single-player campaign.
