Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.4 Guide: NCPD Hunt, New Bikes & Dogtown Black Market

Category: Update Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.4 (August 20, 2026, on PC) is CD Projekt Red's "Final Farewell" to Night City's single-player content ahead of the next mainline game, Project Orion. It is a substantial send-off: a full NCPD Hunt questline with badge progression, three new motorcycles, a hidden Dogtown black market, and a post-launch hot update that finally lets you pilot the M-07 "Unicorn" heavy mech. This guide breaks down everything Update 2.4 adds and how to make the most of it.

Quick take: Finish all NCPD Hunt contracts to earn the Enforcer smart pistol and Titanium Spine α cyberware, and visit the Signal Blind Spot black market in Dogtown for the update's hidden vendor gear.

The NCPD Hunt Questline: Jobs, Contracts & Badges

The centrepiece of Update 2.4 is the NCPD Hunt questline, a structured law-enforcement loop with meaningful progression:

  • 12 side jobs — a full chain of NCPD-flavoured missions spread across Night City.
  • 5 high-difficulty "hunt contracts" — optional endgame-tier jobs that escalate quickly and demand a strong build.
  • Badge ranking system — your hunter progress is tracked through three tiers: Star, Expert, and Master. Completing contracts raises your badge and unlocks better rewards.

The two signature rewards are worth the grind: the Enforcer, a legendary smart pistol that is one of the strongest handguns in the game, and the Titanium Spine α, a rare spine cyberware that gives a significant survivability and mobility edge. Both only drop or unlock at the Master tier, so budget time for the full contract list.

Dogtown's Signal Blind Spot & the Black Market

Dogtown gains a new underground zone in Update 2.4: the Signal Blind Spot, a hidden black market vendor district where the update's most interesting exotic gear shows up. It is easy to miss, so look for the new side quest "Tears of the Blind Dog" — completing it reveals the zone and introduces the vendors who stock black-market-only items. This is the best source of the update's rare weapon and cyberware drops outside the NCPD Hunt reward table.

Mecha Protocol Hot Update: Driving the Unicorn

Less than a week after launch, the August 28, 2026 "Mecha Protocol" hot update delivered one of the most-requested features: you can now drive the M-07 "Unicorn" heavy mech — previously only seen as a vehicle boss — as a playable machine. The mech is a slow, devastating presence with heavy firepower, and it is the clearest sign that Update 2.4 is the farewell tour it claims to be: CDPR is sending players off with the game's most ridiculous toy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Update 2.4 available on all platforms?

A: Update 2.4 launched on PC on August 20, 2026, with console rollout following the standard staggered schedule.

Q: How do I unlock the Enforcer smart pistol?

A: Reach the Master badge tier in the NCPD Hunt questline by completing all side jobs and high-difficulty hunt contracts.

Q: Where is the Dogtown black market?

A: Complete the side quest "Tears of the Blind Dog" to open the Signal Blind Spot vendor zone.

Q: Is 2.4 the last Cyberpunk 2077 update?

A: CDPR has framed 2.4 as the final single-player content push before development shifts fully to Project Orion. The Mecha Protocol hot update is the most recent addition.

Conclusion

Update 2.4 gives Cyberpunk 2077 the send-off it deserves: a proper lawman questline with real progression, three new bikes, a hidden black market, and a playable heavy mech. Run the NCPD Hunt contracts to Master tier, grab the Enforcer and Titanium Spine α, and pay the Signal Blind Spot a visit before Project Orion takes over. It is the definitive last ride through Night City.
