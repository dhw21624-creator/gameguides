New vehicles, a full AutoDrive system, a Photo Mode creative suite, native Mac support on Apple Silicon, and a mountain of technical upgrades. AutoDrive Modes

  • Destination Mode: Set a map marker and let your vehicle navigate automatically through Night City traffic.
  • Wandering Mode: No destination? Enjoy a leisurely cinematic ride — the AI chooses scenic routes.
  • Safety Systems: AutoDrive disables during combat or when the vehicle is heavily damaged. You can override at any time.
  • Compatibility: Works with all owned vehicles including motorcycles.

Delamain Taxi Service

The beloved AI Delamain now offers on-demand taxi services:
Unlock Requirement: Complete "Don't Lose Your Mind" job (any outcome) and receive Delamain's message.
How to Use: Open vehicle call menu → Select "Delamain Cab" → Choose destination → Pay eurodollar fee.
Why It Matters: Hands-free fast travel with full immersion — no loading screens breaking your Night City experience.

27 New Spawnable NPCs

The Photo Mode now includes as spawnable characters: Rita Wheeler, the Cassel twins, various fixers, Dum Dum, the iconic iguana, Brendan (the vending machine), and many more memorable characters from across Night City.
