Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.2: New Features, Best Monowire Build & Phantom Liberty Boss Guide

Update 2.2 drops with a wave of quality-of-life features, a meta-shifting rework to Monowire scaling, and over ten new vehicles hitting the streets of Night City. Whether you're a returning edgerunner or diving into Phantom Liberty for the first time, this guide covers everything you need to chrome up and dominate.

I. Update 2.2: What's New

Customizable Radio Stations

The most requested feature since launch finally arrives. Create up to 5 custom playlists from the entire in-game radio catalog — mix Samurai, Morro Rock, Body Heat Radio, and all 11 stations into your own rotation. Access from the Radio menu under the new "Custom" tab. Each playlist holds 30 tracks max.

Photo Mode 2.0

Photo Mode receives a major overhaul: new drone camera with free-flight mode (up to 50m radius from V), depth-of-field sliders, 8 new filter presets, and the ability to toggle NPC visibility. A new "Action Pause" feature lets you freeze mid-combat — perfect for capturing those chrome-drenched firefight moments.

V. New Photo Mode Tips for Content Creators

Photo Mode 2.0 isn't just for screenshots — it's a powerful tool for uncovering hidden details and Easter eggs. Here are some pro tips:

  • Drone Mode free-flight: Use the drone to clip through building facades in City Center. Several inaccessible interiors are fully modeled — the dev team hid concept art and developer messages in at least 6 locations.
  • Action Pause timing: Pause mid-Sandevistan activation for the most dramatic combat shots. The distortion effect adds a unique chromatic aberration that regular filters can't replicate.
  • NPC Toggle: Disable NPCs in crowded areas like Afterlife or Heywood markets, then re-enable them one by one to find unique character models (including 4 hidden Johnny Silverhand variants).
  • Golden hour in Night City: Set in-game time to 17:45 for the best natural lighting. Combine with the "Cinematic 2077" filter and max depth-of-field for magazine-quality shots.
