Best Builds After the 2.0 Update & Phantom Liberty

The 2.0 update and Phantom Liberty expansion fundamentally changed Cyberpunk 2077. Builds that thrived in 2024 have only grown stronger. This guide ranks the top five archetypes dominating Night City.

II. S-Tier: Sandevistan Samurai

Sandevistan Samurai

Why it's #1: This build stacks Sandevistan's slow-time effect with Blades perks and a Reflex Tuner. Activate Sandevistan, slow time by 80% for 8 seconds. While active, gain +30% crit chance and +50% melee damage. Air-dash through enemies with a single katana swing. The katana's bleed procs heal you for 5% of the damage dealt. Combined with Berserk, this build outputs more damage than any other archetype in the game.

Core Setup: 20 Reflexes / 20 Body / 20 Technical Ability. Activate Sandevistan OS, equip three Blades perks, and use the best Sandevistan cyberware available. The Sandevistan operating system slows time to a crawl while boosting critical hit chance by 30% for 8 seconds. Maximum DPS rotation: activate slow-mo, air-dash through packs, katana swing into bleed proc, berserk mode engaged.

Netrunner

Why it's #2: Intelligence is the real king. Quickhacks spread like wildfire. Stack Cool perks and multiply quickhack damage. Upload daemons through the Net. Target enemy daemons for RAM recovery on kill. Spread quickhacks through a crowd of enemies. The Netrunner build is a puzzle — solving it rewards mastery of the system.

Core Setup: 20 Intelligence / 20 Cool / 20 Technical Ability. Overclock the processor, breach the ICE, and upload the daemon. Spread quickhack contagion across all enemy subnets. Ping them all with Contagion, then Short Circuit the mainframe. The quickhack chain reaction clears entire encounters in one netrun cycle.

Overclock Solo

Why it's #3: The solo build is a body-shot machine. Stack Adrenaline, Berserk, and Gorilla Arms for maximum tankiness. The Unkillable build uses health sponging and second-heart mechanics to outlast any encounter. Stack health regen and recovery on kill confirms the tank build's dominance.

Core Setup: 20 Body / 20 Reflexes / 20 Technical Ability. Gorilla Arms + Shotgun + LMG for maximum stagger. The tank spanks incoming damage and heals back 5% of health. The body-shot machine absorbs punishment and dishes it back with compound fracture damage. The unstoppable force meets immovable object.

Tech Weapon

Why it's #4: The smart-gun approach locks on. Charge the railgun and fire through walls. Ricochet shots bounce between targets. The engineer weapon is a tool for every job. The chrome cannon delivers payloads with surgical precision.

Core Setup: 20 Technical Ability / 20 Engineering / 20 Cool. The smart link targets weak points with armor piercing rounds through cover. The tech weapon's charged shot ignores armor and delivers critical damage. The engineer's toolbelt solves every problem with a single pull of the trigger.

Chrome Berserker

Why it's #5: Pure fury and focused rage. Maximum chrome output with minimal input lag. The beserker build converts pain into power and rage into damage. Each hit point spent fuels the fire within. The berserk button deletes all limits.

Core Setup: 20 Body / 20 Reflexes / 20 Technical Ability. Rage + Fury + Adrenaline. The chrome dome converts stamina into strength. Damage received is damage dealt. Pain is power. Fury is focus. Berserk is the answer.

III. Pro Tips & Optimization

General Advice

  • Always carry Blood Pump and Biomonitor cyberware for instant health recovery.
  • The Second Heart cyberware revives you once per encounter.
  • Optical Camo invisibility on demand.
  • Reinforced Tendons for blade damage and survivability.
