5 Best Builds in Cyberpunk 2077 Phantom Liberty 2.0

The Phantom Liberty expansion and the 2.0 update fundamentally overhauled Cyberpunk 2077. With the level cap raised to 60, a completely reworked perk system, the new Relic skill tree, and an expanded cyberware capacity system, every build in Night City needs a rethink. This guide breaks down the five most powerful builds dominating the current meta — from flaming katanas to reality-bending quickhacks.

I. Fire Samurai Build

Reflexes + Body — Thermal Katana Berserker

REF 20BODY 18TECH 15COOL 4INT 3

The Fire Samurai is a pure melee destroyer that hits enemies with devastating fire-based melee strikes. You charge in, deflect bullets back at your enemies, and finish them with style. The Thermal Katana — found in a furnace in the basement of the facility — is your signature weapon, dealing fire damage on every swing.

Core Perks:

  • Bullet Deflect — Reflects incoming projectiles back at enemies while blocking with your blade
  • Lead and Steel — Allows you to block incoming projectiles with your sword
  • Finisher: Blade Runner — Style-based finishers that eliminate enemies with flair
  • Air Dash + Dash — Maximum mobility for closing gaps and repositioning
  • Adrenaline Rush — Increases HP regeneration, provides a temporary HP boost at level 3
  • Quake + Wrecking Ball — Ground-pound crowd control for full melee domination
  • License to Chrome (maxed) — Expands cyberware capacity for maximum implants

Cyberware: Berserk operating system (boosts melee damage and attack speed, temporary invulnerability), Parabellum or Subdermal Armor for survivability. The Berserk OS makes you an unstoppable blender for its duration — pop it, rush in, and watch everything die.

Weapon: Thermal Katana (primary). The fire damage bypasses many enemy resistances and stacks with your melee perks.

II. God-Tier Netrunner Build

Intelligence + Tech — Overclock Quickhack God

INT 20TECH 18COOL 12REF 7BODY 3

The God-Tier Netrunner turns every battlefield into your personal sandbox. You never fire a bullet — you upload quickhacks that chain through entire squads while your RAM regenerates faster than enemies can react. This is the build for players who want to feel like a digital god.

Core Perks:

  • Overclock — Slows time and lets you apply additional quickhacks with increased recovery rate and damage
  • Hack Queue — Boosts RAM and allows multiple quickhacks uploaded to a single enemy simultaneously
  • Feedback Loop — Increases RAM regeneration with each quickhack kill
  • Proximate Propagation — Reduces quickhack costs and spreads them to nearby enemies
  • Queue Routing — Further reduces RAM cost of queued quickhacks
  • Power Surge + Encryption + System Overwhelm — Maximum hacking damage amplification
  • Ticking Time Bomb — Overclock creates an EMP blast that damages surrounding enemies
  • License to Chrome (maxed) — For full cyberware optimization

Cyberware: Tetratronic Rippler Mk.5 cyberdeck (best-in-slot — massive damage bonus and base RAM), EX-Disk + Memory Boost (increase RAM pool), RAM Reallocator (instant RAM regeneration in emergencies). For defense: Subdermal Armor, Parabellum, Bionic Joints. As an alternative, the Militech Canto Mk.6 cyberdeck grants access to the Blackwall Gateway ability — a devastating endgame option.

Recommended Quickhacks: Weapon Glitch, Cyberpsychosis (control), Detonate Grenade, Synapse Burnout (raw damage). For an Edgerunners-inspired Lucy build, equip the Monowire as your backup weapon.

III. Ninja Assassin Build

Cool + Reflexes — Optical Camo Silent Eliminator

COOL 20REF 18TECH 12BODY 7INT 3

The Ninja Assassin eliminates enemies before they know you exist. You move faster while crouching, sprint silently at level 3 Ninjutsu, and use Optical Camo to reposition into perfect ambush angles. This build rewards patience and precision — every engagement is on your terms.

Core Perks:

  • Focus + Deadeye — Massive handgun and precision weapon performance boosts
  • Ninjutsu — Increased crouch movement speed; at level 3, enables silent sprinting
  • Juggler — Enables effective throwing knife chains as part of your kit
  • Air Dash + Dash — Mobility for rapid repositioning between kills

Cyberware: Optical Camo (signature implant — grants 100% crit chance on silenced pistol shots while active), Mantis Blades (melee finisher for when things get loud). The Optical Camo/silenced pistol synergy is the core of this build — decloak, headshot, recloak, repeat.

Weapon: Silenced pistol (primary), throwing knives, Mantis Blades (backup). The silenced pistol gains a 100% critical hit chance while Optical Camo is active, making every shot lethal.

IV. Techno-Necromancer Build

Technical Ability + Intelligence — Chain Lightning Tech Wielder

TECH 20INT 15COOL 13REF 9BODY 3

The Techno-Necromancer is a tech weapon specialist who turns every charged shot into a room-clearing event. Chain Lightning makes your charged tech shots arc to up to three enemies, and combined with smart weapon tracking from Intelligence perks, nothing escapes your crosshairs.

Core Perks:

  • Bolt — Enhanced charged shots from tech weapons
  • Chain Lightning — Arcing electrical attack that hits up to three enemies per charged shot
  • Ticking Time Bomb — EMP blast on Overclock activation (essential synergy)
  • License to Chrome (maxed) — Cyberware capacity for maximum implant synergy
  • Deadeye + Focus (Cool tree) — Precision damage boost
  • Target Lock Transfer + Acquisition Specialist (Intelligence) — Smart weapon tracking optimization

Cyberware: Flexible — prioritize anything that boosts tech weapon charge speed and smart weapon targeting. Subdermal Armor and heal-on-kill implants round out survivability.

Weapons: Yinglong SMG (smart weapon), Widow Maker tech rifle, custom shotgun. The Widow Maker charges through walls, and with Chain Lightning, you can clear rooms without ever exposing yourself.

V. David Martinez 2.0 Build

Body + Technical Ability — Sandevistan Gorilla Arms Brawler

BODY 20TECH 18REF 14COOL 5INT 3

Channel the legend of David Martinez himself. This build is pure, unapologetic aggression — activate Sandevistan, rush into the enemy line, and pulverize everything with flaming Gorilla Arms. The DB-2 Satara tech shotgun covers mid-range until you close the gap.

Core Perks:

  • All left and center-side Body skill tree perks — Maximum survivability and melee damage
  • Adrenaline Converter — Increases movement speed significantly
  • Blood Pump — On-demand HP regeneration
  • License to Chrome (maxed) — Required to fit both Sandevistan and Gorilla Arms

Cyberware: Militech Apogee Sandevistan (best-in-slot — the same model David used), Thermal Gorilla Arms (sets enemies on fire on hit), Adrenaline Converter, Blood Pump. The Apogee Sandevistan gives you the longest slow-time window available — use it to flank, charge Gorilla Arm heavy attacks, and watch enemies explode.

Weapons: DB-2 Satara tech shotgun (primary ranged), custom Satara shotgun (backup). Thermal Gorilla Arms for everything else.
