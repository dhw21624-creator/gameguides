Elden Ring Best Builds After Patch 1.13

PvE & PvP Meta Builds — Shadow of the Erdtree DLC Included — Updated June 2026

Overview

Patch 1.13 for Elden Ring brought significant balance changes to both the base game and the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC. FromSoftware adjusted poise damage on several weapon classes, rebalanced Ashes of War, and fine-tuned PvP scaling. This guide covers the strongest builds for both PvE and PvP in the current meta, with detailed stat allocations, weapon choices, talisman setups, and playstyle recommendations.

Whether you're tackling the DLC's final boss, invading in Limgrave, or dueling at the Academy Gate, the builds below represent the most optimized setups tested by the community after the 1.13 patch. All builds assume Rune Level 150 (the community standard for PvP and endgame PvE) unless otherwise noted.

1. Bloodfiend's Arm Bleed Build (S-Tier PvE)

Why It's Meta

Patch 1.13 did not touch the Bloodfiend's Arm despite community expectations. This colossal weapon inflicts ridiculous bleed buildup per hit — roughly 180-200 bleed buildup per charged heavy attack when infused with Blood affinity and buffed with Seppuku Ash of War. Against DLC bosses like Promised Consort Radahn and Bayle the Dread, it triggers hemorrhage every 2-3 hits, dealing percentage-based damage that scales into NG+7.

2. Dual Nagakiba Dex/Arc (S-Tier PvP)

Why It's Meta

The Nagakiba's exceptional range — the longest katana in the game — combined with dual-wield L1 speed and blood infusion makes this the most oppressive PvP setup post-1.13. FromSoftware slightly nerfed Rivers of Blood in 1.13, but left nagakibas untouched, cementing them as the #1 PvP katana option. The running L1 catches rolls, and the crouch-poke R1 interrupts most weapon arts.

PvP Tip: Apply Seppuku on your off-hand Nagakiba before combat starts. The self-bleed procs Lord of Blood's Exultation and White Mask for an immediate 30% damage boost that lasts 20 seconds — enough for the entire duel.

3. Blasphemous Blade Faith Build (A+ Tier All-Rounder)

The Blasphemous Blade remains the king of PvE sustain. Taker's Flames heals you for 10% of max HP plus 150 flat HP per enemy hit. In group fights or against large bosses like the Fire Giant, a single weapon art can restore your entire health bar. Patch 1.13 buffed Fire damage slightly in PvE, making this build even more potent.

4. Moonveil Int/Dex (A Tier — Best for Duels)

Moonveil's Transient Moonlight weapon art received a mild speed buff in 1.13, making the R2 variant (horizontal slash) harder to reaction-dodge. Paired with Night Comet from the Staff of Loss (which enemies cannot input-read), this build dominates at mid-range. For PvP, the R1 vertical projectile roll-catches anyone panic-rolling backwards.

Stats: VIG 55 / MND 20 / END 20 / STR 12 / DEX 40 / INT 50

Talismans: Magic Scorpion Charm, Shard of Alexander, Radagon Icon, Erdtree's Favor +2

5. Greatsword of Damnation — Invasion Build (A Tier)

The Sword of Damnation (from the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC) has become the premier invasion weapon. Its unique skill, Golden Cruelty, grabs opponents from surprising range and impales them for ~1400 damage at 50 STR / 35 FAI. The grab cannot be parried and has hyper armor during the lunge, making it a guaranteed trade-win against lighter weapons.

Stats: VIG 60 / END 35 / STR 50 / FAI 35

Invasion Tip: Host trick-mirror + Concealing Veil talisman. Sneak near a group of PvE enemies, then use Golden Cruelty to isolate and one-shot the host's phantom before the host can react.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best build for Shadow of the Erdtree bosses?

The Bloodfiend's Arm bleed build is the undisputed king for DLC bosses. Many DLC bosses have massive HP pools but are vulnerable to hemorrhage. Combined with Cragblade for stance-breaking, you'll stagger and bleed them in rapid succession.

Q: What changed in Patch 1.13 for PvP?

Rivers of Blood received a slight damage nerf (~5%), powerstanced straight sword L1 chain was slowed by 2 frames, and Moonveil's Transient Moonlight R2 is now slightly faster. Dragon Communion incantations had their hyper armor reduced, making faith/arcane hybrid casters more punishable.

Q: What's a good beginner build for Elden Ring in 2026?

The Blasphemous Blade Faith build is the most forgiving for newcomers. It provides massive self-healing, excellent fire damage, and requires relatively low stat investment to get started. Rush to Volcano Manor to get the weapon, then pump Faith.

Q: Is Arcane still worth investing in after 1.13?

Absolutely. While Dragon Communion spells had their poise reduced, Arcane scaling on blood-infused weapons remains untouched. A 45 Arcane investment on a Blood Nagakiba adds roughly 80 bleed buildup per hit — that's the difference between proccing hemorrhage in 2 hits versus 4.

Summary

Elden Ring's Patch 1.13 meta is dominated by bleed builds in both PvE and PvP, with the Bloodfiend's Arm and Dual Nagakibas leading the pack. Faith-based builds with Blasphemous Blade remain the safest choice for PvE progression, while the Sword of Damnation has carved out a niche as the top invasion weapon. The key stat breakpoints for RL150 are 60 Vigor (mandatory), 45-50 in your primary damage stat, and enough Endurance for 101+ poise in PvP. Build around these fundamentals and adapt your talismans to your playstyle — the Lands Between (and the Realm of Shadow) are yours to dominate.
