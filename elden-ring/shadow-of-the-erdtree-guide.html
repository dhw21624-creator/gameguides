Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree Ultimate Guide

Best Builds, Scadutree Blessing System, Boss Strategies — Everything You Need to Conquer the Realm of Shadow

DLC Overview

Shadow of the Erdtree is Elden Ring’s massive expansion, introducing an entirely new region — the Realm of Shadow — roughly the size of Limgrave plus Liurnia combined. The DLC adds over 10 major bosses, 100+ new weapons, a brand-new progression system (Scadutree Blessing), and some of the hardest encounters FromSoftware has ever designed. This guide covers everything you need: how the Scadutree system works, the best builds to dominate DLC bosses, a complete breakdown of Promised Consort Radahn, and locations for every major boss.

Prerequisites: You must defeat Starscourge Radahn and Mohg, Lord of Blood to access the DLC. Enter through the cocoon in Mohgwyn Palace after interacting with the withered arm. Recommended level: 120–150 with fully upgraded flasks (+12).

Scadutree Blessing System Explained

The Scadutree Blessing is the single most important mechanic in the DLC. Without collecting Scadutree Fragments, your damage output and defenses will be severely gimped — regardless of your level or gear. This system replaces traditional level scaling within the Realm of Shadow.

How It Works

  • Scadutree Fragments: Collect these golden fragments scattered across the Realm of Shadow. Each blessing level requires 5 fragments.
  • Max Level: The blessing caps at 20, requiring a total of 100 Scadutree Fragments.
  • Effect: Each level increases your attack power and damage negation only within DLC areas. At level 20, you deal roughly 2x damage and take 50% less damage compared to level 0.
  • Revered Spirit Ashes: A parallel system that boosts the power of your Spirit Ashes and Torrent. Collect Revered Spirit Ash items to increase summon damage and survivability. Max level: 10.
Pro Tip: Before tackling any major DLC boss, aim for Scadutree Blessing level 12–14 for a fair fight, and level 16–18 for Radahn and Bayle. Early zones like Gravesite Plain and Belurat contain enough fragments to hit level 5–7 before your first remembrance boss.

Best DLC Build: Blasphemous Blade Faith (S-Tier)

Why This Build Dominates the DLC

The Blasphemous Blade is the undisputed king of DLC PvE for one reason: sustain. Its weapon art, Taker’s Flames, heals you for 10% of max HP + 150 flat HP per enemy hit. In the DLC, where healing windows are narrow and enemy aggression is relentless, this self-healing is invaluable. Combined with buff stacking, Taker’s Flames can hit for 4,500+ damage against Scadutree-scaled enemies, one-shotting most elite mobs and chunking bosses for 15–20% of their HP bar.

Against large bosses like Bayle, aim for the head for bonus damage. The heal triggers even if the boss blocks the physical hit — the fire damage portion always connects.

Promised Consort Radahn — Complete Boss Strategy

Radahn, Consort of Miquella is widely considered the hardest boss in FromSoftware history. He has two full phases, relentless combo chains, and an arena-wide light show in Phase 2 that can feel impossible to dodge. Here’s exactly how to beat him.

Phase 1: Starscourge Returns

Phase 1 Radahn retains many moves from his base-game fight but with faster recovery and new mixups. His gravity magic now pulls you toward him instead of away, setting up his cross-slash combo.

  • Gravity Pull → Cross-Slash: When he raises his left hand and you see purple energy, immediately stop attacking. The pull is followed by two sweeping slashes — dodge forward-right through the first, then immediately roll again to avoid the second.
  • Lion’s Claw Combo: A 5-hit chain ending in an overhead slam. Dodge the first three hits by rolling into Radahn (not away), then strafe right for the final two. The slam leaves a 1.5-second opening for a charged heavy or weapon art.
  • Gravity Meteor: He leaps into the air and returns as a meteor. Sprint perpendicular to his trajectory and jump as he lands to avoid the AoE shockwave.

Phase 2: Miquella’s Light

At roughly 60% HP, Radahn enters Phase 2. Miquella now rides on his back, and every attack leaves behind trailing light beams that explode after a short delay. This phase is where most players wipe repeatedly.

Critical Rule for Phase 2: When Radahn unleashes the massive circular light beam barrage (Miquella’s signature attack), dodge INTO the light beams, not away from them. The beams track your movement and fan outward — dodging inward places you in the gap between beams where the explosion delay hasn’t triggered yet. Dodging away guarantees you’ll eat multiple beam explosions.
  • Light Clone Barrage: Radahn creates 5 Miquella light clones that slash in sequence. Count the flashes: dodge on flash #1, #3, and #5. The real Radahn attacks immediately after the 5th clone.
  • Holy Nuke Grab: Miquella’s arms glow bright gold and Radahn lunges forward. This is a one-shot grab at most HP levels. Dodge laterally (left or right), never backward. If grabbed, you’re dead — no exceptions.
  • Arena-wide Light Pillars: At ~25% HP, Radahn flies to the center and floods the arena with golden light pillars. Run to the very edge of the arena — the pillars have a dead zone near the boundary walls. Use this window to heal and rebuff.

Essential NPC Questlines (No Spoilers)

Several NPC questlines directly affect boss fights and DLC endings. Here are the ones you should not miss:

Igon — The Broken Drake Warrior

Found near the Pillar Path Waypoint in Gravesite Plain, then again at the foot of Jagged Peak. Completing his questline lets you summon him for the Bayle fight. His dialogue during the battle is some of the best voice acting in the entire DLC. Do not kill Bayle without summoning Igon.

Thiollier & St. Trina

Thiollier’s questline runs through the Stone Coffin Fissure and unlocks an alternate interaction during the final boss. Repeatedly drink St. Trina’s nectar (4–5 times) to advance the quest to its conclusion.

Count Ymir — Mother of Fingers

Found in the Cathedral of Manus Metyr. Ymir teaches new sorceries and his questline unlocks the Metyr boss fight plus the Fleeting Microcosm sorcery — one of the strongest spells in the DLC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What level should I be before starting the DLC?

The recommended level is 120–150 with a fully upgraded weapon (+25 / +10) and at least 12 flasks at +12. More important than raw level is Vigor — you want 55–60 Vigor minimum, as many DLC enemies can two-shot players below 50 Vigor even at high blessing levels.

Q: Does Scadutree Blessing carry over to NG+?

Yes and no. Your blessing level resets to 0 when entering NG+ and you must recollect all fragments. However, any Scadutree Fragments and Revered Spirit Ashes in your inventory carry over — you can immediately use them at a site of grace to reach your previous blessing level without re-exploring.

Q: Can I still access the base game after starting the DLC?

Yes. The DLC is accessed through Mohgwyn Palace but functions as a separate map. You can freely fast-travel between the Lands Between and the Realm of Shadow at any time. DLC progress does not lock you out of any base-game content.

Q: What’s the best Spirit Ash for DLC bosses?

Mimic Tear remains the top choice for most bosses because it copies your build, buffs, and consumables. For Radahn specifically, Black Knife Tiche is arguably better due to her high evasion and Destined Death effect. Remember to upgrade your Revered Spirit Ash blessing at sites of grace to boost summon survivability.

Summary

The Realm of Shadow is FromSoftware’s most ambitious expansion to date, offering 40+ hours of content that rivals the base game in quality and exceeds it in difficulty. Your path to victory is clear: prioritize Scadutree Fragments above all else, aim for blessing level 12–14 before each remembrance boss, run a Blasphemous Blade Faith build for maximum sustain, and remember the golden rule of Radahn Phase 2 — dodge into the light beams. With this guide’s strategies, even the Consort of Miquella will fall. Now go forth, Tarnished, and claim the power of the shadow.
