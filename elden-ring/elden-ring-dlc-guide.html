II. The Boss Gauntlet

1. Divine Beast Dancing Lion (Belurat)

A three-phase elemental nightmare. Stay near the arena edge, roll diagonally through attacks. Lightning/Bleed weapons excel. Punish the long recovery after slam attacks.

2. Rellana, Twin Moon Knight (Castle Ensis)

Dual-wielding knight with sweeping combos. Dodge toward her to get behind the guard. When she splits into two in phase 2, focus one clone at a time.

3. Messmer the Impaler (Shadow Keep)

Aggressive serpent-lord with grab attacks and fire explosions. Roll into his combos, not away. Phase 2 adds serpent spawns—kill them immediately. Fire resistance and Bleed weapons are your best friends.

4-9. Mid-Bosses

Golden Hippopotamus (Shadow Keep entrance), Putrescent Knight (Stone Coffin Fissure), Romina Saint of the Bud (Rauh), Scadutree Avatar, Commander Gaius, and Bayle the Dread each demand specific elemental resistances and positioning. Bring Scarlet Rot boluses, Fire resistance, and patience.

10. Promised Consort Radahn (Enir-Ilim) — FINAL BOSS

Vigor 60 minimum. Maximize Holy resistance (Golden Braid talisman). Use ALL summons.

Phase 1: Dodge the Meteor Dive opening. Roll toward his multi-hit combos. Punish with gravity ashes of war.

Phase 2 (Radahn + Miquella): Sprint sideways during Holy Bombardment. Melee hits now explode with holy after-effects—dodge the sword, then roll away from the ground glow. Avoid the Holy Hug grab at all costs (second grab is instant death).
