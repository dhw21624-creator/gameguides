Black Myth Wukong DLC Hidden Bosses Guide 2026: Beat Wuxiang Mile, Crimson Demon Lord & Nezha

Hidden Boss #1: Wuxiang Mile (无相弥勒) — Three-Phase Nightmare

Wuxiang Mile is the gatekeeper to the true ending. Defeating this boss requires adapting to three radically different combat modes. Each phase tests a distinct skill set: timing, positioning, and burst damage.

PHASE 1 Mortal Flesh — Physical Frenzy

HP Range: 100% – 70% | Threat: Rapid combos with short telegraphs

Wuxiang Mile fights in human form with blistering melee chains. The core threat is the Arhat Fist (罗汉拳) three-hit combo: the third strike is a red-glint attack that must be countered with Rock Solid (铜头铁臂) for a perfect See-Through. Failing the counter results in a stun-lock and massive damage.

  1. Maintain mid-range to bait the Arhat Fist sequence
  2. The moment both fists clasp and emit golden light, cast Immobilize — this is the optimal stagger window
  3. Follow immobilization with a full charged heavy smash combo to break the first health bar quickly
  4. Never greed for extra hits after the red-glint wind-up; the punish window is extremely tight

PHASE 2 Dharma Manifestation — Magic Bombardment

HP Range: 70% – 30% | Threat: Full-screen AoE shockwaves

At 70% HP, Wuxiang Mile grows to colossal size. A giant golden bell shockwave covers the entire arena during the transition — you must use Cloud Step (聚形散气) to leap above it or face instant death. Key attacks include Lotus Descent (tracking floor explosions) and Golden Light Pervasion (360-degree beam sweep).

  1. Exploit Cloud Step's extended i-frames to dodge the initial transition blast
  2. Stay mobile — the Lotus Descent explosions track your last position; keep strafing
  3. When Golden Light Pervasion begins, sprint to the arena edge and time your dodge through the beam
  4. Punish during the 4-second recovery after each major AoE attack with light combos to build focus

PHASE 3 Void Manifestation — Chaos Incarnate

HP Range: 30% – 0% | Threat: Uninterruptible charge attacks

In the final phase, Wuxiang Mile enters a chaotic energy form. It periodically channels a devastating charge-up attack with no visible interrupt window — but the Eye of Chaos (混沌之眼) spirit skill is the exception. Activating "Void Gaze" during the charge wind-up stuns the boss for 3 seconds and cancels the attack entirely. Without this spirit equipped, this phase is borderline impossible to clear hitless.

  1. Conserve Eye of Chaos charges exclusively for the charge-up interrupt
  2. After the stun lands, unleash your heaviest combo — Chrysanthemum Gourd buff + charged heavy smash
  3. Watch for the desperation pattern under 10% HP: rapid teleport strikes that require perfect dodge timing

Hidden Boss #2: Crimson Demon Lord (赤炎魔君) — Nether Swamp Gatekeeper

Found in the Nether Swamp (幽冥沼泽) zone in the far southeast, the Crimson Demon Lord is hidden behind an environmental puzzle that many players miss. This boss boasts extreme fire resistance, rendering fire-based spells and transformations nearly useless.

How to Access the Crimson Demon Lord

The Nether Swamp features complex environmental interactivity. To open the teleport gate to the boss arena, you must cast Immobilize (定身术) on specific dead trees marked by dark-purple glowing flora. These trees are scattered across three locations in the swamp. Once all three are immobilized, a hidden portal activates in the zone center.

While exploring, pay close attention to anomalous purple plant clusters — they mark hidden treasure chests and trigger points. Equip Night Pearl or upgrade your Night Vision talent to navigate the low-light cave sections safely, as shadow ambushers lurk in the darkness.

Combat Strategy

Element: Fire (extreme resistance) | Weakness: High stagger from Smash Stance

The boss spams wide-area fire pillars. Conventional damage routes fail because of its near-immunity to fire. The winning approach is Smash Stance (劈棍势) for high poise-breaking strikes:

  1. Use Smash Stance exclusively — Pillar and Thrust stances lack the stagger potential needed here
  2. Watch the boss's arm raise animation — this is the trigger moment for See-Through counters
  3. Counter successfully to accumulate massive focus, then unleash charged heavy smashes
  4. Avoid standing in lingering fire zones; the DoT stacks rapidly and ignores defense

Hidden Boss #3: Nezha (哪吒) — Wind Tamer's Bane

Nezha appears as the final hidden encounter before the true ending path convergence. This celestial warrior fights with devastating speed and multi-hit spear combos that punish passive play. However, Nezha carries a specific vulnerability that transforms the fight from a war of attrition into a manageable duel.

Weakness: Wind Tamer Spirit (定风珠)

Nezha's Wind Fire Wheels grant him hyper-armor during his spear dance sequences. The Wind Tamer (定风珠) vessel spirit skill directly counters this — activating it strips Nezha of his wind aura for 8 seconds, removing his hyper-armor and applying a 15% damage vulnerability debuff. Cycle this with Immobilize to create alternating damage windows.

  1. Open with Wind Tamer to strip the wind aura immediately
  2. During the 8-second vulnerability window, apply Immobilize and execute a full combo
  3. When Wind Tamer is on cooldown, play defensively — dodge sideways through spear thrusts
  4. Repeat the Wind Tamer → Immobilize cycle; Nezha falls in roughly three rotations with proper gear
