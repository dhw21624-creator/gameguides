I. Combat Fundamentals

Most bosses telegraph their attacks with large, obvious wind-ups. Your core rhythm: Dodge → Punish Recovery → Dodge Again. Wukong's Transformations and Stun Spells create the largest damage windows—use them, don't hoard them.

II. Chapter 1 Boss Dossier

Black Bear Guai — Guardian of the Mountain

Threat Level: High | Element: Fire

His flame-enhanced attacks will incinerate the unprepared. The Flame Ward Talisman, obtained from the Jinchi Elder encounter, reduces fire damage significantly and creates a temporary safe zone during his fire storm phase.

Strategy: Stay at mid-range to bait his charge attack, dodge sideways, then punish his 3-second recovery. When he rears up for the flame sweep, activate Flame Ward immediately.

Baiwu Xianshi (White-Clad Swordsman) — Two-Phase Duel

Threat Level: Very High | Element: Water

Phase 1 is manageable. Phase 2 turns the arena into a water-soaked battlefield where his attacks chain faster and hit wider. Dodge sideways through his multi-hit water combos—back-dodging gets you caught in the follow-up. After every big slam attack, he pauses for a full 4 seconds. That's your window.

III. Hidden Bosses & Secret Encounters

The mountain holds secrets for those who explore. Ring the three bells scattered across Black Wind Mountain to unlock a hidden boss encounter with unique loot and critical lore. Environmental interactions—pulling levers, destroying altars, lighting braziers—often trigger optional fights that drop build-defining equipment.

IV. Advanced Combos: The Flaming Frenzy

Against the Fire Ape Huo Tian Huo Yuan, this sequence deals devastating damage:

  1. Wait & Bait: Let the ape leap and slam the ground
  2. Stun Spell: Cast as it lands—creates a ~6-second stun window
  3. Flame Eyes: Activate immediately to break its armor
  4. Heavy Attack Chain: Unleash your full combo—up to 1.7x critical multiplier on exposed weak points
