Genshin Impact Version 7.1 "A Rekviem for the Underworld" Guide

Category: Version Guide • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: Genshin Impact

Genshin Impact Version 7.1, "A Rekviem for the Underworld", launches on September 23, 2026, directly continuing the story threads opened by Version 7.0's Snezhnaya release on August 12. The patch brings two new limited 5-star characters, a brand-new reaction mechanic, and the game's next major boss encounter. This guide covers everything you need before the version drops: who to pull, how Star Diffusion changes team building, and what the new boss demands.

The Story: A Rekviem for the Underworld

Version 7.1 advances the Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya storyline that began in Version 7.0. The new chapter deepens the region's narrative and leads directly into the Odin boss encounter that caps the patch. If you skipped parts of 7.0, the story content in 7.1 assumes you know the region's factions and key characters, so clear the previous archon quest line before starting this one.

  • Complete 7.0's main quest first: 7.1 continues directly from its ending.
  • Bring a balanced team to the story boss: Odin's encounter will likely have mechanics that punish mono-element teams.
  • Collect region materials early: both new characters will need Nod-Krai/Snezhnaya materials to ascend.

New Characters: Odette and Alyosha

Odette and Alyosha are the two limited 5-stars of Version 7.1. Their exact roles define the patch's team-building conversation, and both are worth evaluating against your existing roster before spending primogems.

CharacterRoleTeam Fit
OdetteFeatured 5-star of the first halfStrong synergy with Star Diffusion teams
AlyoshaFeatured 5-star of the second halfFills a distinct damage or utility niche

The golden rule still applies: pull for the character that fits the teams you already play. If Star Diffusion becomes the patch's strongest mechanic, the character that enables it in your roster is the better pull.

Star Diffusion: The New Reaction

Star Diffusion is Version 7.1's headline mechanic. It changes how element application and reaction order work, which means several established team templates need re-evaluation. The reaction rewards careful elemental rotation rather than spamming one element, so teams built around reaction triggers gain the most.

  1. Re-learn your rotation: application order now matters in new ways.
  2. Test your current teams: some old comps lose value, while others gain a new trigger.
  3. Look for Star Diffusion enablers: characters with consistent off-field application are the patch's hidden MVPs.

Version 7.1 splits its banners across two halves. Odette headlines the first half alongside a rerun slot, and Alyosha takes the second half. Plan around confirmed income: the free primogems from story, exploration and events in 7.1 should cover most players' one-5-star budget for the patch.

New Artifact Sets and Best Teams

Version 7.1 adds new artifact sets tuned to the patch's characters and the Star Diffusion mechanic. Before farming, check whether the new sets beat your existing pieces; farming a new domain is only worth it if the set is a real upgrade for the characters you actually use.

  • Odette teams: build around her Star Diffusion interaction; she rewards consistent elemental rotation.
  • Alyosha teams: use him in the comp where his role replaces your current weakest slot.
  • Patch-best comps: wait for the first-week data before investing resin in new sets.
