Genshin Impact is a gacha game, but it's a predictable one. Banner schedules follow patterns (archons rerun roughly every 6-8 patches, new region characters arrive in .0-.3 patches), and Primogem income per patch is surprisingly consistent. Banner Schedule Patterns

While Hoyoverse doesn't publish a banner roadmap, community observation reveals consistent patterns:

  • New characters: Debut in the patch they're introduced. Most patches feature at least one new 5-star.
  • Archon reruns: Archons (Venti, Zhongli, Raiden, Nahida, Furina) rerun approximately every 6-10 patches after their last appearance. They tend to rerun near major story updates.
  • Regional reruns: When a new region launches (x.0), characters from the PREVIOUS region often rerun in early patches (x.1-x.3).
  • Lantern Rite (Chinese New Year): Always features Liyue character reruns + a free 4-star Liyue character selector.
  • Anniversary (September): Typically features popular reruns and bonus rewards.

How to predict reruns: Track the "days since last banner" for each limited 5-star on community sites (paimon.moe). Characters with the longest absence are most likely to rerun soon. Wriothesley and Shenhe famously went 400+ days without a rerun — these outliers happen, so don't bet everything on patterns.

Creating a Pull Plan

Here's a step-by-step method to plan your wishes:

  1. List your must-have characters: Be honest — which 2-3 characters do you absolutely want? Ignore "nice to have" for now.
  2. Estimate when they'll appear: Use the banner patterns above. New character? Next patch. Archon rerun? Check days-since-last-banner. Mark them on a calendar.
  3. Calculate wishes needed: Budget 160 wishes per character (lose 50/50 + guarantee). Add 210 if you want their weapon. This is your "safe" budget.
  4. Count patches between now and target: Multiply by your patch income (50 F2P, 75 Welkin, 85 Welkin+BP). Can you reach the target? If not, you need to skip someone or open the wallet.
  5. Track your pity: Know your current pity counter and 50/50 status at all times. This directly affects how many wishes you need.

The Discipline Mindset

The hardest part of Primogem planning isn't math — it's psychology. Gacha games exploit FOMO (fear of missing out). Every new character looks amazing in the trial run. Every banner seems "too good to skip." Here's how to stay disciplined:

  • Wait until the last week of a banner: Impulse pulling on day 1 of a banner is how you waste Primogems. Wait 2-3 weeks. Watch reviews. Play the trial multiple times. If you still want them, pull. If the hype fades, you saved your Primogems.
  • Don't "build pity": Pulling on a banner you don't want to increase pity is gambling with your guarantee. An early 5-star wastes your Primogems and your pity. Just save.
  • Convert Primogems to Fates immediately: When you hit 160 Primogems, convert to an Intertwined Fate. This creates a psychological barrier — it's easier to impulse-spend "Primogems" than "Fates I've been saving." Seeing a stack of 100+ Fates reinforces your goal.
  • Skip weapon banner: Unless you're a committed spender or have saved specifically for it, the weapon banner is a Primogem sink. Characters > weapons. A new character opens team options. A weapon makes numbers bigger. Choose characters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many Primogems can I get in one patch as F2P?

A: Roughly 50-60 wishes worth (8,000-9,600 Primogems) in a standard patch. Major expansion patches (x.0) with new regions: 70-85 wishes. Dead patches with minimal events: 40-50 wishes. The average over a full version (6 patches, ~9 months) is roughly 55-60 wishes per patch.

Q: Is it realistic to get every character as F2P?

A: No. Genshin releases roughly 8-10 new 5-star characters per year. A F2P player earns enough to guarantee 3-4 characters per year (assuming losing 50/50 half the time). You cannot get everyone. Pick your must-haves and be at peace with skipping the rest.

Q: Should I pull for constellations or save for new characters?

A: New characters almost always. A C0 new character expands your roster and team options. A constellation makes one character slightly stronger. The only constellations widely considered worth it for low spenders are game-changing C1/C2 spikes (Raiden C2, Furina C2, Nahida C2, Hu Tao C1). Even then, two new C0 characters usually provide more account value than one C2.

Q: What if I lose 50/50 on a character I saved months for?

A: This is exactly why you budget 160 wishes instead of 80. If you saved 160 and lose at 75, you have 85 wishes left — enough to reach soft pity again and guarantee the character. If you only saved 80 and lose, the banner ends before you can reach the guarantee. Budget for the worst case every time.

Q: How do I track multiple character goals at once?

A: Create a simple spreadsheet. Columns: Character Name, Estimated Rerun Patch, Wishes Needed (160), Wishes Saved So Far, Patches Until Target, Wishes Per Patch, Shortfall. Update it at the start of every patch. A visual reminder of your goals makes impulse-pulling much harder to justify.
