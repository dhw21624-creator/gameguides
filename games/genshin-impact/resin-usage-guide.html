Original Resin is Genshin Impact's most precious resource and the #1 bottleneck for account progression. You get 180 Resin per day (replenishing at 1 per 8 minutes). Wasting it is the single biggest mistake new players make — and the single easiest thing to fix. This guide covers exactly how to spend Resin at every stage of the game.

How the Resin System Works

Original Resin caps at 160 (displayed on the map screen). It regenerates at 1 per 8 minutes — meaning 180 per 24 hours if you never cap. Key mechanics:

  • Condensed Resin: Craft at an Alchemy table using 40 Original Resin + 1 Crystal Core. Stores Resin for later use. Doubles domain/ley line rewards (but not boss drops). You can hold up to 5 Condensed Resin at a time.
  • Fragile Resin: Consumable item (from Battle Pass, Adventure Rank rewards, events) that restores 60 Original Resin instantly. These are extremely valuable — do not use them before AR 45.
  • Transient Resin: Buy from Tubby in the Serenitea Pot (Teapot shop) for Realm Currency. Restores 60 Resin, expires after 2 weeks. Buy this every week — it's essentially free Resin.
Never let your Resin cap at 160. Every minute spent at the cap is Resin you'll never get back. If you can't play, log in for 2 minutes and craft Condensed Resin to bank it for later.

Resin Priority by Adventure Rank

AR 1-25: Just Progress

Don't overthink it. Spend Resin on whatever you need to progress: world bosses for character ascension, ley lines if you're short on XP books. The drop rates at low world levels are bad — don't farm for artifacts or talent books aggressively. Focus on Adventure Rank progression (quests, exploration, daily commissions).

AR 25-40: Build Your Core Team

Pick 4 characters (ideally 2 DPS + 2 supports) and invest Resin into: world bosses for their ascension, talent domains to get key talents to Level 6, weapon ascension domains, and ley lines as needed. Do NOT farm artifact domains yet. Use artifacts from world bosses and chests until AR 45.

AR 40-44: Pre-Farm for AR 45

This is the awkward phase. Talent domains now drop purple books more consistently — get your important talents to 6-8. World bosses drop more ascension materials. Stockpile Mora and XP books. You can start doing a few artifact domain runs, but prioritize guaranteed upgrades (talents, weapons, levels) over RNG artifacts.

AR 45+: The Real Game Begins

At AR 45, the highest-level artifact domains unlock — guaranteeing at least one 5-star artifact per 20-Resin run. This is when you start artifact farming and when you should start using your stockpiled Fragile Resin. Priority shift: spend 60-70% of daily Resin on artifacts for your main DPS first, then supports. Continue weekly bosses (3 discounted claims are always worth it). Talent domains on your characters' book days. World bosses as needed for new characters.

AR 45 Artifact Strategy: Farm the domain that benefits MULTIPLE characters on your account. Emblem of Severed Fate / Shimenawa's Reminiscence (Inazuma) is the single most Resin-efficient domain in the game — Emblem is BiS for Xiangling, Xingqiu, Yelan, Raiden, Beidou, and many others. Deepwood Memories / Gilded Dreams (Sumeru) is #2 for Dendro teams.

Fragile Resin: The Most Common Waste

The #1 piece of advice every Genshin veteran gives: save all Fragile Resin until AR 45.

Why? Pre-AR 45 artifact domains don't guarantee 5-star artifacts. Using Fragile Resin before AR 45 gets you 4-star artifacts that you'll replace within a week of hitting AR 45. That's permanently wasted potential. A typical player accumulates 40-60 Fragile Resin by AR 45 from Adventure Rank rewards, Battle Pass, and quests. At AR 45, that's 2400-3600 extra Resin — enough to fully gear 2-3 characters with decent artifacts right away.

Condensed Resin Explained

Condensed Resin doubles your rewards from domains and ley lines (but not world bosses or weekly bosses). Crafting cost: 40 Original Resin + 1 Crystal Core. You can hold up to 5 at a time.

When to craft Condensed:

  • When your Resin is about to cap and you can't play right now (craft 1-3 and log off)
  • On days when your character's talent/weapon books are not available (craft today, use on the right day)
  • When you want to speed-run artifact domains (use 5 Condensed + 160 Original = 9 runs in one session)

Crystal Cores come from Crystalflies — catch them in the open world. Good farming spots: Windrise tree (Mondstadt), Guyun Stone Forest (Liyue), underground waypoints in Sumeru desert. Sayu's passive makes Crystalflies not flee, making farming trivial.

Common Resin Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Using Fragile Resin before AR 45: The single worst mistake. You're trading limited resources for temporary artifacts you'll scrap. Save every Fragile Resin.
  2. Farming artifacts before AR 45: Same reason. The highest-level domain (guaranteed 5-star) unlocks at AR 45. Pre-AR 45 artifact farming is gambling with worse odds.
  3. Spreading Resin too thin: Building 8 characters simultaneously means nobody is built. Focus Resin on one team of 4 until they can clear content, then expand. One strong team > four half-built teams.
  4. Ignoring weekly bosses: The 30-Resin discount for the first 3 claims is the best value in the game. You get ascension gems, boss materials, Dream Solvent, and artifact fodder. Always claim 3 per week even if you don't need the materials right now.
  5. Farming the wrong artifact domain: Don't farm a domain for ONE character. Farm domains where the OTHER set in the domain is also useful. Emblem/Shimenawa and Deepwood/Gilded are the gold standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Should I use Primogems to refresh Resin?

A: Almost never. The first daily refresh costs 50 Primogems for 60 Resin. For free-to-play and low-spenders, Primogems are for wishes — converting them to Resin is extremely inefficient. Light spenders (Welkin + BP) might do one refresh daily if they value faster progression, but it adds up to 1500 Primogems per month. Whales aside, save your Primogems.

Q: How much Resin does it take to build one character?

A: NEEDS VERIFICATION for exact numbers, but rough estimates: Level 1→80 ascension bosses: ~800-1000 Resin. Talents 1→8: ~600-800 Resin (assuming average book drops). Artifacts: completely RNG-dependent, can range from 1000 to 5000+ Resin for a good set. Total per character: 2000-6000+ Resin. This is why spreading Resin thin is disastrous — you can build one character well or three characters poorly.

Q: Is the Battle Pass worth it for the Fragile Resin?

A: The Battle Pass gives Fragile Resin, but its real value is the 4-star weapon (Serpent Spine, Deathmatch, etc.) and the massive pile of Mora, XP books, and talent books. If you value the weapon and resources, the BP is excellent. Don't buy it just for Fragile Resin.

Q: Should I farm artifacts or talents first for a new character?

A: Talents first. Artifacts are RNG — you can spend 1000 Resin and get nothing usable. Talent levels are guaranteed power. Get important talents to 6-8 (or 9-10 for key skills) before deep-diving into artifact farming. Guaranteed upgrades > RNG upgrades.

Q: Can I play Genshin without worrying about Resin optimization?

A: Yes, absolutely. If you're playing for the story, exploration, and casual enjoyment, Resin optimization barely matters. You can clear all story content and most events without optimized artifacts or maxed talents. This guide is for players who want to tackle Spiral Abyss, build multiple characters efficiently, or avoid the frustration of being Resin-gated on progression.
