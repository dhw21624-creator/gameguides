Honkai Star Rail 4.2 "So Laughed the Masses" Guide: Silver Wolf LV.999, Evanescia & Seafeld City

Category: Version Guide • Updated: 2026-10-09 • Game: Honkai: Star Rail

Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.2, "So Laughed the Masses," brings the two most anticipated Elation-path additions of the year: the limited 5-star Silver Wolf LV.999 and Evanescia, alongside a free Elation Trailblazer. Between the new banners, the Seafeld City map, and a brand-new endgame boss, 4.2 is a version built for both collectors and meta chasers. This guide breaks down who to pull, what to expect from each phase, and where the new content fits into your rotation.

Quick take: Pull Silver Wolf LV.999 in Phase 1 if you want the strongest Elation support; grab Evanescia in Phase 2 for a Physical Elation carry. Claim the free Trailblazer: Elation regardless — it is a zero-cost addition to any roster.

The New 5-Stars: Silver Wolf LV.999 & Evanescia

Version 4.2's headline units both sit on the Elation path, but fill different roles:

  • Silver Wolf LV.999 (Imaginary, Elation) — the upgraded, anniversary-flavoured take on the classic Silver Wolf. As an Elation unit she plays as a premium support/offensive hybrid, and her kit is tuned to supercharge the path's core mechanics.
  • Evanescia (Physical, Elation) — the patch's damage carry, built around Physical break-style uptime with Elation's signature bonus chains.

Both are strong in their lanes: Silver Wolf LV.999 is the higher-value account-wide pick because Elation support slots into more teams, while Evanescia is the ceiling for Physical-Elation teams specifically.

Free Unit: Trailblazer (Elation, Lightning)

Alongside the banners, 4.2 gives every player a free Trailblazer: Elation (Lightning). This is a significant freebie — a full Elation-path Trailblazer costs nothing and fills a genuine gap in most rosters. Even if you skip both limited banners, the free unit plus the new light cones below give F2P players a complete 4.2 experience.

Three new light cones arrive with the version: Welcome to the Cosmic City, Until the Flowers Bloom Again, and Tomorrow Together. The first is the signature cone for the 4.2 limited units; the other two cover support and generalist niches. If you are budget-conscious, the free Trailblazer plus the Coterie reruns are the highest-value targets — the rerun pool is unusually stacked this version.

Seafeld City & The New Endgame Boss

Version 4.2 opens Seafeld City as a new explorable location, continuing the version's city-focused storytelling with new areas, side content, and collectibles. On the combat side, the endgame gains the "Paramount Bliss Inverted: Illwish Archlotus" boss, featured in the new endgame content cycle. The fight leans on Elation mechanics — expect it to reward teams that chain the path's bonuses rather than raw single-target damage. It is the perfect proving ground for the version's new units and the free Trailblazer alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When does Version 4.2 release?

A: Version 4.2 "So Laughed the Masses" follows the standard 6-week cadence after 4.1's end date; check the in-game notice for the exact local time.

Q: Is Silver Wolf LV.999 worth pulling over Evanescia?

A: For account value, yes — an Elation support slots into more teams. For Physical-Elation damage, Evanescia is the pick.

Q: How do I get the free Elation Trailblazer?

A: It is a free-obtainable reward in Version 4.2; unlock it through the version's main content path.

Q: Where is the Illwish Archlotus boss found?

A: It is featured in Version 4.2's endgame content cycle, tied to the new Seafeld City area.

Conclusion

Honkai: Star Rail 4.2 delivers the full package: a must-have Elation support in Silver Wolf LV.999, a strong Physical carry in Evanescia, a genuinely useful free Trailblazer, a stacked rerun pool, and a new endgame boss to test it all. Pull by role priority, grab the free unit first, and let Seafeld City's new content fill the next six weeks.
