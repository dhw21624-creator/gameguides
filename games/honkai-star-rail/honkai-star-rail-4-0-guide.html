Honkai Star Rail Version 4.0 Guide

Category: Version Guide • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: Honkai: Star Rail

Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.0 is a milestone release. It concludes the Amphoreus storyline that has carried the game since version 3.0, restructures how several Paths interact with team composition, and rebalances the endgame modes around those changes. This guide covers what actually matters for your account: which new characters are worth pulling, how the Path adjustments change existing teams, and how to spend the version's generous free rewards without wasting them.

Path Adjustments and What They Mean

The headline systemic change in 4.0 is that several Paths now provide team-wide benefits rather than only amplifying their own damage. That single change is more important than any individual character release, because it makes support characters in those Paths valuable for teams they previously did not fit.

  • Support Paths gained value: characters in these Paths now contribute to teams they were previously locked out of.
  • Pure damage Paths stayed linear: no change to how their scaling works, so their relative value dropped slightly.
  • Sustain Paths improved mid-fight: survivability scaling makes longer endgame fights more forgiving.
Account advice: before pulling a new character, re-check whether your existing support roster now fits a team you already own. Version 4.0 makes several previously sidelined characters relevant again.

Endgame Rebalance

The endgame modes in 4.0 reward broader rosters rather than a single hyper-invested team. If you have been building one team to maximum investment, the new rotation will punish you for the gaps in your roster.

  1. Build three functional teams, not one perfect team. The mode rotation now demands breadth.
  2. Level supports before DPS sidegrades. A team-wide buffer improves every team you own.
  3. Check element coverage. Missing an entire element is now a harder wall than missing a specific character.
  4. Do not chase every new DPS. Pull only when the character unlocks a team you can already support.

Spending the Free Rewards

  • Bank pulls for the support banner: support characters retain value across versions; damage dealers rotate.
  • Do not spread resources across five characters: focus investment into two teams and one support.
  • Clear the story first: the Amphoreus finale grants permanent rewards you would otherwise grind for later.
  • Save a reserve: version 4.1 typically brings an account-defining character, and impulse pulling in 4.0 is the most common regret.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to rebuild my existing teams for 4.0?

A: No complete rebuild. Re-check whether the Path changes make a sidelined support character a better fit than your current fourth slot.

Q: Is the new damage dealer worth pulling without a matching support?

A: Usually not. New archetypes in 4.0 are designed to work with specific supports, so a damage dealer alone underperforms.

Q: Are the free rewards enough for a guaranteed limited character?

A: The version grants a substantial pull economy, but treat guarantees as requiring a saved reserve rather than assuming the version alone covers it.

Q: What investment order makes sense for a returning player?

A: Clear the new story, level one team-wide support, then build out element coverage for the endgame rotation.

Conclusion

Version 4.0 rewards breadth over depth. The Path adjustments make support characters more universally useful, the endgame rotation punishes single-team accounts, and the new damage dealers only shine if you can support them. Bank your pulls for a team-wide support, level your existing supports, and treat the Amphoreus finale as the first thing to finish in this version.
