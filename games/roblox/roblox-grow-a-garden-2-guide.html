Roblox Grow a Garden 2 Guide: Farming, Night Raids & Guilds

Category: Game Guide • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: Roblox

Grow a Garden 2 (GaG2) is the sequel to Strawberreh Squad's breakout farming game, and it is not a save transfer. The core plant-and-sell loop returns, but the sequel adds free night raids, guilds, and an entirely new economy on top of it. This guide treats GaG2 as the new game it is: the best starting crops, the server choice that decides your income, and the night-cycle rules that protect your garden from thieves.

The most common mistake new players make is importing habits from the original game. Crops, prices and unlock order have all been adjusted, so the efficient path in GaG2 starts from a clean slate.

Best Starting Crops

Your first purchases should be Carrot and Strawberry. Both are cheap and grow quickly, which matters in the early game when you are trying to establish a sell cycle. Strawberry's higher unit price per growth time makes it the better scaling pick once your garden can handle the volume.

Early rule: replant immediately after every harvest. Idle plots are lost income, and in the first hour of GaG2 that income is the whole game.

Public Servers, Friend Boosts and Thieves

Server choice is the single biggest income decision in GaG2. Public servers provide the friend boost, which raises your sell income when friends are in the same server — but they also open your garden to theft. The trick is managing both sides of that trade.

  • Use the friend boost: play on public servers with friends to increase sale income.
  • Accept the theft risk: other players can take crops, so harvest on time.
  • Know the night rule: staying inside your own garden during the night cycle locks your garden and blocks thieves.

Night Raids and the Lock Rule

Night raids are free to join and reward active participation, but the night cycle also changes how your own garden behaves. If you remain inside your own garden during the night, it locks and thieves cannot touch it. That single decision — raid vs. stay home — is a meaningful economy choice every cycle.

  1. Stay home when your crops are close to harvest: a locked garden protects the exact income you cannot afford to lose.
  2. Raid when your plots are empty or early-growth: the reward is worth leaving the garden unlocked.
  3. Coordinate with guilds: guild raid parties earn more than solo raids in most cases.

Mutations, Sprinklers and Pets

The endgame income engine in GaG2 is mutation farming. Mutated crops can sell for millions of Sheckles, and the way to reach that reliably is to stack the tools that speed up growth and increase mutation chances: sprinklers and pets.

  • Sprinklers: automate watering, letting you maintain far more plots.
  • Pets: accelerate growth further and reduce manual attention.
  • Mutations: the real payout; a single mutation can outweigh an entire day of normal sales.
  • Guild research: several guild upgrades improve mutation odds for all members.

Guild Strategy

Join a guild early and contribute to shared goals. The progression bonuses and group raid rewards compound with the friend boost, and a good guild removes most of the early-game friction from tool and plot unlocks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Grow a Garden 2 a sequel or an update to the original?

A: It is a sequel with a rebuilt economy. Treat it as a new game; legacy strategies and prices do not carry over.

Q: What is the best first crop?

A: Carrot and Strawberry. They are cheap, grow fast, and Strawberry in particular gives the best early value per growth time.

Q: How do I stop thieves from taking my crops?

A: Stay inside your garden during the night cycle. A locked garden blocks thieves.

Q: How do I make millions of Sheckles?

A: Mutation farming with sprinklers and pets. A mutated crop can sell for millions, far more than a normal harvest.

Conclusion

GaG2 rewards players who respect its new economy. Start with Carrot and Strawberry, harvest on time, and treat the night cycle as a deliberate choice between raid rewards and a locked garden. Once the early loop is stable, mutation farming with sprinklers and pets turns a modest garden into a million-Sheckle operation.
