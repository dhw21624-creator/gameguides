Clash of Clans June 2026 Update Guide: Hero Journey, Ruin Witch & Bases

Category: Update Guide • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: Clash of Clans

The June 2026 Clash of Clans update is one of the most consequential of the year because it reworks how hero upgrades work. The Hero Journey system is no longer a permanent stat treadmill; hero upgrades now unlock temporary skill boosts during attacks, and the power you get is tied to how far your base has progressed. On top of that, the update adds the Ruin Witch hero and the Angry Spell. This guide explains the rework, the new tools, and how defence layouts have shifted to survive them.

The key change for attackers: hero power during an attack is now a temporary skill boost rather than a permanent stat, so when you attack matters as much as what you have upgraded. The strength of that boost is derived from your base's unlock progress, which makes Town Hall progression more valuable than it has ever been.

What the Rework Means for Upgrade Order

  • Prioritise base unlocks: the deeper your base progression, the stronger every hero skill boost becomes.
  • Time attacks around boosts: attack windows matter because the skill boost is temporary.
  • Plan one-time skill upgrades: each upgrade is a one-time decision, so spend it on the skill your attack style actually uses.
  • Coordinate with CWL: schedule hero-heavy upgrades for war windows, not just farm windows.

New Tools: Ruin Witch and Angry Spell

The update introduces the Ruin Witch hero (available at Town Hall 16) and the Angry Spell. Both reward players who adapt quickly: the Ruin Witch adds a new damage-and-control profile to TH16 attacks, while the Angry Spell is a flexible addition to spell factories that fits several army compositions.

Defence Layouts for the New Meta

The strongest post-update bases are the Goodbee-style layouts that funnel attackers while keeping the most valuable resources behind multiple layers of defence. Two named layouts stand out: the Rusty Buff base and the King Snore base. Both prioritise protecting the gold storage, elixir storage and dark elixir storage, and both add counter-attack depth for clan war defence.

  1. Protect storage first: the new hero economy means resource protection is the difference between keeping and losing progression.
  2. Use Goodbee funnel layouts: they force ground attackers through a single defended lane.
  3. Copy with your own tweaks: layout links are a starting point; adjust for your trap and wall levels.
  4. Test against the new armies: the Ruin Witch and Angry Spell change what attackers can do at TH16.

Resource and Book Priorities

  • Spend Hero Books and Hammers on Ruin Witch: the new hero is the highest-value TH16 upgrade this season.
  • Sequence defences: upgrade the defences that stop the armies you actually face before the ones that look strong on paper.
  • Bank dark elixir carefully: hero upgrades remain the biggest dark elixir sink in the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the Hero Journey rework change upgrading?

A: Upgrades now grant temporary skill boosts during attacks instead of permanent stats, and boost strength scales with your base's unlock progress.

Q: When can I unlock the Ruin Witch?

A: She is available at Town Hall 16 as part of the June 2026 update.

Q: What is the Angry Spell?

A> A new spell added in the June 2026 update that fits flexibly into several attack compositions.

Q: Which base layout should I use after the update?

A: Goodbee-style layouts such as the Rusty Buff or King Snore bases, adjusted for your trap and wall levels, with storage protection as the top priority.

Conclusion

The June 2026 update reframes hero progression around base unlocks and attack timing, which rewards planning over grinding. Push Town Hall progress, spend one-time skill upgrades carefully, unlock Ruin Witch at TH16, and adopt storage-first defence layouts to stay ahead of the new attack power.
