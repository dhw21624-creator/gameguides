Candy Crush Saga 2026 Events Guide

Category: Events • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: Candy Crush Saga

Candy Crush Saga's 2026 event calendar is built around constant rotation. Between the Sugar Drop series, the player-versus-player Sugar Rush races, and the freely adjustable team events, there is almost always an active source of boosters and rewards. This guide explains how the event ecosystem works, why certain combos stay efficient across every event, and how to spend event rewards on the levels where they actually matter.

The key change in 2026 is the removal of friction around team events: you can switch teams freely without penalty, so the optimal play is to join the team whose current goal matches the levels you plan to play anyway. Rewards accumulate passively while you clear your own board.

Sugar Rush: Races and Timing

Sugar Rush is the event with the clearest strategy: everyone races to be among the first to clear three newly released levels. The winning condition is speed, not score, so the moment a new level batch drops is the moment to start.

  • Play new levels on launch: early clears place far better than clean clears later.
  • Use boosters sparingly: save them for the third level, where the move budget tightens.
  • Do not replay for stars: finishing first matters more than a perfect score.

Combos That Carry Every Event

Whatever the event format, two combinations solve the majority of boards. The first is the colour bomb plus striped candy: detonating a colour bomb swaps every candy of one colour on the board and, when paired with a striped candy, also clears an entire row or column. The second is recognising which blocker type you are facing before you decide where to spend moves.

Efficiency Strategies

Event success is move economy. The fastest players plan several moves ahead, clear the most heavily blocked zone first, and spend their limited moves where they are most likely to trigger a cascade rather than where the biggest match is.

  1. Plan ahead: look two or three moves beyond the obvious match before you touch the board.
  2. Clear blocked zones first: the objective is usually buried behind the blockers, and unlocking it early redefines the rest of the level.
  3. Invest in cascade spots: a match at the bottom of the board creates new matches above it; a match near the top often does nothing.
  4. Spend rewards on choke levels: save boosters for levels whose difficulty comes from the layout, not the move count.

How to Spend Event Rewards

  • Bank colour bombs: they are the most flexible booster for event boards.
  • Do not waste boosters on easy levels: events hand out rewards for clearing anyway.
  • Use the team switch freely: no penalty means you should always be on the team matching your current level position.
  • Prioritise event-exclusive rewards: booster packs return every season; exclusive skins and themes do not.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is it worth switching teams mid-event?

A: Yes. Team events in 2026 have no switching penalty, so you should always join the team whose goal matches the levels you intend to play.

Q: What is the best combo for event speed?

A: The colour bomb plus striped candy. It clears an entire colour and a full row or column, which is the fastest way to hit spread-out objectives.

Q: Should I replay levels to improve event scores?

A: Only for Sugar Rush-style speed races. For collection events, clearing new levels is almost always more efficient than replaying old ones.

Q: How do I know which levels to spend boosters on?

A: Spend on levels where the difficulty comes from blockers and layout, not from a tight move count. Move-budget levels are usually solvable by retrying.

Conclusion

Candy Crush's 2026 events reward consistent play more than any single perfect run. Switch teams freely, race new levels the moment they drop, and lean on the colour bomb plus striped combination to convert limited moves into cascades. Keep booster spending focused on layout-heavy choke levels and the event calendar becomes a steady supply of free progression.
