Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 3 "Runners" Weapon Guide

Category: Weapon Guide • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: Fortnite

Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 3, "Runners", launched on June 6, 2026 with version 41.00, following the Shattered live event. The season pushes mobility harder than any previous one, and its new weapon pool reflects that: several weapons reward aggressive repositioning, while the Sprite system adds a layer of tactical choices you pick up from the map. This guide breaks down the weapon pool, the Sprite rarities, and the extraction-site economy that defines how the season is actually played.

The New Weapon Pool

The season's arsenal leans into burst damage and extendable range. The standout names are the Chaos Exploder Rifle, the Surgical Burst Rifle, and the shotgun family built around extending shots. Named and Mythic variants of existing guns also rotate into the pool through bosses.

Sprite Rarity Effects

Sprites are map pickups that grant passive effects based on rarity. Choosing which Sprite to hold is now a real decision rather than an afterthought.

Extraction Sites and the New Map

Extraction Sites are the season's core economy: contested landing points where teams secure valuable loot and leave before the storm closes. The new map places them at high-risk, high-reward positions, and the mobility-focused Battle Pass items make rotating between sites viable in a way it has not been in previous chapters.

  • Land near an Extraction Site: early loot density is the fastest way to a competitive loadout.
  • Leave before the third zone: extraction fights become unwinnable once the storm forces a choke.
  • Use Runners mobility items to escape contested sites: the season's movement tools exist precisely for this.

Bosses: Skeletor and Voidblade

Two bosses define the season's top-end loot. Skeletor and Voidblade carry the named and Mythic weapons worth contesting, but the fights pull the whole lobby, so arriving first is rarely the winning move.

  1. Do not rush the boss: let the first team engage and take the initial pressure.
  2. Third-party the kill: the boss loot matters more than the kill itself.
  3. Prepare extraction before looting: the best loot in the season is the loot you actually leave with.

Loadout Priorities

  • Carry one extending weapon: the Reacher or Focus shotgun covers the season's fight ranges.
  • Pair burst with a close-range option: the Surgical Burst Rifle struggles in box fights alone.
  • Prioritise mobility items over a third weapon: Runners favours positioning over pure firepower.
  • Match your Sprite to your role: aggressive players want Demon or Fire; rotators want Water or Ghost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best weapon in C7S3?

A: The Mythic variants from the bosses are strongest, but among the standard pool the Surgical Burst Rifle is the most reliable all-round pick.

Q: Which Sprite should I take?

A: Take Earth or Fire early for loot and survival; switch to Ghost or King for late-game fights.

Q: Are Extraction Sites worth contesting?

A: Yes, but time your exit. Extraction fights become unwinnable once the storm closes in.

Q: Should I fight Skeletor and Voidblade?

A: Contest them, but third-party. Their Mythic drops are season-defining, yet fighting them first usually means dying to the team behind you.

Conclusion

Runners is a season about movement and timing. Learn the new weapon pool, match your Sprite to your playstyle, and treat Extraction Sites as timed rewards rather than permanent claim zones. The teams that win in C7S3 are the ones that extract, not the ones that fight longest.
