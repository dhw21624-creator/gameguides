League of Legends Patch 26.17 Tier List

Category: Tier List • Updated: 2026-09-19 • Game: League of Legends

Patch 26.17 hit League of Legends on September 5, 2026, with a mix of Arena mode changes and champion balance that kept most of the established power structure intact. The result is a patch where the champions who dominated 26.16 remain strong, but a few niche picks climbed on the back of the data. This tier list is built around who is actually winning solo queue, with an emphasis on picks that are strong now and stay strong for the rest of the patch.

The Data Behind the Tier List

The most striking stat coming out of 26.16 carried directly into 26.17: Rammus posted a 55.9 percent jungle win rate on a low play rate. A high win rate with low play usually signals a sleeper pick — strong, but not yet discovered by the wider player base. If you want an easy entry into the jungle role this patch, Rammus is the least punished learning curve available.

  • Rammus: 55.9% jungle win rate, low pick rate — sleeper strong.
  • Skarner: maintained dominance across both 26.16 and 26.17, no nerfs landed.
  • Kennen: the buff pushed him into top/mid flex territory.
  • Yorick: continues to punish teams without a split-push answer.

Why Skarner Is the Version Answer

Skarner is the definition of a "version answer" champion: his kit is strong enough that he does not need to be first-picked, yet oppressive enough that letting him through is a mistake. His disruption, pick potential and team-fight presence cover the three things solo queue teams lack most: engage, peel and initiation. He remained untouched through the 26.16–26.17 window, which is why he tops the list.

  1. Pick him whenever he is open: his power does not depend on the enemy draft.
  2. Build for utility: his value comes from disruption, not raw damage.
  3. Force team fights: Skarner's initiation wins games that other junglers cannot start.

Arena Mode Changes

Patch 26.17 also touched Arena, and while the format changes do not affect Summoner's Rift ranking, they change which champions feel playable in the mode. The same philosophy applies: pick champions that create their own win conditions, and prioritise the ones that do not need a coordinated team to execute.

How to Climb With This Tier List

  • One-trick the meta: with Skarner open so often, learning him covers most drafts.
  • Use Rammus as a fallback: if Skarner is banned or picked, Rammus provides the same counter-engage identity with lower mechanical demand.
  • Flex Kennen: a top/mid flex keeps your draft unpredictable without adding champion pool size.
  • Ban the split-push counter: if you play Yorick, ban or pressure the champions that stop split pushes in your elo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best jungle pick in Patch 26.17?

A: Skarner is the version answer. If he is unavailable, Rammus is the strongest sleeper with a 55.9 percent win rate.

Q: Is Rammus actually that strong?

A: Yes. The 26.16 data showed 55.9 percent jungle win rate on a low pick rate, which is the classic profile of an undiscovered pick.

Q: What changed in 26.17 that matters?

A: Arena mode changes and champion balance. Most top picks from 26.16 remained strong, especially Skarner.

Q: Who is the best top laner?

A> Yorick for split-push pressure, Kennen for team-fight value with a top/mid flex option.

Conclusion

Patch 26.17 is a stability patch that rewards picking what the data already proved. Skarner remains the answer, Rammus is the sleeper, and Kennen and Yorick give top lane a choice between pressure and flexibility. Pick one, learn its matchups, and the patch becomes predictable.
