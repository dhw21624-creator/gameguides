The Witcher 3 Next-Gen: Best Builds, Death March & Gwent Master Guide

The Next-Gen update breathed new life into the Continent with ray tracing, photo mode, Netflix-inspired armor, and quality-of-life improvements. Whether you're tackling Death March or building the ultimate Gwent deck, this guide covers it all.

I. Death March Builds

Euphoria Combat/Alchemy (The Undying)

Core: Euphoria mutation + 4 decoctions (Water Hag, Ekimmara, Troll, Succubus). Each toxicity point adds 0.75% attack power. At max toxicity, you gain ~210% bonus damage while regenerating health faster than enemies can deal it. Skills: Acquired Tolerance, Heightened Tolerance, Synergy, Protective Coating, Resolve, Razor Focus. Gear: Ursine Grandmaster set (heavy armor = damage reduction). Enchant with Levity for Cat School technique.

Piercing Cold (Frost Mage)

Core: Piercing Cold mutation. Aard now has a 25% chance to instantly kill frozen enemies. Griffin Grandmaster set for double Sign intensity. Skills: Aard Sweep, Aard Intensity, Far-Reaching Aard, Delusion, Griffin School Techniques. Cast Aard, freeze everything, shatter with follow-up.

IV. Hidden Quests Worth Finding

Equine Phantoms (Blood and Wine): Near Dun Tynne Crossroads, talk to a hermit to start a quest where Roach talks. One of the funniest quests in the game.
The Tower Outta Nowheres: An island in Skellige with a tower that appears from another world. Defeat the golem for unique dialogue with a mage from another dimension.
