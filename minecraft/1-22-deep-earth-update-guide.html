1. Update Overview

Mojang's 1.22 update — The Deep Earth Update — adds the most substantial changes to cave exploration since the Caves & Cliffs update. Underground biomes now feature self-sustaining ecosystem loops, five new mob variants specific to different cave biome types, and a dedicated cave-specific advancement track separate from the existing achievement system. This is not just a content patch — it fundamentally transforms how veteran players approach underground exploration.

2. Underground Ecosystem Loops

The defining feature of the Deep Earth Update is the introduction of self-sustaining ecosystem loops within underground biomes. Each cave biome now has a food chain and resource cycle:

Ecosystem ComponentFunctionPlayer Interaction
Cave FungiBase of the food chain; spawns naturally in all deep biomesHarvest for new brewing ingredients (Glimmering Spores)
Grazing MobsFeed on cave fungi, attracting predatorsHunt for unique drops; observe to locate predator spawns
Predator MobsHunt grazers, drop high-value resourcesDefeat for rare crafting materials and advancement progress
Fungal Growth CyclePredator drops feed further fungal growthUse predator drops as bone-meal equivalent for cave fungi farms
Environmental ResourcesResin blocks, Corrupted Petals, new ore variantsMine for new building blocks and potion ingredients

This means that underground biomes are no longer static — they're living, breathing environments where mob behavior and resource availability shift based on the ecosystem's balance. Over-harvesting fungi in one area can cause grazers to migrate, which in turn affects predator spawns.

3. Five New Mob Variants

Each new mob variant is specific to a cave biome type. Here's the complete breakdown:

1. Glowfish — Crystal Caverns

Type: Passive / Ambient | Behavior: Schools swim through Crystal Caverns, emitting a dynamic mobile light source

The Glowfish is already being incorporated into base-lighting strategies by experienced players. Catch them with a water bucket and release them into your base's water features for a permanent, maintenance-free light source. When threatened, schools scatter and release a brief blinding flash — useful for escaping hostile mobs.

2. Deepstone Golem — Underground Ruins

Type: Neutral Guardian | Behavior: Patrols newly added underground ruins; passive unless you break ruin blocks

The Deepstone Golem is a towering stone construct that guards underground ruins — new structures filled with ancient loot. It's neutral by default, but mining any block within the ruins triggers its aggression. Its attacks deal massive knockback and can collapse ceilings, creating environmental hazards. Defeating it drops Deepstone Cores, used to craft the new Golem Armor Trim.

3. Cave Stalker — Fungal Forests

Type: Hostile | Behavior: Ambushes from ceiling fungi; drops onto players from above

The Cave Stalker is the primary predator in Fungal Forest biomes. It clings to ceiling fungi and drops onto unsuspecting players, dealing fall-damage-based bonus attacks. Listen for its distinctive chittering sound — that's your warning to look up. Ranged weapons are essential; melee players should carry a shield and bait its drop attack.

4. Shimmer Beetle — Crystal Caverns

Type: Passive | Behavior: Burrows through crystal deposits, leaving trails of glowstone dust

The Shimmer Beetle consumes crystal formations and excretes glowstone dust trails. Following these trails leads to rare crystal deposits and hidden caverns. They can be bred with amethyst shards, making them the first farmable source of glowstone dust in the underground.

5. Echo Bat — All Deep Biomes

Type: Ambient | Behavior: Flocks through deep caves; echolocation screech reveals nearby ores through walls

The Echo Bat is the most valuable exploration companion in the deep earth. Its periodic echolocation screech briefly outlines ore blocks through walls within a 20-block radius. Large flocks indicate rich ore deposits nearby. They're skittish — approach slowly or they scatter.

4. Cave Advancement Track

6. Optimal Exploration Strategy

For players diving into the Deep Earth for the first time, here's the recommended progression through the new content. Follow this path to maximize advancement unlocks while minimizing deaths.

  1. Phase 1: Crystal Caverns First. Start in Crystal Caverns (Y=-20 to -55). The Glowfish provides passive lighting, and Shimmer Beetle trails lead to valuable crystal deposits. Collect Glimmering Spores for the Potion of Illumination — it's the best light source for the darker biomes.
  2. Phase 2: Fungal Forests for Potions. Move to Fungal Forests once you have the Potion of Illumination. Harvest all 5 cave fungi types and collect Corrupted Petals. Brew Potions of Spell Warding — these reduce damage from Guardian lasers and Witch potions, making Underground Ruins much safer.
  3. Phase 3: Underground Ruins for Gear. With Spell Warding active, tackle Underground Ruins. The Deepstone Golem is the toughest challenge — use ranged attacks and avoid mining ruin blocks until you're ready to fight. The Golem Armor Trim is one of the best-looking trims in the game.
  4. Phase 4: Molten Depths Endgame. The Molten Depths are the final challenge. Bring Fire Resistance potions and a full set of enchanted gear. The Netherite scrap bonus here makes it the most efficient late-game farming location in the entire update.

Pro Tips

  • Breed Shimmer Beetles early: A farm of 8-10 beetles provides enough glowstone dust for permanent lighting in any base.
  • Listen for Echo Bats: Their echolocation is more reliable than strip mining for finding diamonds and ancient debris.
  • Don't over-harvest fungi: If you strip a Fungal Forest bare, the Cave Stalkers will migrate to neighboring biomes — including Crystal Caverns.
  • Deep Earth Champion reward: The +5 permanent light radius from completing all cave advancements is the single best quality-of-life upgrade in the game.
