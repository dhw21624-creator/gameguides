Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Y-level for diamonds in Minecraft 1.21?
Diamonds generate most frequently at Y=-58 in Minecraft 1.21. Mine at this level for the highest yield. Our ore distribution chart in the survival guide has the full breakdown.
How do I find an Ancient City?
Ancient Cities generate exclusively in Deep Dark biomes under mountains. Look for sculk blocks while mining below Y=0. The warden only spawns if you trigger sculk shriekers three times, so move carefully and place wool to block sound.
Is the 3-hour speedrun checklist realistic for beginners?
The checklist is designed as a structured goal, not an expectation. Most beginners should aim for reaching the Nether within the first hour. With practice, the 3-hour window becomes achievable. Our guide breaks each segment into sub-goals you can tackle at your own pace.
Are crafting recipes changed in Tricky Trials?
Yes — the 1.21 update added the Crafter block (automatic crafting with redstone), new copper block variants, and trial chamber loot. All recipes are covered in our survival guide.
