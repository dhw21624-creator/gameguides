FAQ

Q: What's the best Y-level for diamonds in Minecraft 1.21+?
Y=-58. Diamond ore generation peaks at the deepest levels. Avoid Y=-59 where bedrock starts spawning. Strip mining at Y=-58 with Fortune III pickaxe yields approximately 2.5 diamonds per minute of mining.
Q: How do I survive my first night?
Option 1 (Safe): Build a dirt hut (4x4x3 interior), place a door, add torches. Option 2 (Risky): Pillar up 20 blocks with dirt and wait on top. Option 3 (Active): Craft a stone sword and shield, fight mobs near a well-lit area.
Q: What's the fastest way to get Netherite?
Bed explosion mining at Y=15 in the Nether. Place a bed 6 blocks from yourself, right-click to detonate, clear Ancient Debris. Each explosion clears a 7x7x7 area. Average: 2 Ancient Debris per 20 beds. Bring fire resistance potions!
Q: Is the Warden worth fighting?
No. The Warden drops only a Sculk Catalyst (which you can find naturally in Deep Dark). It has 500 HP and deals 45 damage — enough to kill you in 2 hits even in full Netherite. It was designed as an obstacle to avoid, not a boss to defeat.
