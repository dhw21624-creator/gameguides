Minecraft 2025-2026 Ultimate Update Guide: New Features, Exploits & Speedrun Strategies

Updated: May 2026 | Reading time: 8 min

1. The Netherite Duplication Bug (Patched in 1.20.5+)

One of the most talked-about exploits in Minecraft's recent history involves a durability-timing exploit with the Grindstone and Anvil. This bug allowed players to duplicate Netherite gear by manipulating durability thresholds.

How the Exploit Worked

  • Core Mechanism: When gear durability dropped below 3%, the game's durability check would incorrectly flag the item as "undamaged." By interacting with 3 identical blank gear pieces at a Grindstone or Anvil, players could stack durability and enchantments.
  • Version Limitations: Java Edition 1.20.5+ and Bedrock Edition 1.20.10+ have patched this vulnerability.
  • Server Risks: Approximately 72% of 1.19 survival servers did not patch this bug. However, high-frequency usage (3+ times within 10 minutes) or attempting to duplicate banned items like Bedrock could trigger automatic bans.

4. 3-Hour Beginner Speedrun Guide

New to Minecraft? Follow this optimized progression path to go from zero to diamond gear in under 3 hours.

Phase 1: First 10 Minutes

  1. Punch trees → craft Wooden Pickaxe
  2. Build a simple shelter before nightfall (dirt or wood, 3x3 interior minimum)
  3. Collect wheat seeds, carrots/potatoes, and coal

Phase 2: Mining Rush (10 min – 2 hours)

  • Target Y-level: -58 to -53 for optimal diamond spawn rates
  • Use the "staircase mining" method: dig down at a 45-degree angle, then branch-mine at 2-block intervals
  • Prioritize 5 diamonds: 3 for a pickaxe, 2 for an enchantment table

Phase 3: Automation (2 – 3 hours)

  • Build a zero-redstone automatic wheat farm (water-flow design)
  • Output: ~3 stacks of wheat per hour — enough to sustain 10 sheep and 5 cows
  • Set up basic villager trading hall for enchanted books

FAQ

Q: Is the Netherite duplication bug still working in 2026?
No. It was patched in Java 1.20.5+ and Bedrock 1.20.10+. Only legacy 1.19 servers may still be vulnerable.
Q: What's the best Y-level for diamonds in 2026?
Y=-58 to Y=-53 remains the optimal range. Use the staircase mining method for best results.
Q: How do I find Erosion Caves?
Erosion Caves spawn beneath ocean biomes. Look for glowing entrances underwater or use spectator mode to scout.
Q: Can I install Twilight Forest on mobile?
No. Twilight Forest is PC-only (Java Edition). Mobile players can use Lucky Blocks and Willpack instead.
