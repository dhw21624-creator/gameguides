God of War Ragnarok Valhalla DLC Guide: Best Builds, Secret Boss & Roguelite Tips

Last Updated: June 2025  |  Game: God of War Ragnarök  |  DLC: Valhalla (Free Roguelite Expansion)

The Valhalla DLC is a free roguelite expansion for God of War Ragnarök that transforms Kratos’s journey into a replayable gauntlet through Norse mythology. Unlike the main campaign, every run through Valhalla is different — weapons, perks, and enemy encounters are procedurally generated. This guide covers the core roguelite mechanics, the strongest Kratos build for deep runs, a full strategy for the secret Odysseus boss fight, and how to maximize your Spartan Rage uptime for unstoppable combat loops.

1. Valhalla DLC Roguelite Mechanics

The Valhalla DLC strips Kratos of his full arsenal and forces you to build up power from scratch each run. Understanding the loop is essential for survival.

Core Roguelite Loop

  1. Starting Area (Gateway of Valhalla): Choose your starting weapon — Leviathan Axe, Blades of Chaos, or Draupnir Spear. Each weapon has distinct perk trees and synergies.
  2. Region Progression: Traverse through 4 zones — Helheim (ice enemies, frost resistance needed) → Midgard (mixed enemy types) → Alfheim (light elves, fast attackers) → Valhalla Summit (final boss arena).
  3. Favor of the Valkyries: Defeating elite enemies and mini-bosses drops Favor of the Valkyries. These are your roguelite currency — spend them at Valkyrie shrines between zones to permanently upgrade stats: Strength (+15% per level), Defense (+12%), Runic (+10%), and Vitality (+8%).
  4. Perk Selection: After clearing each zone, choose 1 of 3 random perks. Perks stack multiplicatively — prioritize Damage Amplification and Rage Generation early.
  5. Death & Persistence: Dying resets your run but you keep all Favor of the Valkyries spent on permanent upgrades. The max permanent upgrade level is 20.
Pro Tip: Prioritize Helheim runs first — the frost-resistant enemies are slower and easier to parry, making it the safest zone to farm early Favors. Once you hit permanent upgrade level 10, Midgard and Alfheim become manageable.

2. Best Build: Triple Weapon Mastery

The most consistent build for reaching the Valhalla Summit and defeating the final boss revolves around unlocking and maxing all three weapons and cycling them for maximum damage uptime.

Leviathan Axe

  • Frost Awaken (heavy charged attack): Applies Frost debuff, slowing enemies by 40% for 8 seconds
  • Permafrost perk: After 5 consecutive hits, Axe damage increases by +50%
  • Best against: Helheim draugr, frost-weak elites

Blades of Chaos

  • Whiplash (light combo finisher): Applies Burn, dealing 20% weapon damage/sec for 6 seconds
  • Immolation perk: Burn ticks generate +15% Rage per tick
  • Best against: Alfheim light elves, grouped enemies

Draupnir Spear

  • Vortex (hold R2): Pulls all nearby enemies into melee range, dealing AoE wind damage
  • Siphon perk: Spear kills restore 8% HP
  • Best against: Midgard travelers, ranged attackers
Critical Combo: Open with Frost Awaken (Axe) to slow the enemy → swap to Blades, use Whiplash to stack Burn → finish with Vortex (Spear) to pull them back and trigger Siphon heal. This rotation maintains both crowd control and sustain.

3. Secret Boss: Odysseus Full Strategy

Odysseus is the hidden final boss of the Valhalla DLC, unlocked after completing all 12 Favor of the Valkyries upgrades to max level (20). He appears as a spectral Greek warrior wielding a bow and shield, referencing Kratos’s original saga. This is the hardest fight in the DLC.

Odysseus Boss Phases

Phase 1 — Hunter’s Volley (100%–70% HP)

  • Teleport: Odysseus blinks to a random position every 8 seconds. Track the blue flash to predict his location.
  • Arrow Rain (AoE): He fires a volley of 12 arrows in a cone. Dodge sideways, not backward — the arrows track your last position.
  • Shield Bash: Charges forward with his shield raised. Parry this with L1 (double-tap) to trigger a stun window lasting 4 seconds. Unleash your full Axe combo during this window.
  • Strategy: Stay at mid-range, bait the Shield Bash, parry, punish. Repeat until Phase 2.

Phase 2 — Wrath of Sparta (70%–30% HP)

  • Odysseus summons 4 Spartan phantoms that rush you with spears. Kill them quickly with Blades of Chaos AoE (Whiplash).
  • He gains Arrow Barrage: a rapid-fire 20-arrow stream. Use Shield Block (hold L1) and sidestep between bursts.
  • New attack: Spear Lunge — an unblockable thrust that deals 60% HP. Dodge roll sideways on the audio cue (he shouts “For Sparta!”).
  • Strategy: Focus on adds first, then use Spartan Rage (if available) to burst Odysseus down to 30%.

Phase 3 — Last Stand (30%–0% HP)

  • Odysseus enters Berserker mode: all attacks are 50% faster, parry window shrinks to 0.3s.
  • He uses Teleport + Arrow Rain simultaneously — dodge into his teleport direction to avoid the rain.
  • At 10% HP, he triggers Final Arrow: a single charged shot that deals 99% max HP damage. Block this with a fully charged Shield Bash (R1+L1) to reflect it back, dealing the killing blow.
  • Strategy: Save Spartan Rage for this phase. Use Rage to absorb damage while you position for the Final Arrow reflect. Missing the reflect = instant death.
Odysseus Kill Reward: Defeating Odysseus unlocks the “Ghost of Sparta” cosmetic set (Kratos’s original God of War 2005 appearance) and the permanent perk “Spartan’s Legacy” (+25% damage against Greek-themed enemies in all future runs).

4. Spartan Rage Combat Loop

Maximizing Spartan Rage uptime is the key to dominating the Valhalla DLC. Here’s the optimized combat loop:

Rage Generation Cycle

  1. Open with Blades of Chaos (Whiplash): Apply Burn to 3+ enemies. With the Immolation perk, Burn ticks generate 15% Rage per second per enemy — that’s 45% Rage/sec with 3 burning targets.
  2. Activate Spartan Rage: Once the Rage bar is full, pop Rage immediately. With Rage of the Gods perk, Rage lasts 40% longer and deals +25% damage.
  3. Rage Combo Attack: Inside Rage, use R1 spam (fast punches) for single-target DPS or R2 (ground slam) for AoE crowd control. The ground slam stuns all enemies in a 5-meter radius.
  4. Exit Rage for Weapon Swap Burst: As Rage ends, immediately swap weapons to trigger Triple Threat (+30% damage for 10s) and restart the Burn application loop.
  5. Repeat: With optimal play, you can maintain near-constant Rage uptime, cycling Rage → Burn → Rage every 25-30 seconds.
Avoid: Do not use Spartan Rage defensively (to heal) unless you are below 20% HP. The DPS loss from using Rage for healing instead of damage can cause you to fall behind on the DPS check in Odysseus Phase 3. Only use Rage for healing as a last resort.

5. Final Tips for Roguelite Success

  • Always clear side rooms: Each zone has 1-2 optional combat encounters that award extra Favors. Skipping them will leave you under-leveled for the boss.
  • Prioritize Vitality upgrades: In the early permanent upgrade tree, Vitality (+8% HP per level) outscales Defense until level 10. More HP = more room for error.
  • Use the Valkyrie Shrine strategically: Between zones, always spend all remaining Favors — unspent Favors are lost on death. There’s no benefit to hoarding.
  • Learn parry timings for each zone’s elite: Helheim Draugr Lords (parry the overhead slam), Midgard Travelers (parry the shield charge), Alfheim Dark Elves (parry the dive attack). Mastering these cuts clear time by 30-40%.
  • Run resets are normal: Expect to die 5-10 times before reaching the Summit on your first playthrough. Each death makes you permanently stronger.
