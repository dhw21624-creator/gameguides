God of War Ragnarok: Valhalla DLC Complete Guide — Best Builds & Tips

Updated July 7, 2026 • 14 min read • All Difficulty Levels

The Valhalla DLC is one of the most ambitious free additions to any AAA game — a full roguelite mode where Kratos must fight through randomized combat encounters, build his power from scratch each run, and confront the ghosts of his past. Unlike the base game, there are no checkpoints, no gear carry-over, and no mercy. Every death resets your loadout. But with the right build philosophy, Glyph priorities, and boss strategies, you can conquer Valhalla on any difficulty. This guide covers it all.

1. How Valhalla Works: Roguelite Fundamentals

Valhalla strips Kratos of his gear and drops him into a series of combat arenas across five realms. Each arena cleared offers a choice of Glyphs — temporary upgrades that last only for the current run. Between realms, you visit the Valkyrie Shrine to spend Favors (the DLC's currency) on permanent stat increases, new weapon skills, and cosmetic unlocks.

Run Structure

  1. Choose Starting Loadout: Shield + Spartan Rage type + Relic. Your weapon choice depends on which Favor of the Valkyries questline you are pursuing.
  2. Clear Arenas (5-8 per realm): Each arena has randomized enemy compositions. Higher-difficulty arenas (marked with glowing red portals) offer better Glyph rewards.
  3. Boss Gate: At the end of each realm, face a boss. Early realms feature Draugr Lords, Travelers, and Dark Elf commanders. The final realm pits you against Tyr in a multi-phase battle.
  4. Death = Reset: All Glyphs and temporary upgrades are lost. Permanent upgrades (bought with Favors at the Shrine) persist across runs.

2. Best Builds for Kratos in Valhalla

Valhalla forces you to adapt, but three build archetypes consistently dominate across all difficulty levels. Choose the one that matches your playstyle and weapon preference.

Build 1: Rage of Sparta (Blades of Chaos)

Playstyle: High-risk, high-reward. Maximize Spartan Rage uptime and melt bosses with relentless aggression.

Weapon

  • Blades of Chaos (primary)
  • Focus: Burn buildup + crowd control

Shield

  • Dauntless Shield
  • High-risk parry window for Rage generation

Runic Attacks

  • Light: Cyclone of Chaos
  • Heavy: Meteoric Slam

Key Glyphs: Immolation (Burn ticks generate Rage), Rage of the Gods (+40% Rage duration, +25% damage), Triple Threat (+30% damage for 10s after weapon swap).

Combat Loop: Open with Cyclone of Chaos to apply Burn on 3+ enemies → Immolation generates 15% Rage/sec per burning target → pop Spartan Rage → R1 spam for single-target DPS → exit Rage → weapon swap to trigger Triple Threat → repeat.

Build 2: Frozen Fortress (Leviathan Axe)

Playstyle: Defensive, methodical. Freeze enemies to control the battlefield and set up devastating Runic combos.

Weapon

  • Leviathan Axe (primary)
  • Focus: Frost status + ranged precision

Shield

  • Guardian Shield
  • Parry counter generates shield charge for AoE stun

Runic Attacks

  • Light: Hel's Touch
  • Heavy: Frost Giant's Frenzy

Key Glyphs: Permafrost (heavy Frost attacks deal AoE burst), Frost Awaken (R1 combo finishers apply double Frost), Shield of Valor (parries restore 10% HP).

Combat Loop: Throw axe to apply Frost at range → Hel's Touch for gap close + stun → R1 combo until Frost Awaken triggers → Frost Giant's Frenzy on frozen targets for 2x damage → parry incoming attacks with Guardian Shield for HP sustain.

Build 3: Spear of Destiny (Draupnir Spear)

Playstyle: Agile, versatile. Use the Draupnir Spear's range, speed, and siphon mechanic to adapt to any enemy type.

Weapon

  • Draupnir Spear (primary)
  • Focus: Elemental siphoning + long-range pressure

Shield

  • Shatter Star Shield
  • Shield bash breaks enemy guard and staggers

Runic Attacks

  • Light: Huldra Charge
  • Heavy: Artillery of the Ancients

Key Glyphs: Siphon Mastery (spear detonations steal enemy element for +20% damage of that type), Wind Runner (+25% movement speed after dodge), Elemental Storm (detonating 8+ spears triggers AoE explosion of stolen element).

Combat Loop: Plant 4-6 spears in priority targets → Huldra Charge to close distance → detonate spears for Siphon → Artillery of the Ancients against large groups → dodge to trigger Wind Runner → reposition and repeat.

5. Tyr Boss Fight: Complete Strategy

Tyr is the final boss of every Valhalla run — a four-phase gauntlet that tests everything you have learned. Here is the full breakdown:

Phase 1: Sword & Shield Tyr

Tyr fights conservatively with sword and shield. He mixes fast horizontal slashes with a delayed shield bash. Key openings: After a 3-hit sword combo, after a failed shield bash, and during his slow overhead slam (dodge left, then punish for 3-4 hits).

Danger: If Tyr raises his shield and glows blue, he is about to parry you. Stop attacking immediately or you will be stunned and eat a full combo.

Phase 2: Dual-Wield Tyr

Tyr discards the shield for dual swords. His attack speed doubles. Key openings: After his spinning slash combo (6 hits — dodge backwards for the first 5, then parry the final overhead), and after his leaping stab (side-dodge, then R1 combo).

Danger: Tyr periodically leaps into the air for a delayed AoE slam. The red ring appears before he lands — dodge at the last moment or eat 40% HP damage.

Phase 3: Spear Tyr

Tyr switches to a long-range spear with sweeping attacks and a devastating charge. Key openings: After the charge (he lunges across the arena — side-dodge and he staggers against the wall), and after his 2-hit thrust combo.

Danger: Tyr gains an enrage timer in this phase. If the fight drags beyond ~3 minutes, he permanently powers up with glowing red eyes — all attacks become unblockable and deal double damage. Aggression is mandatory here. Pop Spartan Rage and do not let up.

Phase 4: Kratos Mirror Match

In the final phase, Tyr mimics Kratos — wielding all three weapons (Blades, Axe, and Spear) and swapping between them. He can use Spartan Rage and will parry your attacks if you are predictable. Key openings: After his own Rage ends (he is briefly stunned), after a failed Leviathan Axe throw (side-dodge the returning axe), and during his weapon-swap animation.

Victory Condition: Survive until Tyr's Rage meter depletes. Once his Rage ends, he collapses — approach and press R3 to trigger the final cinematic. You do not need to reduce his HP to zero. Focus on survival, not damage.

Avoid: Do not use Spartan Rage defensively (to heal) during the Tyr fight unless you are below 20% HP. The DPS loss from healing instead of damaging during your Rage window can cause you to fail the Phase 3 enrage check. Only heal with Rage as a last resort.

7. Rewards & Unlockables

Completing Valhalla runs and the Favor of the Valkyries questline unlocks the following permanent rewards for use in both Valhalla and the base game:

  • Legacy Rage: A new Spartan Rage type — transforms Kratos into his younger, Greek-era self with the Blade of Olympus. Deals massive AoE damage but drains Rage 50% faster. Unlocked by completing all Favor of the Valkyries objectives.
  • Armor of the Berserker: Full cosmetic set for base-game Kratos. Each piece unlocks from defeating Tyr on progressively higher difficulties.
  • Valkyrie Queen Armor (Freya): Cosmetic set for Freya, unlocked by clearing Valhalla on Show Me Mastery.
  • Young Kratos Skin: Complete Valhalla on any difficulty to unlock the Greek-era Kratos appearance for the base game.
  • Rond of Obliteration: Shield attachment that causes parries to deal AoE damage. Drops from Tyr on Show Me Resolve or higher.
