Vantage (Class: Recon)

Passive — Spotter's Lens: ADS unarmed or with mid/long-range scopes to scan enemies. Displays legend name, shield rarity, and squad size at any range.
Tactical — Echo Relocation: Deploy Echo (flying bat companion). Jump to Echo's position. 20-second cooldown.
Ultimate — Sniper's Mark: Equip custom sniper rifle (5 shots). First hit marks target (+15% damage to team), consecutive hits deal 2x damage.

The new Promenade Labs POI replaces the old Promenade. Verticality is extreme—three underground levels with zip-line rotations between them. Control the middle level for access to all exits. The Phase Driver now connects Terraformer to the Labs, cutting rotation time by 40%.
