Diablo 4 Season 6 Guide: Best Classes, Leveling & Endgame Pit Strategy

The Season of the Ascendant has reshaped Sanctuary. New Legendary Aspects, reworked Paragon Boards, and a fresh Battle Pass demand updated strategies. This tome covers the fastest leveling path, the strongest builds for each class, and how to push past Pit Tier 100.

II. Fastest Leveling Strategy (1-100)

  1. World Tier 1 until level 30. The 20% XP bonus on WT2 isn't worth the slower kill speed.
  2. Domhainne Tunnels (Scosglen) is the #1 dungeon for XP density. Reset by leaving game and rejoining.
  3. Helltides become efficient at level 45+. Farm Aberrant Cinders and open Mystery Chests (250 cinders) for guaranteed Legendaries.
  4. Capstone Dungeon WT3 at level 50-55 with upgraded gear.
  5. Nightmare Dungeons only at tiers where monsters are max 10 levels above you—beyond that, the XP penalty outweighs the bonus.

III. The Pit: Endgame Progression

The Pit of the Artificer scales infinitely. Each tier increases monster HP and damage. Your goal:

  • Tier 1-20: Unlock Torment difficulties and masterworking materials.
  • Tier 21-60: Farm Obducite for Masterworking. These tiers drop the most per minute.
  • Tier 61-100: Prestige push. Requires fully Masterworked gear and optimized Paragon.
  • Tier 101+: Leaderboard territory. Only S-tier builds with perfect gear can compete.

Masterworking Priority: Focus on your primary damage skill ranks first, then Critical Strike Damage, then defensive affixes. A single well-rolled Masterwork on a 2H weapon can increase your DPS by 30%.
