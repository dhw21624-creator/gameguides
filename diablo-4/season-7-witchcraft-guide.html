THE COVEN RISES — SEASON OF WITCHCRAFT

Diablo 4 Season 7 Guide: Witchcraft Powers, Best Builds & The Butcher Strategy

Season 7 — the Season of Witchcraft — has descended upon Sanctuary with a dark new power system, a rebalanced endgame, and a familiar foe returning deadlier than ever. The Coven of Hawezar has unlocked twelve supernatural abilities across three schools of magic, and those who master them will dominate the leaderboards. This guide covers every Witchcraft Power, the meta-defining Blood Wave Necromancer, the Soul Harvest mechanic, and how to hunt the Butcher for his exclusive drops.

I. Witchcraft Powers: Complete System Breakdown

The Witchcraft Power system introduces 12 new supernatural abilities divided into three distinct schools. Powers are unlocked by collecting Fragments of the Witch from seasonal quests, Whispers, and Helltide chests. You can equip one power from each school simultaneously, and powers level up by consuming duplicate fragments.

II. Best Build: Blood Wave Necromancer

The Blood Wave Necromancer has emerged as the undisputed king of Season 7. A massive buff to the Blood Wave ultimate — now dealing an absurd 15,000% weapon damage — combined with the Soul Harvest Witchcraft Power creates a build capable of one-shotting dungeon bosses and clearing Pit Tier 130+.

Gear Priority

  • Weapon: Two-handed Scythe with +Blood Wave ranks, Overpower Damage, Intelligence, and Critical Strike Damage. Tempering: Blood Wave Cooldown Reduction.
  • Chest: Blood Artisan's Cuirass (unique) — generates free Bone Spirit casts that also Overpower.
  • Amulet: Deathless Visage — increases Blood Wave damage per enemy hit.
  • Rings: Fastblood Aspect + Sacrificial Aspect for maximum Essence generation.
  • Masterworking Priority: Blood Wave ranks > Overpower Damage > Maximum Life > Intelligence.

Rotation: Corpse Tendrils (group enemies) → Soul Harvest stacks capped → Coven's Favor proc → Blood Wave → clean up with Blood Lance. Against bosses, hold Blood Wave for Overpower windows from Rathma's Vigor for 3x effective damage.

III. The Butcher: Season 7 Farming Guide

The Butcher returns in Season 7 with a dramatically increased spawn rate — now appearing in 5% of all dungeons (up from 1% in previous seasons). He also has an expanded loot table with a brand-new unique weapon exclusive to Season 7.

Strategy: The Butcher is highly aggressive but lacks gap-closers. Kite him around pillars and environment obstacles while maintaining Soul Harvest stacks. His hook pull has a 12-second cooldown — track it and save your mobility skill to dodge it. For Necromancers, Blood Golem taunt buys you crucial windows to reposition and drop Blood Waves safely. When he drops below 30% HP, he enters a frenzy state (+40% attack speed, +25% movement speed) — pop Veil of the Coven or Blood Mist to survive the burst phase.

IV. Fastest Leveling: 1-60 Roadmap

  1. Season Questline first (levels 1-25). Completing the Coven storyline unlocks all three Witchcraft school slots and grants 8-10 Fragments of the Witch.
  2. Domhainne Tunnels (Scosglen) remains the #1 XP dungeon. With Soul Harvest equipped, you gain an extra 30% damage at full stacks, doubling clear speed from Season 6.
  3. Whispers of the Dead at level 30+. Whispers now reward Fragments of the Witch, giving you a faster path to max-level Witchcraft Powers.
  4. Helltides from level 45. Helltide Commanders have a 15% chance to drop 3-5 Fragments. Open Mystery Chests (250 cinders) for guaranteed Legendaries.
  5. Capstone Dungeon WT3 at level 50. Enter Nightmare Dungeons and push tiers where monsters are 10 levels above you for optimal XP.

V. Endgame: Pit Strategy for Season 7

  • Tiers 1-20: Unlock Torment difficulties. Witchcraft Powers alone can carry you through these tiers even with suboptimal gear.
  • Tiers 21-60: Farm Obducite for Masterworking. Soul Harvest + Coven's Favor rotation lets you clear rooms in one Blood Wave cycle.
  • Tiers 61-100: Requires fully Masterworked gear with at least 2/3 correct affixes on each piece. Blood Golem survivability becomes critical — ensure he has at least 40% damage reduction from Paragon nodes.
  • Tiers 101-130+: Leaderboard push territory. Swap Infernal Brand for Veil of the Coven if survivability is an issue. The Blood Wave cooldown must be under 8 seconds with Fastblood Aspect for consistent Overpower procs.
