III. Vlinder: Meta Support Loadout

Passive: Extends downed timer, displays ally silhouette/HP with Med Drone.
Med Drones: Heals allies and self.
Windmill Smokes: Throwable curved smokes for area denial.
Ultimate (Flutter Rescue UAV): Auto-revives ally within 55m, provides explosive shield.
Loadout: MCX LT (Compensator, extended mag, 2x-3x scope) + FS-12 secondary. Prioritize healing speed/gadget cooldown perks.

IV. Assault Loadouts

D-Wolf: M4A1 or CI-19 with suppressor + 3x scope. G18 secondary for emergencies. High mobility flanking.
Nox: Suppressed M4A1/CI-19 with max control/stability. One-shot potential from stealth.
