II. Meta Team Compositions

Miyabi Frost Anomaly (S+ Meta)

Core: Miyabi + Yanagi + Yuzuha + Lucia. Stack Frost Anomaly, trigger massive Disorder damage. Swap Lucia for Rina or Astra Yao for ATK.

Ellen / Soldier 0 Anby Physical DPS

Core: Ellen or S0 Anby + Lycaon + Astra Yao + Yuzuha/Lucia. Stun-lock with Lycaon, then unload Physical DPS with Astra Yao buffs.

Dialyn / Trigger / Ju Fufu Stun Teams

Core: Dialyn/Trigger/Ju Fufu + Astra Yao + Yuzuha/Lucia. Extend stun windows, burst during vulnerability.
