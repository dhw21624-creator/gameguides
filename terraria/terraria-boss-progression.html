I. Pre-Hardmode: The Foundation (7 Bosses)

The recommended order for new adventurers. Each boss gates critical progression items and NPCs.

1. King Slime (Optional)

Summon: Slime Crown, during Slime Rain, or spawns naturally at outer map edges.
Prep: Iron/Lead armor, any ranged weapon (Shuriken, bow, wand).
Tactic: Stay mobile. Build a rope tower—King Slime can't reach you above 35 blocks. Free kill.

2. Eye of Cthulhu

Summon: Suspicious Looking Eye at night (6+ NPCs, 200+ HP, 10+ defense).
Prep: Gold/Platinum armor, strong bow (Fire Arrows), Hermes Boots.
Tactic: Build a 2-3 level platform arena (200+ blocks wide). Dodge its charges in phase 1-2, then unload everything during the stationary phase 3.

3. Eater of Worlds (Corruption) / Brain of Cthulhu (Crimson)

Summon: Destroy 3 Shadow Orbs / Crimson Hearts, or use Worm Food / Bloody Spine.
Prep: Upgrade to Demonite/Crimtane gear after first kill. Piercing weapons (Vilethorn, Jester Arrows).
Eater Tactic: Fight in the Corruption chasm—piercing weapons hit multiple segments simultaneously.
Brain Tactic: Clear Creepers in phase 1 with piercing, then dodge and punish the exposed brain in phase 2.

4. Queen Bee — 5. Skeletron — 6. Deerclops — 7. Wall of Flesh

Progression note: Queen Bee drops Bee Wax gear (excellent for Skeletron). Skeletron unlocks the Dungeon. Deerclops is optional but drops strong pre-Hardmode gear. Wall of Flesh triggers Hardmode—prepare your world before summoning.

III. Essential Arena Design

Every boss fight benefits from preparation:

  • Campfires + Heart Lanterns: Stacking regen—place them throughout your arena.
  • Platforms: 3 levels, 200+ blocks wide, spaced 15-20 blocks apart vertically.
  • Buff Stations: Sharpening Station, Ammo Box, Crystal Ball, Bewitching Table.
  • Potions: Ironskin, Regeneration, Swiftness, Endurance, Lifeforce (Hardmode).

Pro Tip: Prep your world for Hardmode before killing the Wall of Flesh. Dig 4-block-wide hellevators to contain Corruption/Crimson spread. Your future self will thank you.
