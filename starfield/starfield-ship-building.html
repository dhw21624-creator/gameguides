I. Ship Building Fundamentals

Every ship consists of mandatory components: Reactor (power generation), Grav Drive (jump range), Engines (speed/maneuverability), Shield Generator (defense), and Weapons (three hardpoint groups). The key trade-off is mass vs. mobility—every module adds mass, and heavier ships turn slower.

III. Ship Combat: Advanced Tactics

Power Management: The single most important skill. Full power to shields while under fire, redistribute to weapons during attack runs, and keep 1-2 pips in engines for emergency boosting. Bind power allocation keys for instant switching.

Targeting System: Focus fire on enemy grav drives first—a disabled enemy can't escape, letting you board and commandeer their ship. Boarding sells for 3-5x the value of destroying.

Class C vs Class A: Class C ships have the most firepower but slowest turns. Class A fighters can out-turn anything. The meta favors Class B as the sweet spot—outfit a Class B reactor with top-tier particle beams and you'll dominate.
